Hardware & Arm Mounting (iPhone or iPad)

Physically mounting and framing the device on the revolving 360 arm

  • 1.1

    Mount the iPhone / iPad securely on the Arm Clamp

    Place your iPhone (iPhone 11 or newer recommended) or iPad onto the motorized rotating arm mount. Ensure the clamp is tightly tightened and won't wobble under centrifugal force.

  • 1.2

    Select Orientation & Lens

    Set orientation to Vertical / Portrait (9:16) for standard Instagram Reels / TikTok format, or Landscape if doing wide corporate clips. In the app, choose the 0.5× Ultra-Wide lens so guests' full bodies and outfits fit easily inside the frame.

  • 1.3

    Connect to the Local Booth Wi-Fi Router

    Join the iPhone/iPad and your laptop to the same dedicated booth Wi-Fi router (5GHz band recommended). No venue internet is required — clips transfer ultra-fast offline over the local LAN.

Pro Tip: Plug in a small portable power bank to the phone mount arm to keep the phone at 100% battery throughout a 4-hour event!

3

Auto-Sensitivity Calibration ("Configure Sensitivity")

Teach the app your platform's exact spin speed to eliminate false triggers

  • 3.1

    Tap "Configure Sensitivity"

    Tap the Configure Sensitivity button on the phone screen (or click Calibrate from the laptop 360 Desk). The 2-step calibration wizard will begin.

  • 3.2

    Step 1: Hold Still (1.5 seconds)

    Leave the arm stationary. The app reads baseline floor vibrations and platform rumble (from guests stepping on the glass) to set the noise floor.

  • 3.3

    Step 2: Spin the Arm

    Turn on your 360 motor for a normal test spin. The app measures the peak & median rotation speed (°/s) and automatically calculates your ideal start and stop thresholds (e.g. Start at 25°/s, Stop at 11°/s).

  • 3.4

    Lock the Screen (Shield Mode)

    Tap the Lock button. The screen locks completely with a dark overlay to prevent guests, wind, or centrifugal force from accidentally tapping any buttons. Hold for 1.4 seconds anytime you need to unlock.

🔄 Stop Mode: Follow the Arm (Recommended)

Recording automatically stops the moment the arm slows down, adding a smooth 1.5s deceleration tail so the final pose wraps up naturally.

⏱️ Stop Mode: Fixed Duration

Guarantees every clip is exactly N seconds (e.g., 10 seconds), regardless of when the operator presses the motor stop button.

4

Live Shooting & Automatic Background Transfer

Zero operator taps required on the phone during the party

  • 4.1

    Guests Step onto the 360 Platform

    Operator cues the guests: "3, 2, 1, spin!" The TV Live Wall automatically shows: GET READY 🌀.

  • 4.2

    Operator Starts the 360 Remote

    Press the start button on your physical 360 motor remote. As the arm rotates, the phone's CoreMotion gyroscope detects sustained spin (>300ms) and instantly auto-starts recording with a chime sound.

  • 4.3

    Auto-Stop & Instant Wi-Fi Transfer

    When the spin finishes, recording auto-stops. The high-framerate (60fps/120fps) video file is compressed in H.264 and transferred automatically over local Wi-Fi to your laptop within 2 to 4 seconds!

Offline Protection: If the booth Wi-Fi ever stutters, the clip is never lost! It remains safely stored on the iPhone in the pending queue and automatically uploads once the Wi-Fi reconnects.

Option B: Using a DSLR / Mirrorless, Capture Card, or GoPro Desktop v1.1.94+

Complete process when mounting a Canon EOS, Nikon DSLR/Z, GoPro, or HDMI camera inside the 360 ring light with a Surface Pro / Windows tablet or laptop

  • B.1

    Mount the Camera, Ring Light & Windows Tablet on the Arm

    Attach your ring light to the rotating 360 arm, mount your Canon EOS (e.g. M6 II, M50, R50, R10, 90D, 6D II), Nikon DSLR/Z, or GoPro inside the center of the ring light using a wide-angle lens so guests fit top-to-bottom without excessive headroom.

    Attach your Windows tablet (Surface Pro 8 or faster) or PC securely to the arm (or keep your laptop on a table with a long slip-ring / tethered setup) and connect the camera straight into the tablet with a high-quality data USB cable (avoid unpowered hubs).

    Sony Alpha note: Sony cameras over USB transfer still photos only in PC Remote mode. Connect your Sony's HDMI output to an HDMI Capture Card (e.g. Elgato Cam Link) and pick 🎥 HDMI Card / Webcam in step B.3.

  • B.2

    Prepare the Camera Body for 60 / 120 fps Slow Motion

    Before plugging in, insert a fast SD card with free space (Canon and Nikon save 60/120fps movie files to the card first, then stream straight into the app) and set these on the camera menu:

    • Turn physical Mode Dial to Movie (Video Camera icon)
    • Enable High Frame Rate Movie: 1920×1080 at 119.9 / 120 fps (or 60 fps) for ultra-smooth slow motion
    • Set Movie Shooting Exposure to Movie Manual Exposure (M) and Exposure Simulation: Disable (if available) so live adjustments respond immediately
    • Set Movie Servo AF: Off (focus once before the spin or switch lens to MF so focus never hunts while spinning around the guest)

  • B.3

    Connect in 360 Desk → 📷 Record 360 & Dial DSLR Settings

    Open 360 Desk on Desktop 1.1.94+ and click the 📷 Record 360 tab at the top. Choose 📷 USB DSLR / GoPro (or 🎥 HDMI Card / Webcam for capture cards / webcams) and click Connect. Frame the guest using the live view crosshair.

    Click ⚙️ DSLR Settings → click ↻ Read, then tune your exposure:
    • Shutter Speed: tap 1/125 · 60fps (for 60fps recording) or 1/250 · 120fps (for 120fps HFR)
    • Aperture, ISO & White Balance: balance brightness under your ring light, click Apply, and click 🎯 AF once to lock focus on a test stand-in.

  • B.4

    Choose Arm Trigger & Run "Configure sensitivity"

    Under 🌀 Arm Motion Trigger, pick how the recording starts:

    • 📐 Windows Tablet Gyro (Surface Pro / 2-in-1 on arm): Tick Start recording when tablet starts rotating sideways → click Configure sensitivity → click Run 5s Auto-Calibrate (hold still 2s, then spin 3s at event speed). Pick Fixed duration (e.g. 7s) or Follow arm stop.
    • 📱 Paired iPhone/iPad (Wireless Arm Sensor): Keep your laptop on a table while an old iPhone mounted on the arm (paired via Section 2) detects rotation wirelessly and triggers your DSLR.
    • ⏱️ Countdown / Start Button / Remote Clicker: Set a Countdown (e.g. 3s) and fire hands-free using ● Start 360 Recording or a wireless clicker / USB foot pedal (F9 / Next).

  • B.5

    Optional Boomerang / Live Wall Cues & Automatic USB Import

    Tick 🔁 Reverse / Boomerang if you want instant forward+backward playback synced with Presets, and keep 📺 Show countdown & REC cues on Live Wall / TV checked.

    Click 🧲 Arm Trigger (turns green ARMED) and spin the platform! Recording starts on spin, stops automatically at your duration or arm stop, and streams the .MOV / .MP4 directly into your 360 Inbox with a live transfer progress bar — immediately feeding into Section 5 (Ready-Made Edits) and Section 6 (Live TV Wall & 2-Step QR)!

Tip: Switch off camera auto-power-off / Eco Standby in your DSLR menu and use an AC dummy battery or full charge before a long event so USB Live View stays awake between guests.

Live TV Wall & Offline QR Code Sharing

Display live looping videos on external screens and let guests download in seconds

  • 6.1

    Connect an External TV / Projector

    Plug an HDMI cable from your laptop into the venue's large TV or projector. In photobooth4u, open Live Wall (or go to http://localhost:47777/wall in a second browser window and drag it to the TV).

  • 6.2

    Real-Time Crowd Cues

    While the arm is spinning, the TV wall pulses with "360 SPINNING 🌀". When rendering, it indicates "RENDERING MAGIC ✨" to keep the crowd excited.

  • 6.3

    Seamless High-Def Video Slideshow

    Once rendering finishes, the branded 360 video immediately plays in high definition on the TV with audio. It loops continuously in a dynamic slideshow with previously finished videos.

  • 6.4

    Guest Download via 2-Step Offline QR Code

    Alongside the video on the Live Wall (and on the operator's sharing station), guests see the 2-Step QR Code:

    Step 1 (Wi-Fi QR): Guest points phone camera at the Wi-Fi QR → Taps "Join Network" to link to your booth Wi-Fi.

    Step 2 (Download QR): Guest scans the video QR → Instant mobile download page opens in Safari / Chrome → Guest taps Save Video directly to Camera Roll!

Online Cloud Backup: If your laptop has an active mobile hotspot/SIM connection, the video also syncs to the online cloud gallery so guests can view it from home afterwards.

Quick Operator Pre-Flight Checklist

Run through these 5 checks before opening the booth to event guests

  • A

    Lighting Check

    Ensure ring light or LED bar on the arm is turned ON and tilted slightly downward toward guests' faces. Guests should face the light.

  • B

    Tap-to-Focus & AE/AF Lock

    On the iPhone/iPad, tap a person standing in the center and tap the AE/AF Lock button. This prevents exposure flickering when the camera spins past bright room lights.

  • C

    Perform 1 Complete Test Spin

    Spin the empty platform for 8 seconds. Verify the iPhone triggers auto-record, sends the clip, renders the music & overlay, and plays on the Live TV Wall.

  • D

    Download Test on your Personal Phone

    Scan the 2-Step QR with your personal phone to ensure you can save the MP4 to your gallery in under 5 seconds.

  • E

    If Using a DSLR / GoPro / Capture Card (Option B)

    Verify SD card has free space, mode dial is on Movie (60/120fps, Movie Manual Exposure, Exposure Simulation: Disable, Servo AF: Off), Shutter is set to 1/125–1/250 in ⚙️ DSLR Settings, and complete one Run 5s Auto-Calibrate test spin.
