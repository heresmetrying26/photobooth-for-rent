Step 1 · Link Laptop & Tablet First

Connect Both Devices on Wi-Fi (Online or Offline)

Before scanning the QR code, connect your laptop and Android / Huawei tablet using one of these two reliable Wi-Fi methods:

Option A · Same Wi-Fi Router Recommended · Online or Offline

  1. Connect both the laptop and the Android / Huawei tablet to the exact same Wi-Fi router / SSID (works with internet OR your own offline pocket router).
  2. Avoid hotel/mall “Guest Wi-Fi” that blocks device-to-device traffic.
  3. Offline router tip: If the tablet pops up “No internet — switch networks?”, tap Stay connected (or turn Airplane Mode ON → Wi-Fi back ON).

Option B · Laptop Booth Wi-Fi / Mobile Hotspot 100% Offline · No Router Needed

  1. Turn ON your laptop’s Mobile Hotspot / Booth Wi-Fi.
  2. Connect your Android / Huawei tablet to that laptop Wi-Fi name.
  3. No venue router needed — stays private and works 100% offline.

Two Quick Tips Before You Pair:

• Do not use USB Tethering: Many Android / Huawei tablets either disable USB Tethering or block the Guest Screen over USB tethering. Always connect via Same Wi-Fi Router or Laptop Booth Wi-Fi / Hotspot (you can still plug a charger or power bank into the tablet to keep it at 100% battery).
• If your tablet was used standalone before: Make sure “Make this tablet the booth Wi-Fi” (in tablet Setup → 6 Share) is switched OFF so the tablet stays connected to your router or laptop Wi-Fi.

Step 2 · Start on the Laptop

Open the Tablet Bridge Pairing QR Code on Your Laptop

Always open the pairing QR on your laptop first. This works on both the Desktop App (online or offline) and the Online Website (with Print Helper).

Option 1 · photobooth4u Desktop App Online & Offline

  1. Install / update to photobooth4u Desktop v1.1.105+ from /downloads.
  2. Activate your rental code on the laptop only (tablet needs no extra code).
  3. Open Setup → 7 Session → Screen & lock → Guest screen.
  4. Scroll to Huawei / Android Tablet Bridge (Guest Screen + Camera) — your QR code is ready immediately (works with or without internet).

Option 2 · Online Website (Chrome / Edge) Online

  1. Start photobooth4u Print Helper v1.1.21+ on the laptop first (keep its window open; allow Windows Firewall for Private & Public if asked).
  2. Open photobooth4u.netlify.app/app on the laptop and unlock your code.
  3. Open Setup → 7 Session → Screen & lock → Guest screen.
  4. The Tablet Bridge QR appears automatically (if you opened Print Helper afterwards, click Check again). Once opened, the local bridge keeps running even if venue Wi-Fi loses internet mid-event!

Don’t see the Operator Menu pop-up when long-pressing?

• Hold your finger still for 1 full second on a blank background spot or the top logo/header area (not on a scrollable list, button, or text box).
• If you accidentally tapped 🌐 Booth Mode & URL (Online / Offline) inside the Operator Menu, there is also an 🖥️ Open Laptop Guest + Camera Bridge button at the bottom of that pop-up too.
• Make sure you opened the installed photobooth4u Android App (not Chrome browser on the tablet) — the Operator Menu and Bridge scanner live inside the Android app.

Step 4 · Scan the Laptop QR & Verify Camera

Pair in 2 Seconds & Control the Tablet From Your Laptop

1. Scan Inside the Tablet Bridge Screen

  1. Tap the big red 📷 Scan Pairing QR on Laptop button.
  2. Allow Camera permission when Android asks, then point the tablet at the laptop’s QR code from Step 2.
  3. Within 2 seconds, the tablet switches into full-screen Guest Screen mode and the laptop shows green: Connected: [Tablet Name] (Guest Screen + Camera ready).
  4. On the laptop, open Setup → 3 Camera and confirm Tablet Camera · [Tablet Name] is selected.

2. How to Flip Camera, Re-Scan QR, or Exit Bridge Mode

Once paired, the tablet runs full-screen so guests cannot accidentally leave the booth. To open the hidden Tablet Bridge menu anytime:

  • From the Laptop: Click Flip tablet camera in Step 7 (or New pairing code to revoke old links).
  • From the Tablet: Long-press the TOP-LEFT corner of the tablet screen (or press Android Back) to pop up the Tablet Bridge menu!

Do NOT scan the pairing QR with your tablet’s regular Camera app!

Scanning with the regular Camera app opens a browser tab without the tablet’s native Camera2 feed or offline Wi-Fi binding. Always scan using the red 📷 Scan Pairing QR on Laptop button inside the photobooth4u app.

Step 5 · Event-Day Checklist & Troubleshooting

Offline Checklist & Quick Timeout Fixes

1

Use Latest App Versions

Update to Desktop v1.1.105+ (or Print Helper v1.1.21+ for online web) and Android APK v1.2.17+ for full offline Wi-Fi & auto-reconnect fixes.

2

Allow Windows Firewall

When Windows Defender Firewall asks for photobooth4u or Print Helper, tick both Private & Public and click Allow (or set your Wi-Fi to Private in Windows Settings).

3

Do Not Disturb ON

Switch Do Not Disturb ON on the tablet so pop-up notifications never cover the guest camera. (Screen stay-awake is already automatic.)

4

Raise Tablet Volume

Turn up the tablet’s Media Volume so guests hear the spoken countdown (“3, 2, 1, Smile!”) and shutter clicks from the tablet speakers.

ProblemQuick Fix
Tablet says “Timed out reaching 192.168.x.x:47777” (online or offline) 1. Confirm both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network (or the tablet is connected to the laptop’s Booth Wi-Fi / Mobile Hotspot), and keep USB Tethering OFF.
2. On Windows, open Settings → Network & internet → Wi-Fi → [your Wi-Fi] and change Network profile type to Private (or allow photobooth4u / Print Helper on both Private & Public in Windows Firewall).
3. On your router / pocket Wi-Fi, turn AP Isolation / Client Isolation OFF.
4. On an offline tablet with a SIM card, turn Airplane Mode ON → Wi-Fi back ON, and tap Stay connected if Android says “No internet”.
