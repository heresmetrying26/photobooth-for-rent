Part 1 · Choose Your Connection (Wired First)

USB-C Tethering (Best & Most Secure) vs. Private Booth Wi-Fi

For paid events, always prefer Option A (USB-C + USB Tethering) — it creates a direct, isolated cable link between laptop and tablet with zero venue Wi-Fi risk.

Option A · USB-C + USB Tethering Most Secure · Offline

  1. Connect your Android / Huawei tablet to your laptop with a reliable USB-C data cable (not a charge-only cable).
  2. On the tablet, turn ON USB Tethering:
    • Huawei / HarmonyOS: Settings → Mobile network → Personal hotspot → More → USB tethering
    • Samsung Galaxy Tab: Settings → Connections → Mobile Hotspot and Tethering → USB tethering
    • Xiaomi / Redmi / Generic Android: Settings → Portable hotspot → USB tethering
  3. Windows automatically creates a private wired link (often named Ethernet 2 / Ethernet 3 or Remote NDIS) — photobooth4u prioritizes it automatically in the pairing QR!

Option B · Laptop Booth Wi-Fi / Same Router Wireless

  1. Use this if your tablet is on a separate kiosk stand away from the laptop, or if your Wi-Fi-only tablet hides the USB Tethering menu.
  2. Best offline wireless: Switch on your laptop’s Mobile Hotspot / Booth Wi-Fi and connect the Android tablet to it.
  3. Router wireless: Connect both the laptop and tablet to your own dedicated pocket router. (Avoid hotel/mall “Guest Wi-Fi” networks that isolate devices.)

Part 2 · Step-by-Step Setup (Under 2 Minutes)

How to Pair Your Laptop & Android Tablet

  1. 1

    Install Both Apps (Activate Laptop Only)

    Download and install photobooth4u Desktop on your Windows or Mac laptop and the photobooth4u Android APK on your tablet from photobooth4u.netlify.app/downloads. Enter and activate your rental code on the laptop only — the Android tablet needs no separate license code for Bridge mode.

  2. 2

    Open the Tablet Bridge QR Code on the Laptop

    In photobooth4u Desktop, open Setup → 7 Session → Screen & lock and scroll down to Guest screen → Huawei / Android Tablet Bridge (Guest Screen + Camera). Leave the pairing QR code visible on screen.

  3. 3

    Launch Bridge Mode on the Android Tablet & Scan QR

    Open photobooth4u on your Android / Huawei tablet. Right on the activation/start screen, tap 🖥️ Laptop Guest + Camera Bridge (if the tablet is already activated, open the Operator Menu and tap the same button). Tap 📷 Scan Pairing QR on Laptop, allow camera permission, and scan the laptop’s QR code.

  4. 4

    Verify Live Camera & Guest Screen

    Within 2 seconds the tablet switches into full-screen Guest Screen mode and the laptop shows a green status: Connected: [Tablet Name] (Guest Screen + Camera ready). Open Setup → 3 Camera on the laptop and confirm Tablet Camera · [Tablet Name] (Front) (or (Rear)) is selected and live!

Tip: Reconnecting Later Is One Tap

The tablet remembers the last paired laptop address automatically. If you unplug or restart, just tap Connect on the tablet’s Bridge screen — or tap New pairing code on the laptop anytime you want a brand-new security token and QR code.

Part 4 · Event-Day Checklist & Quick Fixes

Pre-Event Checklist & Troubleshooting

1

Test Your USB-C Data Cable

Some charging cables omit data pins. Plug in before the event and confirm USB tethering turns ON without greying out.

2

Allow Firewall Once

On Windows, if a Defender Firewall prompt appears when opening photobooth4u, tick Private and Public networks and click Allow access.

3

Turn On Do Not Disturb

Switch Do Not Disturb ON on the tablet so notifications and pop-ups never cover the guest camera screen. (Screen keep-awake is already automatic!)

4

Check Tablet Volume

Raise the tablet’s Media Volume so guests clearly hear the spoken countdown (“3, 2, 1, Smile!”) and shutter click from the booth.

ProblemQuick Fix
Tablet says “Could not reach laptop” Verify USB Tethering is switched ON (or both devices are on the same private Wi-Fi / Laptop Hotspot). Check Windows Firewall allows photobooth4u on both Private & Public profiles, then re-scan the QR code.
USB Tethering option is greyed out on a Wi-Fi-only tablet Some Wi-Fi-only Android tablets hide USB Tethering until the USB cable is plugged in, or disable it in firmware. Switch to Option B: Laptop Mobile Hotspot / Booth Wi-Fi (still 100% offline and private!).
Tablet is connected, but laptop still shows built-in webcam Open Setup → 3 Camera on the laptop and select Tablet Camera · [Tablet Name] from the Camera dropdown.
Tablet says “Bridge pairing expired” You clicked New pairing code or restarted the laptop app. Long-press the top-left corner on the tablet → Change Laptop / Re-scan QR and scan the new QR in Step 7.
How do I exit Guest Screen mode on the tablet? Long-press the top-left corner of the tablet screen (or press Android Back) to open the Tablet Bridge operator menu: Switch Camera, Re-scan QR, or Exit Bridge.
Countdown voice or shutter sounds still play on the laptop Tap once anywhere on the tablet screen after pairing so audio activates, and verify the tablet’s Media volume is up and not muted.
