Quick Overview · Two Proven Ways to Pair Laptop + iPad

Pick the Setup That Matches Your Booth

Because Apple locks iPadOS camera access to the active foreground app (and does not include a built-in native bridge camera sender on iPad), choose between Method 1 (iPad as USB Extended Camera) or Method 2 (iPad as Native Booth + Laptop as Print Station):

Method 1 · iPad as USB Extended Camera

Your laptop runs photobooth4u Desktop and selects the iPad inside Setup → 3 Camera. Guests view your laptop screen or an HDMI TV.

Method 2 · iPad Booth + Laptop Print Station

Run photobooth4u natively on the iPad (camera + guest screen together), and let the laptop silently print to DNP, HiTi, Epson, or SELPHY.

Important Note About Apple “Continuity Camera”

Apple’s built-in Continuity Camera for Mac works with iPhone only — Apple does not support using an iPad as a native Continuity Camera webcam. To use an iPad as a laptop camera (on either Windows or Mac), follow Part 1 & Part 2 below. (If you happen to have a MacBook + iPhone, Continuity Camera appears automatically in photobooth4u with zero extra apps!)

Part 1 · Choose a USB Camera Bridge App (For Method 1)

Recommended Apps to Turn Your iPad into a Laptop Webcam

Always connect over a direct USB-C or Lightning cable instead of venue Wi-Fi — it keeps video local, prevents wireless lag, and keeps your iPad charged all event long:

Camo Studio Recommended

Windows & Mac · USB-C / Lightning. Most reliable plug-and-play iPad webcam bridge. Gives manual lens switching (Front / Rear / Ultra-Wide), focus lock, white balance, exposure, and zoom. Free tier works smoothly over USB; Pro adds 4K & pro controls.

Iriun Webcam Free · 4K

Windows & Mac · USB or Wi-Fi. Lightweight, clean, and supports up to 4K resolution on the free tier. Great simple option if you want quick setup without extra menus.

DroidCam Windows USB

Windows (direct) & Mac (via OBS). Low-latency USB & Wi-Fi streaming from iPad to Windows laptops. Ideal if you already run OBS or want a small, minimal client.

Part 2 · Step-by-Step USB Setup (Method 1)

Connect Your iPad Camera to Windows or Mac

Windows 10 / 11 Laptop + iPad Direct USB

  1. Install Apple USB Driver (once): Install Apple Devices from the Microsoft Store (or Apple iTunes) so Windows detects iPads reliably over USB.
  2. Install the companion apps: Install Camo Studio (reincubate.com/camo) or Iriun Webcam (iriun.com) on Windows, and install the matching app on your iPad from the App Store.
  3. Plug in USB cable & Trust: Connect your iPad to the laptop with a USB-C or Lightning data cable. Unlock the iPad and tap Trust This Computer (enter your passcode).
  4. Start the camera stream first: Open Camo (or Iriun) on both iPad and Windows. Choose your lens (Front or Rear), lock white balance/focus if desired, and turn on iPad Orientation Lock.
  5. Select inside photobooth4u: Open photobooth4u Desktop → Setup → 3 Camera → pick Camo (or Iriun Webcam) in the Camera dropdown.

MacBook (Apple Silicon / Intel) + iPad Plug & Play

  1. No Apple driver needed: macOS recognizes iPads over USB automatically.
  2. Install the companion apps: Install Camo Studio or Iriun Webcam on your Mac, and install the matching app on your iPad from the App Store.
  3. Plug in USB cable & Trust: Connect your iPad with a USB-C or Lightning data cable and tap Trust on the iPad if prompted.
  4. Approve Virtual Camera (first time only): If macOS prompts to allow the Camera System Extension under System Settings → Privacy & Security, click Allow and restart photobooth4u Desktop once.
  5. Select inside photobooth4u: Open photobooth4u Desktop → Setup → 3 Camera → pick Camo (or Iriun Webcam) and verify live framing.

Where Do Guests Look in Method 1?

Because iPadOS requires Camo / Iriun to remain open on screen to stream video, the iPad displays the camera app rather than the photobooth4u Guest Screen. Pair your iPad camera with either:
• Your Laptop Screen facing guests (compact booth), or
• An External HDMI Monitor / TV mounted below the iPad — click Setup → 7 Session → Screen & lock → Open guest screen and extend it onto the TV!

Part 3 · Want Your iPad to Be BOTH Camera & Guest Touchscreen?

Method 2: Native iPad Photobooth + Laptop Print Station

If you only need your laptop to drive a USB photo printer (DNP DS620/RX1HS, HiTi P525L, Epson L8050/L18050, Canon SELPHY, or thermal receipt printer), this is the cleanest and most secure iPad + Laptop setup — zero third-party camera apps, zero lag, and guests tap & pose right on the iPad!

3-Step Setup: Native iPad Booth + Laptop Print Station 0 Extra License Slots

  1. Run photobooth4u natively on your iPad: Open the photobooth4u iPad app (or Safari) and select the iPad’s built-in front or rear camera in Setup → 3 Camera. (Native camera preview, full Center Stage / Ultra-Wide support, and interactive Guest Screen all on one screen!)
  2. Start Print Station on your Laptop: Plug your USB printer into the laptop, open photobooth4u Print Helper, and visit photobooth4u.netlify.app/print-station/ using the same rental code. (Print Station does not consume an extra device slot!)
  3. Link the iPad to Print Station: On your iPad, go to Setup → 4 Printer and select Send to a laptop print station. Every finished photo prints silently on the laptop automatically.

Part 5 · Pre-Event Checklist & Quick Fixes

Event-Day Checklist & iPad Troubleshooting

1

Use a Data Cable

Test your USB-C or Lightning cable ahead of time. Charge-only cables will power the iPad but will never carry video to Camo / Iriun.

2

Set Auto-Lock to Never

On iPad, open Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never so the screen never sleeps and pauses your camera stream.

3

Do Not Disturb + Rotation Lock

Swipe down Control Center on iPad and turn ON Focus / Do Not Disturb and Orientation Lock so notifications and accidental rotation never disrupt shots.

4

5500K Daylight + WB Lock

Use a steady 5500K softbox or ring light facing guests. In Camo Studio, lock white balance and focus once so skin tones stay consistent all night.

ProblemQuick Fix
iPad is plugged into Windows via USB, but Camo / Iriun doesn’t see it Install Apple Devices from the Microsoft Store (or iTunes) to provide Apple’s USB driver. Unplug, unlock the iPad, replug into a direct laptop USB port, and tap Trust This Computer.
Camo / Iriun shows live video, but isn’t listed in photobooth4u Launch Camo Studio / Iriun before opening photobooth4u Desktop (restart photobooth4u once after first-time installation). On Mac, allow the Camera Extension in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
Camera feed freezes after a few minutes Check iPad Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never, keep the camera app open in the foreground (don’t swipe to another app), and use a direct USB cable instead of Wi-Fi.
Preview looks rotated sideways or zoomed in Mount the iPad in the same orientation as your print layout, enable Orientation Lock in iPad Control Center, and check Crop to layout in photobooth4u Setup → 3 Camera.
Guest preview text / signs look backwards twice or un-mirrored Disable mirroring inside Camo / Iriun and let photobooth4u’s Mirror toggle handle it cleanly so printed photos always read correctly.
Can I scan the Laptop’s Android Bridge QR code on an iPad? No — the built-in camera-sender Bridge is Android-only. Opening the laptop’s Bridge URL in iPad Safari works as a wireless Guest Screen only (great if your laptop uses a DSLR or webcam), not as an iPad camera source.
