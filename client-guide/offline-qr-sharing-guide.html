Start Here · How It Works

How Offline QR Works (Zero Internet Needed)

With the photobooth4u Offline App, photos stay on your booth device and transfer directly to the guest’s phone over a private Booth Wi-Fi hotspot (http://192.168.x.x:47777). Think of Booth Wi-Fi like a direct wireless bridge (similar to SHAREit or AirDrop) — it uses zero internet, zero data load, and no SIM card.

  • Photos save directly on your booth device (never needs internet).
  • Guests download via local Wi-Fi QR (http://<device-ip>:47777) — even if your laptop/tablet happens to be online.
  • 2 QR scans: Step 1 Join Booth Wi-Fi QR → Step 2 Photo QR.

Web Browser Version (photobooth4u.netlify.app) Cloud QR

  • Runs in Chrome/Safari with live internet and uploads photos to the cloud gallery.
  • Guests scan 1 QR code using their own 4G/5G mobile data or venue Wi-Fi — no Booth Wi-Fi needed.
  • If venue internet drops, photos wait in the upload queue until back online.

💻 Windows Laptop (Desktop App) — 100% Offline Setup No Router Needed

Your Windows laptop creates its own Booth Wi-Fi hotspot directly inside photobooth4u. Guests connect to your laptop and download photos at http://192.168.137.1:47777.

  1. 1

    Turn Windows Wi-Fi ON

    Make sure the laptop’s Wi-Fi is switched ON and Airplane Mode is OFF. You do not need to connect to any venue Wi-Fi.

  2. 2

    Open Setup → Step 6: Share & start

    Scroll to Booth Wi-Fi for guests and switch ON “Make this laptop the booth Wi-Fi (no router needed)”. This also turns on “Turn it on whenever the app opens” so it starts automatically next time.

  3. 3

    First time offline? Click “Set up (one time)”

    Windows normally blocks Mobile Hotspot when there is no internet. If the Set up (one time) button appears, click it and choose Yes on the Windows Administrator prompt. It installs Microsoft’s silent Loopback adapter + Firewall rule once — after that, offline Booth Wi-Fi starts in 1 click forever.

  4. 4

    Check or customize your Booth Wi-Fi name & password

    The app generates a unique name (e.g. photobooth4u-xxxx) and password automatically. Want your own event name? Edit the boxes under Name and password for the booth Wi-Fi (password must be 8+ characters) and click Save name & password.

  5. 5

    Verify the address & print your WIFI QR Sign

    Confirm Guests’ phones shows http://192.168.137.1:47777 ✓. Click Download WIFI QR Sign to print for the booth table — and during the event, tap the Wi-Fi button in the bottom dock anytime to pop up the Join QR on screen.

💻 Mac Laptop (Desktop App) — 100% Offline Setup Internet Sharing or Router

Mac can host its own offline Booth Wi-Fi using macOS Internet Sharing with a one-time BoothDummy loopback setup, or connect to a pocket Wi-Fi router (easiest).

Option A: Mac Hosts Its Own Booth Wi-Fi

  1. In photobooth4u, go to Setup → Step 6: Share & start.
  2. Click Set up (one time) and enter your Mac password / Touch ID. This creates the offline BoothDummy loopback service once.
  3. Switch ON “Make this laptop the booth Wi-Fi”. macOS opens System Settings → General → Sharing.
  4. Click the (i) beside Internet Sharing: set Share your connection from: BoothDummy, check To computers using: Wi-Fi, click Wi-Fi Options to set a name & password, click Done, then turn Internet Sharing ON.
  5. Back in photobooth4u, type that exact Wi-Fi name & password into the Booth Wi-Fi for guests boxes so the Join QR matches.
  6. Confirm Guests’ phones shows http://192.168.2.1:47777 ✓ (or 192.168.3.1). Click Allow if macOS Firewall asks to accept incoming connections.

Option B: Pocket Router / Phone Hotspot Fastest on Mac

  1. Turn on a pocket Wi-Fi router (no SIM/internet needed) or a spare phone’s Personal Hotspot (mobile data OFF).
  2. Join your Mac to that Wi-Fi from the top menu bar.
  3. In photobooth4u Setup → Step 6: Share & start, leave “Make this laptop the booth Wi-Fi” OFF.
  4. Click Use current Wi-Fi (allow macOS Keychain once, or type the password manually).
  5. Confirm Guests’ phones shows http://192.168.x.x:47777 ✓ and click Download WIFI QR Sign.

📱 Android Tablet / Phone (Offline App) — 100% Offline Setup Built-in Booth Wi-Fi

The Android APK creates its own local Wi-Fi directly (Wi-Fi Direct, starting with DIRECT-pb-) — no router, SIM card, or mobile data needed.

  1. 1

    Switch Android Wi-Fi ON (and Location on Android 12 or older)

    Turn your tablet/phone’s Wi-Fi ON — it does not need to join any network. On Android 12 or older, also toggle Location (GPS) ON in Quick Settings (required by Android for local Wi-Fi Direct).

  2. 2

    Turn ON “Make this tablet the booth Wi-Fi”

    Open Setup → Step 6: Share → switch ON “Make this tablet the booth Wi-Fi (no router needed)” (it says “tablet” on phones too) and tick “Turn it on whenever the app opens”.

  3. 3

    Allow the Android permission once

    Tap Allow when prompted for Nearby devices (Android 13+) or Location (Android 12 & older). Wait a few seconds for “The booth Wi-Fi is on ✓”.

  4. 4

    Optional: Customize name & password (Android 10+)

    Android requires Wi-Fi Direct names to start with DIRECT-pb-. You can edit the text after DIRECT-pb- and set an 8+ character password, then tap Save name & password. On Android 9 or older, Android picks the name/password automatically and the Join QR updates to match.

  5. 5

    Save the WIFI QR Sign & check the Wi-Fi button

    Confirm Guests’ phones shows an address with ✓ (e.g. http://192.168.49.1:47777). Tap Download WIFI QR Sign (saved in Pictures/photobooth4u).

Alternative 1: Use Android’s Own Phone Hotspot

Want a clean Wi-Fi name without the DIRECT-pb- prefix (or if Wi-Fi Direct is busy)?

  1. Tap Use the phone’s own Hotspot instead in Step 6.
  2. Turn ON Android’s native Mobile Hotspot with any name & password (mobile data can be OFF).
  3. Return to photobooth4u and type that same name & password in the Wi-Fi boxes so the Join QR matches.

Alternative 2: Pocket Router or Venue Wi-Fi

Using a Wi-Fi printer (like Canon SELPHY / Epson) or expecting 30+ guests?

  1. Keep “Make this tablet the booth Wi-Fi” OFF (when ON, Android may not use another Wi-Fi).
  2. Connect the tablet (and printer) to your pocket router or venue Wi-Fi.
  3. Type the router’s Wi-Fi name & password in the Wi-Fi boxes to generate the Join Wi-Fi QR sign.

🖥️ iPad (Offline App) — 100% Offline Setup Pocket Router or Phone Hotspot

Apple iPadOS does not allow apps to create their own Wi-Fi network directly, so there is no built-in hotspot toggle on iPad. Instead, connect the iPad to any small offline Wi-Fi router or spare phone hotspot — zero internet or SIM load needed!

  1. 1

    Power on a Pocket Wi-Fi Router or Spare Phone Hotspot

    Turn on any travel/pocket router (no SIM or internet plan required) or turn on Personal Hotspot / Mobile Hotspot on a spare phone (mobile data OFF). Tip: make sure “AP / Client Isolation” is OFF on routers.

  2. 2

    Join the iPad to that Wi-Fi

    On the iPad, open Settings → Wi-Fi and connect to that router or hotspot. If iPadOS shows “No Internet Connection”, that is normal — stay connected.

  3. 3

    Check “Guests’ phones” in Setup → Step 6: Share

    Open photobooth4u → Setup → Step 6: Share. Confirm Guests’ phones shows your iPad’s local server address with a check mark (e.g. http://192.168.x.x:47777 ✓).

  4. 4

    Enter the Wi-Fi name & password for the Join QR

    Under Booth Wi-Fi for guests, type your router or hotspot’s exact Wi-Fi name and password. The Join Wi-Fi QR appears immediately — tap Download WIFI QR Sign to print it for your table.

Using an iPad paired with a Windows/Mac Laptop Desktop App? If you opened the booth on iPad Safari from the laptop’s local link (http://<laptop-ip>:47777/ipad/...), the laptop itself hosts the Booth Wi-Fi and serves all guest QR codes — you don’t need a separate pocket router!

Part 2 · Desktop Deep-Dive (Windows & Mac)

Desktop App: Offline vs. Online — When & How to Turn On Booth Wi-Fi

Many operators get confused when they test the Desktop App at home (with internet) and then bring it to a venue (offline or on hotel Wi-Fi). Here is the exact process for both offline and online so QR sharing works every single time.

Key Fact for Desktop App Users: Even when your laptop is online (connected to home Wi-Fi, venue Wi-Fi, or Ethernet), the Offline Desktop App still saves photos locally and serves guest QR codes over local Wi-Fi (http://<laptop-ip>:47777). Turning ON Booth Wi-Fi gives guests a direct, reliable Wi-Fi connection straight to your laptop in both offline and online setups!

Scenario A: Laptop is OFFLINE No Internet / No Venue Wi-Fi

Your laptop is at an outdoor, basement, or remote venue with zero Wi-Fi or signal.

  1. Windows — Turn Booth Wi-Fi ON: Open Setup → Step 6: Share & start → toggle “Make this laptop the booth Wi-Fi” ON.
  2. One-Time Offline Unlock: If Windows says there is no connection to share, click Set up (one time) → click Yes. This adds the offline Loopback adapter + Firewall rule so Booth Wi-Fi works with zero internet. (Do this once at home before your gig so event day is instant!)
  3. Mac — Turn Booth Wi-Fi ON: Click Set up (one time) once (BoothDummy), then toggle Booth Wi-Fi ON and enable Internet Sharing (BoothDummy → Wi-Fi) in System Settings → Sharing, and type that Wi-Fi name & password in the app boxes.
  4. Check QR Ready: Confirm Guests’ phones shows http://192.168.137.1:47777 ✓ (Windows) or http://192.168.2.1:47777 ✓ (Mac).
  5. Backup Option (Zero Hotspot Needed): Plug in or switch on an offline pocket router (no SIM), keep Booth Wi-Fi OFF, join the router Wi-Fi, and click Use current Wi-Fi.

Scenario B: Laptop is ONLINE Home Wi-Fi, Venue Wi-Fi, or Ethernet

Your laptop already has internet, and you still want instant Desktop QR sharing. You have 2 choices:

  1. Way 1 — Turn Booth Wi-Fi ON anyway (Recommended at Venues!): On Windows, simply switch ON “Make this laptop the booth Wi-Fi”. Because Windows already has internet/Wi-Fi, the hotspot turns ON right away — your laptop stays online and broadcasts its own clean Booth Wi-Fi (192.168.137.1:47777)!
    Why? Hotel, mall, and ballroom Wi-Fi often turns on AP / Client Isolation (which blocks phones from opening your laptop’s QR link). Turning ON Booth Wi-Fi bypasses that block completely!
    On Mac: You need Ethernet / USB-C LAN if you want internet + Wi-Fi Internet Sharing at the same time; otherwise use Way 2 or a pocket router.
  2. Way 2 — Keep Booth Wi-Fi OFF & share on the current Wi-Fi (Home testing / Your own router): Stay connected to your home Wi-Fi or your own pocket router, leave “Make this laptop the booth Wi-Fi” OFF, and click Use current Wi-Fi. Confirm Guests’ phones shows http://192.168.x.x:47777 ✓. Any guest phone on that same Wi-Fi scans the Photo QR and downloads immediately!

Part 3 · What Guests Do at the Booth

The 2-Step Guest QR Scan Flow (All Devices)

Whether you run on Windows, Mac, Android, or iPad, the guest experience is identical: scan Wi-Fi first, then scan the Photo QR. No app to install, works on both iPhone and Android guest phones.

1

Scan the Booth Wi-Fi QR

Guest opens their phone’s Camera app, points at your printed WIFI QR Sign (or the Booth Wi-Fi QR on screen), and taps Join.

2

Tap “Stay Connected” (Important!)

When the phone warns “Wi-Fi has no internet access”, guest must tap Stay Connected / Keep Wi-Fi. Do not switch back to 4G/5G.

3

Scan Their Photo QR

After the session ends, guest points their Camera at the Photo / GIF / 360 QR on the booth screen and taps the popup link.

4

Tap “Save Photo”

The photo strip, solo shots, GIF, or 360 video opens instantly in Safari/Chrome. Guest taps Save photo straight to their camera roll!

Table Sign Tip: Print the WIFI QR Sign from Setup → Step 6: Share & start and place it right beside the booth so guests can already join the Booth Wi-Fi while waiting in line!

Part 4 · 60-Second Pre-Event Test & Fixes

Event-Day QR Checklist & Troubleshooting

1

Check the Green ✓ Address

In Setup → Step 6, verify Guests’ phones shows http://192.168.x.x:47777 ✓ before starting.

2

Test With Mobile Data OFF

Turn off 4G/5G on your own phone, scan the Wi-Fi QR, shoot 1 test photo, scan the Photo QR, and tap Save.

3

Print the WIFI QR Sign

Click Download WIFI QR Sign and put it on the table. Keep the booth’s bottom-dock Wi-Fi button ready too.

4

Keep Device Awake

Plug in charger and disable sleep/auto-lock so your laptop or tablet never drops the Booth Wi-Fi mid-event.
