Booth Wi-Fi: ON or OFF?

Setup → step 6 Share → “Booth Wi-Fi for guests”

📶
The rule: guests’ phones must be on the same Wi-Fi as the booth phone, or their photo link will not open.
TURN IT ON ✓

No router at the venue

The Redmi makes its own Wi-Fi for guests. Its name starts with DIRECT-pb-.

Make this tablet the booth Wi-Fi
Turn it on when the app opens
1 · Scan the Wi-Fi QR
→
2 · Scan the photo QR

Print on the SELPHY with the USB cable. While this is on, the phone may not use other Wi-Fi.

LEAVE IT OFF ✗

Venue has Wi-Fi / router

Join the Redmi to that Wi-Fi. Guests join the same one.

Make this tablet the booth Wi-Fi
Turn it on when the app opens
1 · Scan the Wi-Fi QR
→
2 · Scan the photo QR

Type its name and password in the Wi-Fi boxes on the same page. The SELPHY can print by USB or by that Wi-Fi.

How to turn the booth Wi-Fi on (once, before guests arrive)

  1. Switch the phone’s Wi-Fi ON. It does not need to join anything.
  2. In Setup → step 6 Share, tick “Make this tablet the booth Wi-Fi” (it says “tablet” on a phone too) and “Turn it on whenever the app opens”.
  3. Allow Nearby devices or Location if Android asks. Wait for “The booth Wi-Fi is on ✓”.
  4. Tap Download WIFI QR Sign and Download QR sign. Print both for the booth.
