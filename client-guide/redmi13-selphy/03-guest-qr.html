photobooth4u · Redmi 13 + Canon SELPHY CP1300

How guests get their photos

Two QR scans: first the Wi-Fi, then their own photo.

1

Guest takes the photos

Tap the screen, strike a pose. The Redmi counts down and takes the shots.

2

Scan the Wi-Fi QR only if booth Wi-Fi is ON

Open the phone’s Camera, point it at the Wi-Fi sign, tap Join. If it says “No internet”, tap Stay connected.

3

Scan their own photo QR

When the session ends the booth shows “Scan to get your photo”. Point the Camera at it and tap the link.

4

Open, save, share

The photos open on the guest’s phone. They save or share from there, with no app to install.

Photo won’t open? The guest’s phone probably went back to mobile data. Check it is still on the booth Wi-Fi.
Put the signs by the booth. “Point your phone camera here, then scan your photo QR at the booth.”
