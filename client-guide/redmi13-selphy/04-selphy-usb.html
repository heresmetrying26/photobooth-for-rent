photobooth4u · Redmi 13 + Canon SELPHY CP1300

Print with the SELPHY: USB cable

Best for events. It does not drop out and needs no Wi-Fi.

1
Put in the paper cassette and the ink cassette (a matching set). Switch the SELPHY on.
2
Plug the OTG adapter into the Redmi, then the USB cable into the SELPHY’s USB port.
3
Android asks “Allow photobooth4u to access the USB device?” Tick Always open, tap OK.
4
Open Setup → step 4 Printer. Choose the paper size Selphy postcard (100×148).
5
Under Printer 1, tap Canon SELPHY CP1300 (USB) · Photo (SELPHY) until it shows a ✓.Not listed, or it says “Canon printer”? Tap it and allow USB. Or open “Find a Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or Instax printer” → Find Wi-Fi / network printers (it checks USB too).
6
Tap Test print. Check the colours on the print, not only on the screen.
