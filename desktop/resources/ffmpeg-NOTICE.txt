photobooth4u and FFmpeg
=======================

photobooth4u's 360 video desk edits videos with FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org), a separate program
that is installed next to the app in the "ffmpeg" folder. photobooth4u starts it as its own process and
exchanges only files and command-line options with it.

The FFmpeg build included here is provided by the "ffmpeg-static" project
(https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static). It includes components under the GNU General Public License
(GPL), including the x264 H.264 encoder. The licence texts are in this folder (ffmpeg-static-LICENSE and
ffmpeg-static-README.md) and at https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html.

FFmpeg is a trademark of Fabrice Bellard. The FFmpeg source code, and the source of the build used here, are
available at https://ffmpeg.org/download.html and https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static. You may
replace this file with your own build of FFmpeg: the app uses the ffmpeg program in this folder.
