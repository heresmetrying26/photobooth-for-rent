What happens, second by second

✅ Works today

  • Desk QR → phone opens a page to Record now / Choose a video and sends it to the desk.
  • Android tablet "Record 360": scans the desk QR, records 6–15 s, can start when the arm moves or a guest steps in.
  • Desk Auto mode: edits every new video and makes its QR.

🛠 Software to build

  • The desk holds the recording settings and the phone reads them live (nothing typed on the phone).
  • A Start command from the desk to the phone.
  • Follow the arm: stop when the phone senses the arm has stopped.
  • iPhone path. Safari on a plain Wi-Fi address can't open the live camera or motion sensor. The realistic route is the iOS app (it already drives the camera). Record 360 is currently off in iOS.

🔧 Needs hardware / your answer

  • Turning the arm can't be done by software alone. The arm has its own motor controller.
  • What is the arm's brand / model? If it has Bluetooth/Wi-Fi control, or a remote we can wire a small Wi-Fi relay across, the desk can start and stop it.
  • Until then: the desk matches the video to the arm's spin time. The phone senses start and stop.
