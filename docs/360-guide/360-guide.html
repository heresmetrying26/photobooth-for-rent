📦 Required Hardware Checklist

  • 1. 360 Platform & Arm: Any motorized or manual 360 spinner carrying an iPhone, Android phone, iPad, or camera. Start the arm with its own remote or switch.
  • 2. Laptop Base Station: Windows 10/11 or macOS running the photobooth4u Desktop App. (All ready edits, slow-mo rendering, frames, and music run inside the laptop app; the website shares clips as recorded up to 28 MB.)
  • 3. Built-in 5GHz Booth Wi-Fi: Turn on “Make this laptop the booth Wi-Fi” in Setup → Share & start (Windows & Mac), or use a compact 5GHz booth router / OS Mobile Hotspot (no internet needed).
  • 4. Optional Crowd Screens & Printer: Spare iPad/tablet for the Sharing Station, TV/projector for the Live Wall, dye-sub/thermal printer for Spin & Print, and a Bluetooth selfie clicker.

🎥 Recording & Frame Rate Settings

  • Best Quality & FPS: Set the recording phone/camera to 1080p at 60 fps (smooth 0.5× slow-mo) or 120 fps (0.25× super slow-mo). High-FPS clips render real slow-motion frames fast with no interpolation wait.
  • Hold Portrait (9:16): Mount the phone upright for TikTok, Reels, and Stories. The studio can also crop to Square (1:1) or Landscape (16:9).
  • Lock Focus & Exposure: Tap and hold on the guest in the center of the platform before spinning so brightness does not flicker.
  • Clip Duration (8–15 s): One full rotation is ideal. Shorter clips render in seconds and save guest download time.

Custom Canva Frames, Drag Text, Music & Guest Choice

Tweak any preset without leaving the Presets tab, upload custom transparent PNG overlays from Canva, synchronize slow-mo ramps to music beat drops, or let guests choose their own look.

✨ Quick Customise (Right Under ✨ Presets)

  • Accent colour: Tap any of the 8 color swatches (Gold, White, Blush, Hot Pink, Cyan, Lime, Orange) or pick any custom hex via Own.
  • Motion chips: Switch speed style in one click (🐢 Slow-mo, 🧊 Super slow, ⚡ Speed ramp, 🎢 Fast-slow-fast, 🔁 Boomerang, ▶ Original).
  • Text quick-adjust: Snap text to ⬆ Top, ↕ Middle, or ⬇ Bottom, and resize with A− / A+.
  • Effect intensity: Toggle Off, Subtle, Normal, Bold, or click 🎲 Shuffle effect to try another particle animation.
  • Keep your own speed: Untick "Presets also set the speed and colour grade" to browse frames/effects without changing your motion settings.

🎨 Look & Frame Tab (Custom PNG & Live Physics)

  • Designer frames: Pick Wedding Floral & Gold, Gilded Art Deco / Gala, Neon Club / Cyberpunk, Classic Frame & Label Band (adjustable border color, band color & opacity), or None.
  • Upload Custom Frame PNG: Export a transparent PNG/WebP overlay in Canva (1080×1920 for 9:16 portrait, 1080×1080 square, or 1920×1080 landscape) and click Upload Custom PNG.
  • Effect Movement & Physics: Choose Trajectory (🌀 Vortex Orbit, ⬇️ Falling Cascade, ⬆️ Rising Float, 💥 Radial Burst, 🎵 Beat Pulse, 💨 Wind Drift), Location, Speed, Opacity, Size, and Wobble.
  • Format & Quality: Portrait 9:16, Square 1:1, or Landscape 16:9; export at 1080p, 720p, or 540p Light.

✍️ Text & Event Branding (Drag-to-Position)

  • 2 Independent Lines: Line 1 (Heading / Couple Names) and Line 2 (Subtitle / Date / Hashtag), with emoji support.
  • 8 Built-In Fonts: Alex Brush, Dancing Script, Cinzel, Playfair Display, Montserrat, Bebas Neue, Oswald, and Courier Typewriter.
  • WYSIWYG Canvas Control: Drag text anywhere directly on the Live 360 Studio Preview and pull the corner handle (⇲) to resize, or choose presets (Top, Upper/Lower Third, Center, Bottom) and Always on vs. Fade in / out.

🎵 Music, Beat-Drop Sync & Video Stingers

  • Sound Track: Select Music, Original video sound, or Mute. Click Choose music track for a default song, or ＋ Add tracks to assign a dedicated song to each preset look.
  • Beat-Drop Sync: Enter a Beat drop (s) timestamp (or leave Auto) so the speed ramp, Dynamic zoom punch-in, and optical Beat flash hit on the drop.
  • Intro / Outro Stingers: Click ＋ Add intro/outro clips to load short sponsor or logo MP4 clips, then set Opens with / Ends with inside ✨ Presets.

🙋 Guest Choice & Rotating Looks (Automatic Variety)

Give Each Guest a Different Look: Open 🙋 Guest choice & rotating looks in ✨ Presets and tick Give each new video a different look. Choose Shuffled, no repeats or In list order and toggle which preset chips are included.
Let Guests Pick on Screen or Phone: Tick Ask the guest to pick a look when their video arrives (10 s / 20 s / 30 s / No limit). Guests see live-rendered tiles on the booth screen—or right on the phone 360 Send page, where they can also type their name onto the video!
⚡ Fast Rendering Tip: When your camera records at 60 fps or 120 fps, leave Smoother slow motion unticked—slow-mo renders directly from real frames in seconds. Tick Faster video export (when shown) to use GPU hardware encoding, and use 540p Light on crowded booth Wi-Fi so guests download videos up to 6× faster.

2-Step QR Sharing, Spin & Print, Live Wall & Troubleshooting

Share finished 360 videos with zero venue internet, print photo strips & keychains with embedded 360 video QR codes, run a separate Sharing Station & TV Live Wall, and fix common issues fast.

📲 2-Step Offline QR & Online Sharing

  • 2-Step Booth Wi-Fi QR: Enter your booth Wi-Fi name & password once in Setup → 6 Share. Share + QR shows Step 1: Network (auto-joins Wi-Fi with no typing) and Step 2: Download (opens the video immediately).
  • Dual Online & Offline: With internet, guests use their phone's native Share sheet (TikTok, IG, WhatsApp, AirDrop). Offline, guests tap Save video (iPhone Safari: tap the download arrow / Share → Save Video).
  • One-Tap GIF & Square: Click GIF (~5 s loop) or Square beside Share + QR to attach social-ready versions to the guest's gallery.

🖨 Spin & Print (Strips & Keychains from 360)

  • Instant Physical Keepsakes: Click Spin & Print on the 360 Desk to extract frames from any 360 spin and print branded 2×6 strips, 4R prints, or 2.5×7.5″ keychains.
  • Print Mode: Choose Multi-angle sequence (fills 3-photo strips with different spin angles) or Solo (1 hero frame).
  • Video QR on the Print: Each printed strip includes a scannable QR code linking right back to the guest's 360 video! Tick and auto-print strip / keychain in Audio & FX for automatic printing.

📱 Sharing Station (Separate iPad / Tablet)

Connect an iPad or tablet to the booth Wi-Fi and open http://<laptop-ip>:47777/station. Guests step away from the 360 arm, browse finished spins (with the preset name badge shown), tap their clip, and scan their personal QR code. Resets automatically after 35 s.

📺 Live Wall & Crowd Sync (TV / Projector)

Connect a TV/projector browser to the booth Wi-Fi and open http://<laptop-ip>:47777/wall. Displays live countdown cues ("3... 2... 1... JUMP! 🎬") during recording, then pops up a celebration replay banner ("✨ New 360 Spin Ready!") with an on-screen QR code as soon as each render finishes.

Field Troubleshooting Quick Matrix

Symptom Cause Quick Fix
Can't see Presets / No Edit controls Using the website booth or an older build without video engine. Open the photobooth4u Laptop App → Extras → 360 desk → ✨ Presets (reinstall latest app from /downloads/ if needed).
Phone / Station / Wall can't reach laptop Different Wi-Fi networks or macOS/Windows firewall blocking port 47777. Turn on Make this laptop the booth Wi-Fi in Setup → Share & start (or join the same booth Wi-Fi); tap Scan QR in Record 360 and run 🔌 Connect → Test. Allow firewall access on Mac/Win.
Slow-mo is choppy or slow to render Phone recorded at 30 fps or Smoother slow motion is on for 60 fps clips. Set arm camera to 1080p @ 60 fps, untick Smoother slow motion, keep clips 8–12 s, and use 720p or 540p Light.
iPhone clip shows black preview in Desk HEVC (.mov) codec preview on some laptops. Wait 2–3 seconds—the desk auto-creates a lightweight preview copy while still rendering the final MP4 from the full original.
Guest phone drops booth Wi-Fi Phone switched back to mobile data because booth Wi-Fi has no internet. Tell Android guests to tap Stay connected on the "No internet" prompt, then scan the Step 2 QR again.
