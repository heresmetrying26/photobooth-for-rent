iPad + USB DSLR Photobooth Guide

Connect your Canon, Nikon, or Sony camera straight to your iPad with a USB-C cable for real studio photos, flash sync, and events that work without internet.

Step 1: Install the photobooth4u iPad app

iPadOS 15 or newer

After you download photobooth4u-kiosk-ipad.ipa from our Downloads page, it must be put onto the iPad from a computer (a Windows PC or a Mac). Do this once, before your event:

  1. 1
    On the computer, install Sideloadly (free, from sideloadly.io).
  2. 2
    Plug the iPad into the computer with a cable. If the iPad asks, tap Trust.
  3. 3
    Open Sideloadly, drag the .ipa file into it, type your Apple ID email, then click Start.
  4. 4
    On the iPad open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID, then tap Trust.
  5. 5
    If you see Developer Mode under Settings → Privacy & Security, turn it On and let the iPad restart.
  6. 6
    Open the photobooth4u app on the iPad and enter your code. Done!
Good to know: with a free Apple ID the app stops opening after 7 days, so re-install it (steps 2 and 3) the week of your event. A paid Apple Developer account lasts one year.

Step 2: The Single-Port iPad Hardware Wiring Chain

Because iPads have only one USB-C (or Lightning) port, you must route power and camera data through a Powered USB-C Hub with Power Delivery (PD) so the iPad never dies during a live event.

Essential Hardware Checklist

  • iPad with USB-C: iPad Pro (any), iPad Air 4/5/M2, iPad 10th Gen, or iPad mini 6. (Lightning iPads require Apple Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter).
  • Powered USB-C Hub: Must feature at least one USB-A/C data port AND a USB-C Power Delivery (PD) In port (Anker, Ugreen, Satechi).
  • High-Wattage Wall Charger: 30W–65W USB-C wall adapter plugged into the hub's PD port to power both the iPad and attached peripherals.
  • Camera USB Data Cable: Genuine camera manufacturer data cable (avoid charge-only phone cords).

Pro Event Tip: AC Dummy Battery

Do NOT rely on regular camera batteries at long weddings or corporate gigs. A DSLR in constant Live View drains a battery in 60–90 minutes.

Use a DC Coupler (Dummy Battery) Plug a dummy battery directly into a wall outlet or high-capacity power bank. Your camera stays powered on for 12+ hours with zero battery swaps mid-event.

Camera Menu Configuration by Brand

Before plugging your camera into the iPad hub, set the camera dial and menu settings to allow external PTP host tethering control.

Canon EOS

Canon EOS DSLRs & Mirrorless

EOS R, R5, R6, R8, R10, R50, RP, 5D IV, 6D II, 90D, 80D, 200D/250D, M50 II

  • Dial: Set top dial to Manual (M) mode.
  • Exposure: Shutter 1/125s, f/5.6 – f/8, ISO 200–400 (with strobe).
  • USB Mode: Set to PC Remote or Computer.
  • Wi-Fi/Bluetooth: Turn OFF in the camera menu (avoids port lockout).
  • Auto Power Off: Set to Disable so camera never sleeps.
  • SD Card: Keep a formatted SD card inside camera.
Nikon

Nikon Z & D-Series

Z5, Z6, Z7, Z50, Zfc, D850, D780, D750, D7500, D5600, D3500, D3400

  • Dial: Turn dial to Manual (M) mode.
  • Focus Mode: Set to AF-S (Single AF) with wide-area or face detection.
  • USB Connection: Set to MTP / PTP in Setup Menu.
  • Standby Timer: Set c2 (Standby Timer) to No Limit or 30 mins.
  • Flash Sync: Set built-in flash or hot-shoe speedlite to Manual.
  • Live View: Enabled automatically upon iPad connection.
Sony Alpha

Sony Alpha Mirrorless

A7 III, A7 IV, A7C, A7R, A6000, A6100, A6400, A6600, A6700, ZV-E10

  • Dial: Set mode dial to Manual (M).
  • USB Connection: MENU → Setup → PC Remote (Crucial!).
  • USB LUN Setting: Set to Multi.
  • PC Remote Settings: Save Destination → PC + Camera (keeps SD backup).
  • Smartphone Control: Set to OFF (required to allow USB tethering).
  • Power Save Start Time: Set to 30 Min or Off.

Studio Lighting & Strobe Flash Synchronization

Because the iPad triggers the physical shutter of your DSLR via PTP, you get true hot-shoe flash synchronization. Mount a Godox, Neewer, or Flashpoint wireless radio trigger (e.g., Godox Xpro or X2T) onto your camera's hot-shoe. When the countdown finishes on the iPad screen, the camera shutter fires and triggers your studio softboxes or beauty dishes with zero shutter lag.

iPadOS Kiosk Setup & USB Permissions

Configure your iPad settings to keep the screen awake, enable permanent USB accessory data access, and lock down the booth so guests cannot exit into your apps.

1. Prevent USB Disconnect (Crucial)

By default, iPadOS locks down its USB-C port after 60 minutes of inactivity to protect your device. For live events, you must disable this lock:

  1. 1
    Open iPad Settings.
  2. 2
    Tap Face ID & Passcode (or Touch ID & Passcode).
  3. 3
    Scroll down to the Allow Access When Locked section.
  4. 4
    Toggle USB Accessories to ON (Green).
Your DSLR will now stay permanently tethered without sudden mid-event disconnects.

2. Apple Guided Access (Kiosk Lock)

Lock the iPad into photobooth4u so guests cannot swipe home, view notifications, or change iPad settings:

  1. 1
    Open Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access.
  2. 2
    Turn Guided Access ON and set a 4-digit Passcode.
  3. 3
    Open the photobooth4u app.
  4. 4
    Triple-click the Top Power Button. Tap Start in the upper right.
To exit after the event: Triple-click the Top button, enter your passcode, and tap End.

3. Launching & Testing in photobooth4u

Step A: Unlock Licence

Enter your photobooth4u code on the activation screen. The iPad needs Wi-Fi once to verify; thereafter it works 100% offline.

Step B: Select Camera

In the booth top bar, tap the Camera icon. Your attached Canon, Nikon, or Sony DSLR will appear in the list. Tap it to start Live View.

Step C: Test Capture

Tap the screen to trigger a countdown. The camera fires, the photo downloads to the iPad, and the photo strip preview renders instantly!

4. Local File & Photos Storage Architecture

Every photo taken on the iPad is automatically saved in two locations on device (zero cloud required):

1. In-App Event Storage Documents/Pictures/photobooth4u/<Event>/ Full-resolution originals and finished formatted photo strips (2x6 or 4R).
2. iPad Photo Library (Camera Roll) Apple Photos App → Recents Easily AirDrop all event photos to your client's iPhone or Mac at the end of the night!

Local Wi-Fi QR Downloads & Printing

Guests scan an on-screen QR code to download their high-res photos and GIFs directly from the iPad over local Wi-Fi without needing cell service or cloud uploads.

How Local QR Downloads Work

The iPad app runs an embedded read-only HTTP server on port 47777 (LocalShareServer.swift):

  • Step 1 (Join Wi-Fi): Guests connect their smartphones to the same local Wi-Fi or pocket router as the iPad.
  • Step 2 (Scan Result QR): At the end of each session, the screen displays a QR code linking to: http://<ipad-ip>:47777/e/<event>/
  • Step 3 (Instant Save): The guest's phone opens the branded mobile gallery with a Save Photo button.
Zero data usage: All photos transfer locally over the venue router at up to 100 Mbps!

Wireless Printing from iPad

Print physical 4R photo cards or 2x6 strips instantly:

  • AirPrint Dye-Sub Printers: Connect directly to Canon SELPHY (CP1300, CP1500), Epson PictureMate, or DNP/HiTi via wireless print servers (WPS Pro).
  • Receipt Photobooth: Connect to thermal receipt printers over Bluetooth or network IPP.
  • Auto-Cut: Set your layout to 2x6 dual strips; AirPrint sends the cutting command directly to the printer spooler.
Turn on Silent Auto-Print in booth settings to skip all confirmation dialogs.

  • [ ] Guided Access locked (Triple-click top button).

Troubleshooting: What If the Camera Won't Connect?

1. Unplug & replug: Unplug the USB-C cable from the iPad and plug it back in. iOS will re-initialize the ICDeviceBrowser session.
2. Check USB mode: On Sony, confirm USB is set to PC Remote. On Canon, turn off Wi-Fi/Bluetooth in camera menu.
3. Check power: Ensure the iPad hub's power LED is glowing. A hub without enough current cannot handshake with a DSLR sensor.
