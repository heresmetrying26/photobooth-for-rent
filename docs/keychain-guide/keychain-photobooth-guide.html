Photobooth Keychain Printing System

How to Print Keychain Strips with Frame Collage

A complete operator guide for preparing, previewing, and gang-printing acrylic keychain inserts at 100% true scale using custom frames on 4R, A4, and Letter paper.

Why Exact Scaling is Critical for Keychains

Acrylic keychain casings have fixed, rigid cavities. If your print is even 1 mm too wide, it will buckle or wrinkle; if it is scaled down by a "Fit to Page" driver setting, it leaves loose gaps inside the casing. This guide walks you through setting up pixel-perfect 1:3 keychain strips (2.5 × 7.5 in and 2 × 6 in) and printing multiple copies on a single sheet.

Paper Size vs. Keychain Sizing Matrix

Paper Size Paper Dimensions Max Keychain Size (100% Scale) Capacity per Sheet Status

US Letter 216 × 279 mm (8.5 × 11 in) 2.5 × 7.5 in (63.5 × 190.5 mm) 3 upright side-by-side Exact 100% Scale
8R / 8×10 203 × 254 mm (8 × 10 in) 2.5 × 7.5 in (63.5 × 190.5 mm) 3 upright side-by-side Exact 100% Scale
4R (4×6 in) 102 × 152 mm (4 × 6 in) 2.0 × 6.0 in (Classic Mini Strip) 2 upright side-by-side Exact 100% Scale
4R (4×6 in) 102 × 152 mm (4 × 6 in) 2.5 × 7.5 in (Requires shrinking) 1 – 2 strips Shrunk to ~80% (2.0×6.0 in)
Critical Rule on 4R Paper: Standard 4R paper is physically 6.0 inches tall. A 7.5 in strip physically exceeds 4R paper length. If you need true 2.5 × 7.5 in acrylic inserts, you must load A4, Letter, or 8R paper. If using 4R paper, purchase 2.0 × 6.0 in or 1.8 × 5.5 in keychain blanks.

Frame Setup

Step 1 & Step 2

Designing Your Template & Uploading into Booth

  1. Design at 300 DPI Resolution:
    Create your frame canvas in Photoshop, Canva, or Illustrator:
    • 2.5 × 7.5 in: 750 × 2250 px (or 1500 × 4500 px for 600 DPI).
    • 2.0 × 6.0 in: 600 × 1800 px (for 4R photo paper).
  2. Cut Out Transparent Photo Windows:
    Leave 2, 3, or 4 cutout boxes where guest photos should appear. The windows must have an alpha transparent background or pure solid white (#FFFFFF).
  3. Maintain 3 mm Safe Margins:
    Keep names, wedding dates, and logos at least 35 px inside the cut border so trimming does not clip them. Export as a transparent PNG.
  4. Upload in Step 3 (Layout / Design):
    In booth setup, go to Step 3 → Frames with photo spots → Click + Add frame. The booth auto-detects all windows and confirms: strip · fits 2.5x7.5 keychain ✓
Step 3 Layout Cards: Select the Frame collage card to arrange multiple keychain strips on a single sheet.

Template Checklist

  • Aspect Ratio: Exactly 1:3 vertical ratio (e.g., 750 × 2250 px).
  • Color Space: sRGB, 8-bit PNG with transparent alpha channel.
  • Photo Windows: 100% transparent cutouts or pure white (#FFFFFF).
  • Safe Margins: Keep text ≥ 35 px (3 mm) away from cut lines.
Photo Window Detection: The booth automatically counts transparent cutout rectangles. If your frame has 3 windows, the booth automatically programs each session to capture 3 shots.

Sheet Arrangement

Step 3 & Step 4

Arranging Multiple Keychains on the Sheet

1. Sheet Controls (Step 3)

In the Frames on this sheet panel:

  • Put Frame on Sheet: Tap your uploaded frame (it will show a checkmark ✓).
  • Sheet Size: Choose A4, Letter, or 8×10 for 2.5 × 7.5 in keychains (or 4×6 for 2x6 mini strips).
  • Pattern: Pick 3 strips for A4 upright, or 2 side by side for 4R.
  • Add Copies: Tap + Add a copy so duplicate strips fill all slots. Captured photos fill all strips at once!
Sheet Controls: Pick the frame, set Sheet size to A4, select 3 strips, and add copies.

2. Interactive Sheet Preview

Click Open preview (eye icon) to inspect the sheet:

  • Spacing: Drag any strip to fine-tune margins between cuts.
  • Photo Population: All 3 strips populate with the session photos.
  • Cut Guidelines: Dashed gray lines indicate where to trim.
Preview Canvas: 3 real-size keychains laid out on A4 paper with dashed cutting lines.
Operator Tip — Test on Plain Paper: Before printing on premium 260 gsm photo paper, print 1 test copy on ordinary office paper. Hold the cut paper behind an empty acrylic casing to double-check that window borders and text margins align perfectly.

Printer Driver & Assembly

Step 5 & Assembly

Printer Driver Scaling & Acrylic Assembly

1. Print Settings & Keychain Mode

Print Settings UI: Select A4 paper and toggle ON Keychain strips at real size (2.5×7.5 in). The booth automatically calculates 3 strips per page at 100% scale.
Driver Setting Required Value Why
Scale / Sizing 100% / Actual Size Never choose "Fit to Page" or "Shrink". It distorts the 2.5 × 7.5 in dimensions.
Borderless OFF (Normal Margins) Borderless mode zooms the page by 2% to 4% to bleed off edges, which enlarges the strip.
Media Type Photo Paper Glossy Ensures rich contrast and droplet density.
Print Quality High / Best Produces sharp text, dates, and fine lines.

2. Trimming & Acrylic Assembly

4-Step Flow: Print at 100% → Trim along dashed guidelines → Slide into acrylic casing → Snap cover shut.

Assembly Best Practices

  • Paper Weight: Use 200 to 260 gsm glossy or luster photo paper. Thinner paper curls; thicker stock prevents the lid from clicking shut.
  • Trimming: Use a rotary trimmer or craft knife. Cut right along the inside of the dashed gray lines.
  • Double-Sided: Place two trimmed strips back-to-back inside the casing so both sides display photos.
  • Snap Shut: Press the clear acrylic lid firmly into the recess until both locking tabs click.
Summary of Dimensions:
• Keychain Strip (A4/Letter): 750 × 2250 px (2.5 × 7.5 in)
• Mini Strip (4R Paper): 600 × 1800 px (2.0 × 6.0 in)
• Resolution: 300 DPI • Aspect Ratio: Exact 1:3 vertical
