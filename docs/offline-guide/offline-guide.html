★ Event day

Quick start

Do these in order. The app must be installed and activated already (pages 3 to 5).

  1. 1

    Plug everything in

    Laptop on its charger. Guest screen by HDMI, set to Extend (Windows: Win + P → Extend). Camera on a full or dummy battery. Printer on, paper loaded.

  2. 2

    Start the booth Wi-Fi

    Connect the laptop to a pocket Wi-Fi router or a phone hotspot, and have guests join the same one. It doesn't need internet: a router with no SIM works, and a phone hotspot works with mobile data turned off. Guests only need to reach the laptop. (The laptop's own Windows "Mobile hotspot" won't turn on unless the laptop is already connected to a network, so don't rely on it. Macs can't host their own Wi-Fi at all.)

  3. 3

    Open photobooth4u

    From the Start menu (Windows) or Applications (Mac). It opens straight into the booth.

  4. 4

    Choose the camera

    Setup → 5 Session → Camera. Windows: Nikon / Canon camera over USB. Mac: DSLR / mirrorless over USB, then pick the camera. Or a webcam / HDMI capture card.

  5. 5

    Check Setup and Share & start

    Design, name, layout as usual. In Share & start, check the event name, Photos on this laptop and Guests' phones (it shows the address guests use). Type the booth Wi-Fi name and password if guests don't know it.

  6. 6

    Choose the printers

    In Photo Session tap 🖨 Print settings, pick each paper size and its printer. Printing is built in: no Print Helper, no pop-up.

  7. 7

    Test with your phone

    Take one session. Join the booth Wi-Fi on your phone, scan the photo QR, tap Save photo. Print once.

  8. 8

    Open the guest screen

    In Setup → 5 Session, tap Open guest screen. You're ready.

01 · Once per laptop

Install the app

We send you the installer link. Pick the one for your laptop.

Windows 10 / 11
  1. 1

    Run the installer

    Double-click photobooth4u Setup….exe.

  2. 2

    "Windows protected your PC"

    Click More info → Run anyway. (The app isn't signed with a paid certificate yet; this is normal.)

  3. 3

    Allow admin once

    Click Yes. It lets guests' phones reach the laptop on the booth Wi-Fi.

macOS
  1. 1

    Open the .dmg

    Use the arm64 file for Apple Silicon (M1–M4), x64 for Intel. Drag photobooth4u into Applications.

  2. 2

    First open: right-click → Open

    If macOS still refuses: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

  3. 3

    Allow connections

    When macOS asks to accept incoming network connections, click Allow. That's the guests' QR.

New version? The app tells you when one is out. Your activation, designs and photos stay. See Updating the app (page 7) and Pop-ups (page 8).

02 · Once per laptop

Activate this laptop

  1. 1

    Connect to the internet (just this once)

    Any Wi-Fi or phone hotspot.

  2. 2

    Type your code

    The app opens on Activate this laptop. Type your PB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX code and tap Activate.

  3. 3

    Done

    The booth opens. From now on it works with no internet. The licence is tied to this laptop, so copying the app to another one doesn't work: activate each laptop with your code.

No internet at all?

Tap No internet? Activate without internet. The app shows a Machine ID (WIN-… / MAC-…).

Send us your code and that Machine ID (e.g. by text from your phone). We send back an activation key.

Paste it in the box and tap Activate.

03 · Camera

Use your DSLR / mirrorless camera

Over USB the app presses the real shutter, so your camera's settings, focus and flash all work. Nothing extra to install.

Windows
  • Nikon, Canon EOS (DSLR, M and R) and Sony Alpha. Built into the app.
  • Plug the camera straight into the laptop with a data USB cable, switch it on, and pick Nikon / Canon / Sony camera over USB. After that it reconnects by itself.
  • Canon: close EOS Utility. Older models (60D, 70D, 600D, 7D…): Menu → Live View shoot: Enable. Newer: Choose USB connection app → Photo Import / Remote Control. Wi-Fi off.
  • Sony: on the camera set USB Connection → PC Remote (MENU → Setup → USB) and close Imaging Edge. If it won't connect, use its webcam mode (USB Streaming) or an HDMI capture card.
Mac
  • Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm and most others.
  • Canon: Photo Import / Remote Control USB mode. Nikon: nothing to set.
  • Quit Photos, Image Capture and EOS Utility if they open.
  • Pick DSLR / mirrorless over USB, choose the camera, Connect.
For every camera
• Mode dial on P, A/Av, S/Tv or M; save as JPEG (or RAW + JPEG). • Auto power off: disable. Use a dummy battery for long events. • SD card in the camera. • Webcams and HDMI capture cards work too (no flash).

04 · Printing

Printing

  1. 1

    Set up the printer once

    Install its driver and do one test print from the laptop. In the printer's own preferences set the paper size, borderless and, for 2×6 strips, cut.

  2. 2

    Choose a printer per paper size

    In Photo Session → 🖨 Print settings, pick a printer for each size (e.g. 4R on one, 2×6 strips on another). The app remembers it.

  3. 3

    Auto-print

    Print once to test, then tick Auto-print every session. Prints go straight to the printer, no pop-up.

Good to know
  • The Print Helper isn't needed: it's built into the app.
  • Same printer for two sizes? Add it to the laptop twice, each with its own defaults and name.
  • The paper sizes in your package appear in Print settings.

05 · Sharing without internet

Guests' photos over Wi-Fi

Every photo is saved on the laptop. The laptop also serves a small photo page on the booth Wi-Fi, so guests save their photo straight to their phone. Nothing is uploaded.

Guest joins the booth Wi-Fi→Scans their photo QR→Taps Save photo
  1. 1

    One Wi-Fi for laptop and guests

    A small pocket router at the booth (no SIM or internet needed), or a phone hotspot (mobile data can be off). Venue Wi-Fi works too, if guests' phones can join it. The laptop's own Windows "Mobile hotspot" needs the laptop to be connected to a network first, so use a router or phone hotspot instead.

  2. 2

    Check Share & start

    Guests' phones shows the address guests open, with a ✓. If it says the laptop isn't on a Wi-Fi network, connect it first.

  3. 3

    Optional: Join Wi-Fi QR

    Type the Wi-Fi name and password under Booth Wi-Fi for guests. Guests then see a Join Wi-Fi QR next to their photo QR: scan it first, then the photo QR.

  4. 4

    Print the QR sign

    Under QR sign, save the sign with the Wi-Fi and All photos QR codes and print it (A5 or bigger) for the booth table.

Photos on this laptop

Saved in Pictures/photobooth4u/<event name>/, one file per session.

Open photos folder and Change folder are in Share & start. Copy the folder to a USB drive or upload it for your client after the event.

Guests can't open the photo?

Their phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the laptop (phones sometimes switch back to mobile data: turn data off, or join the Wi-Fi again). The guests' page works until the app is closed.

Guest payments offline

Vouchers

QR payments (GCash, Maya) need the internet. Vouchers work offline: set Guest payment to Vouchers in Setup → 5 Session, sell the printed codes at the table, and each code works once.

06 · New versions

Updating the app

When a new version is out, a bar appears at the top of the booth. It needs internet, so update before the event at home or on any Wi-Fi, never on event day. Your activation, designs and photos stay.

Windows
  1. 1

    The bar says "Version … is ready"

    The app downloads it by itself in the background. You can keep using the booth.

  2. 2

    Tap Restart now

    Later is fine too: it then installs when you close the app.

  3. 3

    Click Yes when Windows asks

    "Do you want to allow this app to make changes?" is normal. Click Yes.

  4. 4

    Done

    The app closes and opens again by itself on the new version.

macOS
  1. 1

    The bar says "Version … is out"

    Tap Download. It shows the progress; keep using the booth meanwhile.

  2. 2

    A window opens by itself

    The new version arrives in your Downloads folder as a disk image. Show it again reopens the window.

  3. 3

    Drag it into Applications

    Drag photobooth4u onto Applications and choose Replace.

  4. 4

    Quit the app and open it again

    The bar is gone and you're on the new version.

Laptop with no internet? It keeps working as it is. To update, connect it to any Wi-Fi or phone hotspot once, open the app, and the bar appears within a few hours (or straight away after you close and reopen the app).

07 · Pop-ups you may see

Pop-ups: what to click

The app isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows and macOS ask a few extra questions the first time. These are normal. Here is what to answer.

Windows pop-upWhat to do
"Windows protected your PC"Click More info, then Run anyway. Seen when you first run the installer.
"Do you want to allow this app to make changes to your device?"Click Yes. Seen when installing and updating. It lets guests' phones reach the laptop on the booth Wi-Fi.
Windows Defender Firewall asks to allow photobooth4uTick Private networks and click Allow access, so guests' phones can open their photo.
Mac pop-upWhat to do
"photobooth4u can't be opened" / "Apple cannot check it"Right-click the app and choose Open, then Open again. Or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
"photobooth4u wants to use your confidential information stored in 'photobooth4u Safe Storage' in your keychain"It's the app's own saved settings. Type your Mac login password and click Always Allow (it won't ask again). It may ask again after an update.
"Do you want the application to accept incoming network connections?"Click Allow. That is what lets guests scan the QR and open their photo.
"An item named photobooth4u already exists"Click Replace (while updating).
A pop-up that isn't here? Don't guess: take a photo or screenshot of it and message us (Facebook Messenger, m.me/roktetnachi). Never type your code or password into a window that appeared from a website or a message.

08 · Your licence

Your licence

How long it works
  • Lifetime: renews by itself whenever the laptop has internet. With no internet it keeps working for 75 days + 30 days' grace, then asks to connect once.
  • 6-Month Pass / per-event: works until the code's end date, with or without internet.
  • Changes we make for your code (print sizes, vouchers, retakes…) arrive when the app starts with internet, or tap Check for changes in Share & start.
New laptop or reinstalling?
  • On the old laptop: Share & start → Deactivate this laptop. That frees its device slot.
  • Activate the new laptop with the same code.
  • Old laptop lost or broken? Message us and we free its slot.
  • Don't set the laptop's clock back: the booth locks until the clock is right again.
