Part 1: Hardware & Connectivity

Core Hardware Capabilities

Cameras, Printers & Offline Networking

Understanding how each platform interfaces with external photography equipment, commercial printing engines, and guest network connections.

📷 DSLR & Mirrorless USB Control (PTP)
Allows the booth software to command the camera's hardware shutter, synchronize external studio strobes/flashes, pull the live viewfinder video stream, and instantly download high-resolution RAW/JPEG captures.
Desktop: Native (No Zadig) Android: USB-OTG Host Online: WebUSB (Chrome only)
🔍 Supported Camera Brands & Models
• Canon EOS: Rebel T3i–T8i, 60D, 70D, 80D, 90D, 7D, 5D Mk III/IV, EOS R, RP, R10, R50, R6.
• Nikon: D3200, D3400, D5300, D5600, D7100, D7200, D750, D850, Z50, Zfc, Z5, Z6.
• Sony Alpha: A7 III, A7 IV, A6000, A6400, A6600 (Set USB to PC Remote).
• Others (Fujifilm, Lumix, Olympus): Supported via clean HDMI capture or USB webcam mode.
Canon PTP Nikon MTP/PTP Sony PC Remote HDMI CamLink
💻 macOS Camera Conflict Breaker (camfree)
On macOS, system daemons like ptpcamerad and Image Capture automatically hijack plugged-in DSLRs. The Mac desktop app automatically terminates these processes on launch so photobooth4u connects without permission errors.
Mac App Exclusive Automated Release
🧾 Bluetooth & USB Thermal Receipt Printing
Direct hardware printing for trending 58mm and 80mm receipt photobooths. The Android app features a dedicated Kotlin RFCOMM SPP bridge with Floyd-Steinberg dithering and 256-line raster slicing to prevent buffer overflow on low-memory receipt printers.
Android: Direct Bluetooth SPP Desktop: System Spooler 58mm / 80mm
🖨️ Commercial Dye-Sub & Inkjet Printers
• Dye-Sub Photo Printers: DNP DS620, RX1HS, HiTi P525L, Citizen CX-02, Sinfonia CS2, Mitsubishi CP-D70DW. (Supported natively on Desktop with zero-dialog silent auto-cut).
• Portable & Inkjets: Canon Selphy CP1300/1500, Epson EcoTank L-series, Brother, HP. (Desktop via driver; Android via driverless IPP/Mopria).
Desktop: Multi-Queue Routing Android: IPP / ESC/P-R
📡 Built-in Local Share Server (Port 47777+)
Both Desktop and Android run a local micro web server. When guests scan the Photo QR, they connect over local Wi-Fi directly to the laptop/tablet:
http://<device-ip>:47777/e/<event>/. Includes HTTP 206 Range headers so smartphones stream MP4s smoothly without lag.
Desktop Server Android Server Zero Data / SIM
📶 Autonomous Wi-Fi Hosting Comparison (No External Router Needed)
How to let guests scan QR codes in venues with no existing Wi-Fi or phone signal:
Windows Desktop: Uses NetworkOperatorTetheringManager via PowerShell. Automatically configures Microsoft Loopback adapter and firewall rules to host a local hotspot with zero internet.
Android APK: Activates Wi-Fi Direct group (DIRECT-pb-photobooth4u-...) at 192.168.49.1 or Local-Only Hotspot. Generates instant Join-Wi-Fi QR for guests.
Mac Desktop & Online: Connects to any portable pocket router or mobile phone hotspot (no cellular data required on the router, just a local network).

Part 2: Features, FX & Monetization

Experience, Creative & Monetization

Interactive Experience, Design & Payments

From automated voice assistants and roving photographers to cashless PayMongo QR payments and 360 spin desks.

🎙️ Virtual Assistant & Voice Countdown
Interactive synthesized voice prompts that hype up guests and keep the photo queue moving: "Get ready… Three, Two, One… Smile!". Prompts automatically sync with countdown numbers and support English and Tagalog device voices.
Online: Web Speech Desktop: Native TTS Android: TextToSpeech API
🚶‍♂️ Roving Photoman / Photographer Mode
Untether the photographer from the booth! A roving photographer roams the venue, takes pictures with their phone, scans the booth's Roving QR code, and uploads photos over local Wi-Fi. The laptop automatically places them into the event's Canva frame and sends them to the printer.
Desktop Built-in Local Wi-Fi Upload Instant Auto-Print
🔒 Lock Photobooth & Kiosk Protection
Prevents guests, curious clients, or kids from messing with booth configurations:
• Desktop/Online: Passcode-protected admin settings dock.
• Android APK: Immersive sticky full-screen (hides navigation/status bars), screen keep-awake (FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON), and back-button interception.
Passcode Lock Android Kiosk Mode Unattended Operation
📸 Live Event Gallery & One-Click Reprints
Operators can open the "All Photos" drawer in the bottom bar at any time to review past captures. Tapping any photo reveals an instant "Print photo" button to produce extra copies on demand on the designated printer without opening file explorer.
Desktop: Direct Print Android: Local Gallery Online: Cloud Drawer
💳 Payments: QR PayMongo vs. Offline Vouchers
• Online (PayMongo QR): Dynamic QR codes displayed on screen for GCash, Maya, ShopeePay, and banks. Camera and printing only unlock once the webhook confirms payment.
• Offline (Single-Use Vouchers): Sell pre-printed numbered codes at the registration desk. Guests enter code on the booth touch screen to initiate a session.
Online: GCash & Maya Desktop: Vouchers Android: Vouchers
🎥 Video Guestbook & Motion Modes
• Video Guestbook: Guests record a 5–30 second greeting with microphone sound. Generates an instant Video QR code to download.
• Animated GIF Mode: Generates a looping GIF alongside photo strips.
• Printable Flipbooks: Decomposes a 6-second clip into 16–40 cards formatted for stack-cut collation with an animated QR on the cover.
Video Guestbook Animated GIF Printable Flipbook

Choose Offline Desktop when:

You run full-scale wedding or event booths with a DSLR/Mirrorless camera, studio flash strobes, dual screens (TV for guests), and a dye-sub printer (DNP, HiTi) for instant high-volume cutting.

Choose Android APK when:

You want an ultra-portable, battery-powered setup, a mobile roving tablet stand, or a trending low-cost Thermal Receipt Photobooth printing directly to Bluetooth receipt rolls.

Choose Online (Cloud) when:

Your venue has high-speed Wi-Fi, you want automated cashless QR payments (GCash/Maya) before guests shoot, or you run self-service iPad studio booths with cloud backup.

Choose Online + Offline Combo for:

Maximum business versatility. Handle indoor venue events with no cell service using the Desktop/Android apps, and switch to Cloud Online when hosting self-shoot studios or cashless pop-ups!
