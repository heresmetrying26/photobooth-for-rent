The Three Versions Explained

Web / Cloud Platform

🌐 Online Booth

Runs in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge) on PC, Mac, iPad, or Phone
  • No Installation Needed: Just open the link, enter your code, and begin taking photos immediately.
  • Pay With QR: Collect payments easily via GCash, Maya, or ShopeePay before guests shoot.
  • Google Drive Auto-Backup: Automatic cloud backup of all photos and animated GIFs.
  • 7-Day Online Gallery: Guests get a link to browse and download all event photos online.
  • What's Needed: Requires a steady Wi-Fi or mobile data connection at the venue.
Best for: Studios with Wi-Fi & iPad kiosks
Standalone Computer App

💻 Desktop (Win/Mac)

Installable software for Windows 10/11 laptops/PCs and Mac (Apple Silicon & Intel)
  • 100% True Offline: Connect to internet once to activate; zero internet needed at events.
  • DSLR & Mirrorless Cameras: Connect Canon, Nikon, or Sony cameras via USB cable for sharp photos and flash sync.
  • Fast Automatic Printing: Automatically prints 2x6 strips and 4R postcards silently without pop-up dialogs.
  • Built-in Laptop Hotspot: Windows laptop creates its own Wi-Fi so guests can download photos without a router.
  • Second Screen for Guests: Plug in a TV or second monitor via HDMI so guests can see a live view of themselves.
Best for: Pro event rentals, weddings, & high volume
Tablet App

📱 Android Tablet

Dedicated app for Android tablets (Samsung Galaxy Tab, Lenovo, Xiaomi, and more)
  • Thermal Receipt Printing: Connects directly via Bluetooth to 58mm & 80mm receipt printers for vintage booths.
  • Kiosk Screen Lock: Keeps screen awake all day, hides home/back buttons, and prevents guests from closing the app.
  • Built-in Tablet Wi-Fi: Tablet shares photos directly to guest phones via local Wi-Fi without needing internet.
  • Connects to DSLR: Plug in Canon, Nikon, or Sony cameras using a simple USB-C adapter cable.
  • Ultra-Portable: Lightweight, cable-free, battery-powered setup that you can carry anywhere.
Best for: Receipt photobooths & mobile tablet stands

Cameras, Printers & Offline Downloads

How each version connects to your photography equipment, printers, and guests' phones without requiring technical setup.

📷 DSLR & Mirrorless Cameras (USB Cable)
Plug your camera into your laptop or tablet with a standard USB cable. The booth controls the camera's shutter button, fires your studio flash or ring light, shows a live camera preview on screen, and transfers sharp photos in seconds.
Desktop: Plug & Play Android: USB Adapter Online: Chrome Browser

• Canon: Rebel T3i–T8i, 60D, 70D, 80D, 90D, 7D, 5D Mk III/IV, EOS R, RP, R10, R50, R6, and more.
• Nikon: D3200, D3400, D5300, D5600, D7100, D7200, D750, D850, Z50, Zfc, Z5, Z6, and more.
• Sony: A7 III, A7 IV, A6000, A6400, A6600 (simply set USB mode to PC Remote).
• Webcams & Others: Any USB webcam or HDMI capture card (Cam Link) is supported.
Canon Nikon Sony Webcams
💻 Mac Automatic Camera Connection
On Mac laptops, system photo apps can sometimes lock the camera when you plug it in. Our Mac app automatically frees up the camera on launch so it connects right away with zero setup errors or permissions headaches.
Mac App Exclusive Automatic Camera Fix
🧾 Thermal Receipt Printers (58mm & 80mm)
Perfect for trending, low-cost receipt photobooths! Print photos onto black-and-white paper rolls (like a store receipt). On Android tablets, simply connect via Bluetooth and print clear vintage photos with zero ink required.
Android: Direct Bluetooth Desktop: USB or Bluetooth 58mm & 80mm Rolls
🖨️ Photo Printers & Studio Printers
• Commercial Dye-Sub Printers: DNP DS620, RX1HS, HiTi P525L, Citizen CX-02, Sinfonia CS2, Mitsubishi CP-D70DW. (Prints cut 2x6 strips or 4R postcards automatically and silently).
• Home & Portable Printers: Canon Selphy CP1300/1500, Epson EcoTank (L-series), Brother, HP.
Desktop: Automatic Silent Print Android: Wi-Fi Printers
📡 Direct Phone Downloads (No Internet Needed)
Both Desktop and Android share photos locally. When guests scan the Photo QR code with their phone camera, they connect directly to the laptop or tablet over Wi-Fi. Photos and videos save smoothly even in basements with zero mobile signal!
Desktop App Android App No SIM or Data Needed
📶 Built-in Wi-Fi Hotspot (No External Router Needed)
How guests connect to download their photos in venues with bad phone signal or no venue Wi-Fi:
Windows Laptop: Your laptop turns into its own Wi-Fi hotspot with one click. Guests connect to it without needing any internet or Wi-Fi router.
Android Tablet: The tablet creates its own local Wi-Fi connection with a quick QR code for guests to scan and join.
Mac & Online: Connect your laptop to any portable pocket Wi-Fi or phone hotspot (no internet data needed, just a shared local network).

Choose Offline Desktop when:

You run full-scale wedding or event booths with a DSLR/Mirrorless camera, studio flash strobes, dual screens (TV for guests), and a dye-sub printer (DNP, HiTi) for instant high-volume cutting.

Choose Android Tablet when:

You want an ultra-portable, battery-powered setup, a mobile roving tablet stand, or a trending low-cost Thermal Receipt Photobooth printing directly to Bluetooth receipt rolls.

Choose Online (Cloud) when:

Your venue has high-speed Wi-Fi, you want automated cashless QR payments (GCash/Maya) before guests shoot, or you run self-service iPad studio booths with cloud backup.

Choose Online + Offline Combo for:

Maximum business versatility. Handle indoor venue events with no cell service using the Desktop/Android apps, and switch to Cloud Online when hosting self-shoot studios or cashless pop-ups!
