Part 01 · Hardware Architecture

Single-Port Samsung Tablet Wiring

Samsung Galaxy Tablets (Tab A9, A9+, S6 Lite, S7/S8/S9) only have one USB-C port. Here is how you keep the tablet continuously charged while simultaneously connecting a DSLR camera and receipt printer.

Crucial Component

1. The USB-C Powered Hub with PD

Do NOT buy an ordinary passive OTG dongle (the small ₱150 adapter with 1 USB port). An ordinary OTG cable blocks charging—your tablet battery will drop to 0% after 2 to 3 hours of live booth operation.

Must-Have Specifications:

  • USB-C Power Delivery (PD) In: Must support 60W or 100W PD input. You plug the wall charger directly into this port.
  • USB 3.0 Type-A Ports (at least 2): One connects to your DSLR camera cable; the second can connect a USB receipt printer or USB flash drive.
  • Proven Brands: Ugreen 5-in-1 / 6-in-1, Baseus Metal Glam, Anker 332 USB-C Hub.
Power & Cables

2. Wall Charger & Power Requirements

A standard 10W or 15W phone charger is too weak. The hub itself consumes 5W–10W, leaving too little wattage for the Samsung tablet.

Recommended Power Setup:

  • Charger: Use a 45W or 65W GaN USB-C PD Charger (e.g. Samsung 45W Super Fast Charger, Ugreen 65W GaN, or Baseus 65W).
  • Cable: 100W E-Marker USB-C to USB-C cable (minimum 2 meters) to reach the venue wall socket safely.
  • Result: The tablet maintains 100% battery all day even with the screen at max brightness and DSLR live view active!
💡 Paalala sa Samsung Tablet USB Connections (Sagot sa Tanong ng Kliyente)
Dahil iisa lang ang USB-C port ng Samsung tablet, USB-C Hub na may "Power Delivery (PD)" port ang solusyon. Isaksak ang 45W/65W charger sa PD port ng Hub, ang DSLR camera sa USB-A port ng Hub, at ang thermal receipt printer naman ay ikonekta via Bluetooth (wireless) para walang kalat na kable sa booth!
Dye-Sub Photo Printers vs. Thermal Receipt Printers

Thermal Receipt Printers (XP-80C, TM-T82X, Rongta): Work 100% natively on Android tablets via Bluetooth or USB. Zero computer needed!

Dye-Sub Printers (DNP DS620, HiTi P525L, Citizen): Dye-sub photo printers require Windows/Mac raster drivers. If you want to use dye-sub prints alongside a tablet, run our Laptop Print Station over Wi-Fi (the tablet sends the photo wirelessly to the laptop print queue). For receipt booths, a thermal printer is direct and laptop-free.

Part 02 · Devices & Imaging

Camera & Thermal Printer Setup

How to configure your camera for crisp shots and calibrate your thermal receipt printer for sharp, high-contrast black & white photo strips with zero ink.

Camera Options

Connecting Your DSLR / Mirrorless

The Android photobooth4u app features a built-in USB PTP camera driver that connects directly to Canon, Nikon, and Sony cameras.

  1. 1

    Connect USB Cable

    Plug the camera USB cable into the USB-C Hub. Turn the camera ON. Android will show a prompt: "Open photobooth4u to access camera?" → Tap Always Allow / OK.

  2. 2

    Set Camera Settings

    Canon EOS: Mode dial to P, Av, Tv, or M. Enable "Live View shoot". Set Auto Power Off to "Disable". Quality: JPEG (Fine/Normal).
    Sony Alpha: Menu → USB Connection → PC Remote. USB LUN Setting: Multi.
    Nikon: USB mode to PTP / MTP. Mode dial to M or Auto.

  3. 3

    Select in App

    In photobooth4u, open Setup → Session → Camera and select "DSLR / Mirrorless over USB cable". The live preview will appear on the tablet screen instantly.

Budget Option: Tablet Front Camera

You don't even need a DSLR! The Samsung Tab front-facing camera with our built-in Screen Flash feature creates a clean, authentic retro selfie experience with zero extra cables.

Thermal Printer Integration

Thermal printers use heat-sensitive paper roll—no ink, ribbons, or cartridges. They are immune to smudging and print in under 3 seconds.

Pairing via Bluetooth on Android:
  1. 1

    Pair in Android Settings

    Turn printer ON. Android Settings → Connected devices → Bluetooth → Pair new device. Tap your printer name (e.g. XP-80C, POS-80). PIN is usually 0000 or 1234.

  2. 2

    Select in photobooth4u

    Go to Print Settings → Receipt 80mm (or 58mm). Toggle Bluetooth thermal receipt printer → Connect.

  3. 3

    Halftone & Dithering

    photobooth4u automatically applies Floyd-Steinberg dithering so portraits and QR codes print sharp without blurry grey blocks.

📌 Tip sa Thermal Paper Roll
Bumili ng "BPA-Free High Density Thermal Paper" sa Shopee/Lazada (karaniwang ₱25–₱35 per roll ng 80x80mm). Ang isang roll ay nakakapag-print ng mahigit 150 hanggang 200 photo strips! Ibig sabihin, wala pang ₱0.40 ang puhunan mo bawat print ng bisita.

Step-by-Step Setup

Connecting Your PayMongo Account in photobooth4u

  1. 1

    Get Your PayMongo API Keys

    Log in to your PayMongo Dashboard (paymongo.com). In the left sidebar, navigate to Developers → API Keys. Copy your Public Key (starts with pk_live_...).

  2. 2

    Paste Key into photobooth4u

    In the booth, open Setup → 7 Session → Guest Payment. Toggle "Enable Pay with QR Ph (PayMongo)" to ON. Paste your Public Key into the API Key field.

  3. 3

    Set Session Price & Included Prints

    Enter your desired price (e.g., 50 for ₱50.00). Specify how many printed receipt strips are included per session (usually 1 or 2 strips). Tap Save.

  4. 4

    Enable Auto-Start & Auto-Print

    Under Session settings, turn ON "Automatic start upon payment confirmation" and in Printer settings turn ON "Print automatically after each session".

Part 04 · Kiosk Security & Fail-Safes

Tablet Lockdown & Offline Vouchers

How to lock down the Samsung tablet so guests cannot tamper with settings, and how to accept cash payments seamlessly using offline voucher tickets if venue internet fails.

Tamper-Proof Kiosk

Locking the Samsung Tablet

In an unattended or self-service receipt booth, guests and curious kids might swipe to exit or change settings. Use this two-layer lock:

  1. 1

    photobooth4u PIN Lock

    Go to Setup → 7 Session → Lock Booth. Set a secret 4-digit PIN (e.g. 2468). The Setup button disappears, leaving only the guest interface. To re-enter Setup, tap the secret lock icon in the upper right corner and enter your PIN.

  2. 2

    Android App Pinning (Kiosk Mode)

    On the Samsung tablet, open Settings → Security and privacy → More security settings → App Pinning → ON. Check "Ask for PIN before unpinning".
    Open photobooth4u → tap Recent Apps → tap the photobooth4u circular icon → tap Pin this app.
    The Home, Back, and Notification bars are completely disabled!

  3. 3

    Keep Screen Awake While Charging

    Settings → About tablet → tap "Build number" 7 times to unlock Developer options. Go to Settings → Developer options → Stay awake (Screen will never sleep while charging). Kiosk stays awake 24/7.

Emergency Fallback

Offline Cash & Voucher Tickets

If venue Wi-Fi drops or mobile cellular signal in a basement mall is dead, PayMongo QR cannot generate. photobooth4u includes a 100% Offline Voucher Engine as an emergency backup.

  1. 1

    Generate Vouchers in Advance

    In Setup → 7 Session → Guest payment, tap Generate Voucher Codes (e.g. 50 or 100 codes). Each code is an 8-digit unique token (e.g. 4821 9302).

  2. 2

    Print Voucher Tickets Sheet

    Tap Print tickets (or open /pay/tickets.html). It prints an A4 sheet with 10 perforated ticket slips with event name, price, and code ready to cut.

  3. 3

    Cash Transaction at Counter

    Guests pay cash (₱50 or ₱100) to the cafe barista or event coordinator → they hand the guest a physical voucher ticket.

  4. 4

    Guest Redeems on Tablet

    Guest taps the kiosk screen → taps "I have a voucher" → enters their 8-digit code. The booth validates locally without internet and triggers the session immediately!

Single-Use Protection

Each voucher code expires immediately after one use. It cannot be shared or reused by other guests.

Zero Internet Required

The voucher database is stored securely in the tablet's local storage. Works even in airplane mode.

Mixed Payment Allowed

You can have BOTH PayMongo QR and Voucher mode active at the same time on the welcome screen.

💡 Pro-Tip para sa Unmanned Booths
Kung ilalagay mo ang booth sa isang cafe o milk tea shop, pwede kang makipag-partner sa may-ari: ibigay ang voucher tickets sa kanilang kahera. Sila ang tatanggap ng cash sa POS nila, at maghahati kayo sa kita bawat buwan (revenue share)!

Part 05 · Operations & Support

Event Checklist & Troubleshooting

Keep this quick reference guide handy during setup and live hours to instantly diagnose and resolve common hardware, payment, or printing issues.

Issue / Symptom Probable Cause Immediate Fix
Tablet battery draining while plugged into hub Charger wattage is too low, or non-PD USB-C hub being used. Upgrade wall charger to at least 45W or 65W GaN USB-C PD. Verify hub has a dedicated PD-IN port. Do not use 10W phone bricks.
DSLR camera turns off after a few minutes Camera power saving / Auto Power Off timeout engaged. In DSLR settings, set Auto Power Off → Disable / Off. Use a dummy AC battery coupler for all-day continuous power.
Thermal printout is too faint or blurry Low print head density or low-quality thin paper roll. In photobooth4u Print Settings, increase Thermal Contrast / Darkness. Always use premium high-density BPA-free thermal rolls.
PayMongo QR Ph code does not generate Internet disconnected or incorrect Public Key entered. Check Wi-Fi / hotspot connection. Re-check API key starts with pk_live_. Fallback to Voucher Mode immediately.
Receipt printer prints blank paper Thermal paper roll installed upside-down. Thermal paper is single-sided heat-sensitive. Flip the roll over so the smooth coated side feeds toward the thermal print head.
Bluetooth printer disconnects intermittently Tablet Bluetooth power optimization or interference. Turn tablet Bluetooth off and on. In Android Battery settings, set photobooth4u to "Unrestricted" battery usage.
Guests accidentally exit the app App Pinning or Kiosk PIN Lock not activated. Activate Lock Booth PIN in Setup, and enable Android App Pinning from Recent Apps screen (see Page 5).
10-Point Pre-Event Operator Checklist
  • USB-C Hub: 45W+ charger plugged into PD port; tablet shows "Fast Charging".
  • DSLR Camera: Mode dial set to P/M; Auto Power Off disabled; lens focus locked.
  • Thermal Printer: Paper roll loaded correctly; test print executed and cut.
  • Spare Paper: At least 3 extra rolls of 80mm thermal paper stored in booth enclosure.
  • PayMongo Active: Test ₱1 live transaction performed; payout account confirmed.
  • Offline Backup: 50 printed voucher ticket slips packed in event toolbox.
  • Screen Clean: Tablet screen wiped with microfiber cloth; camera lens clean.
  • Kiosk Lockdown: App pinned in Android; photobooth4u PIN lock active.
  • Audio & Countdown: Tablet volume set to 100% for voice prompts and beeps.
  • Emergency Hotspot: Pocket Wi-Fi or phone hotspot ready as backup data source.
