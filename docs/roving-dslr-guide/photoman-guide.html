photobooth4u · Roving Photoman

Shoot anywhere. Frame, edit and print from the laptop.

Two ways to get photos in: a phone that scans a QR, or a DSLR tethered to an Android phone. One place to finish them: the Photographer Desk on the laptop base station.

Laptop app: Desktop 1.1.89 or newer Windows & Mac Works on the booth's own Wi-Fi, no internet needed for transfer
The big picture

What this version adds

New Its own template. Photoman prints use their own template (a built-in layout, one of your frames, or a design from the Template Maker). It never changes the booth's layout.

New Auto-frame. New photos are framed, given a QR and printed without a click, and it steps aside while you pick photos by hand.

New Photos in any order. For a template with several spots, tap one photo per spot, or let one photo fill every spot.

New Live Output Preview. It shows the photos that have just arrived and the print you just made, not a stock sample.

New Edit photo. Brightness, contrast, warmth and colour before printing.

New Phone as remote. The photographer's phone can pick the template, switch Auto-frame, and make or reprint a photo.

Tip: use the pop-out window at busy events

In its own window the desk keeps framing and printing while the main booth is serving guests. Inside the booth window, Auto-frame waits until the booth is idle. The label at the top shows Booth Capturing · Photoman Ready when the booth is busy.

Option A

Send photos from a phone by scanning the QR

No app to install. The phone opens a small page served by the laptop and sends its photos straight into the queue.

  1. On the laptop, open Connect & QR

    Look at Booth & Camera Links. You want a green tick on Roving DSLR / Phone QR ready. If you see Booth Wi-Fi / LAN address needed for Phone QR, connect the laptop to the booth Wi-Fi first, then press Refresh.

  2. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi

    It must be the same network as the laptop.

  3. Scan the QR with the phone's normal camera app

    A page titled Photoman Control & Queue opens in the phone's browser. (Copy link under the QR gives you the same address to send by message.)

  4. Send photos

    Tap 📸 Take a Photo to shoot with the phone, or 🖼️ Choose Photos to send several from the gallery at once. A progress bar shows each upload.

  5. Watch them arrive

    Each photo appears in the laptop's Queue within a couple of seconds. A 📱 badge marks photos that came in over Wi-Fi from a phone or the Bridge.

On the website version (no laptop app) the QR is different: the phone uploads into the event's online folder and photos show up on the desk about every 10 seconds. The phone remote in section 9 is a laptop-app feature.

Option B

DSLR connected to an Android phone

The camera is plugged into an Android phone. Every time you press the camera's own shutter, the photo is sent over Wi-Fi to the laptop's queue.

💻 Desk queue

Photographer Desk

  1. Set the camera

    Image quality JPEG (or RAW + JPEG), camera mode dial not on movie, camera Wi-Fi and Bluetooth off, a memory card inside. Sony: set USB Connection to PC Remote. The full menu table for Canon, Nikon and Sony is in the Roving DSLR Guide (PDF).

  2. Install the Roving DSLR Bridge on the Android phone

    Download it from /downloads (section Roving DSLR Bridge), tap the file to install, then open Roving DSLR Bridge. It is free and needs no booth code.

  3. Pair it with the laptop

    Phone on the booth Wi-Fi. On the laptop open Photographer desk → Connect & QR. In the phone app tap 📷 Scan QR Code on Laptop and point it at the laptop screen. It sets itself up, tests the link and shows ✅ Connected! Base station ready.

  4. Plug in the camera

    Connect the USB OTG cable from the phone to the camera and switch the camera on. If Android asks to open the app when the device is connected, choose Always allow. The status card goes from WAITING to READY and shows the camera model and battery. Tap Test Shutter once to check the whole chain.

  5. Optional: Pocket Mode

    Tap 🔒 Pocket Mode before the phone goes into your pocket. The screen dims and ignores touches, and the shutter link stays live. Tap the unlock button 3 times to come back.

  6. Shoot

    Use the camera normally. About half a second after each shot the phone buzzes to confirm the photo reached the laptop, and it shows up in the Queue. If Wi-Fi drops, shots wait in the phone and are sent when the signal returns.

Already using the camera's own software on the laptop? (EOS Utility, Imaging Edge, FTP.) In Connect & QR press Choose folder and pick the folder that software saves into. New files in that folder join the queue. You can also drop photos onto the desk or press Choose photos.

Two photographers? Both scan the same QR. Their photos merge into the one queue.

On the laptop

Template & Auto-frame

Open the 🖼️ Template & Auto tab (or scroll to Photoman Template & Flow).

Photoman template

The list has four groups. The number is how many photos the template needs.

  • Same as the booth: copies whatever layout the booth uses right now.
  • 🎨 My Photoman templates: your own designs from the Template Maker.
  • Built-in layouts: Solo, strips, grids and so on.
  • My frames: frames you uploaded in Setup.

Choosing a template here never changes the booth's own layout, so the booth and Photoman can share one laptop.

✨ New Template (Template Maker)

Press New Template and start from a preset: 4R Hero + Title, 4R Wide + Text, 4R Polaroid, 4R Hero + 2, 4R 2×2 Grid, 2×6 3-Shot Strip, Square 1:1, or Import PNG Frame.

  • Set the background colour or picture, and an optional PNG overlay.
  • + Photo Spot, + Text, + Logo. Drag to move, drag a corner to resize.
  • Each spot has an Assigned Photo #, so you decide which shot goes where.
  • ✓ Save & Use Template when done. Reopen it later with ✎ Edit.

When the template has several photo spots

SettingWhat happens
Separate photos (tap one photo per spot)You tap photos in order and they fill spot 1, 2, 3 and so on. Good for a hero + 3 layout or a 4-photo strip of different shots.
One photo fills every spotOne photo is repeated in all the boxes. Good for 2×6 strips cut in two, or a double-print.

This setting only appears when the template has more than one spot.

⚡ Auto-frame new photos

Switch it on and the desk does the work: each new photo (or each group of photos, for a multi-spot template) is placed in the template, gets its Guest QR, and is printed if Print after each session is on.

The rules

  • Only photos that arrive after you switch it on. Photos already in the list are left alone.
  • With a template of 4 spots and Separate photos, it waits for 4 new photos and uses them in the order they arrived.
  • It pauses while the Edit photo window is open, while a print is being made, and (inside the booth window) while the booth is busy.

Want to choose which photo goes where?

Just start tapping photos in the queue. Auto-frame waits for you and does not take your photos in arrival order. It carries on 30 seconds after your last tap. To stop it completely, untick Auto-frame new photos.

📺 Also show result on Booth Screen: when the booth is idle, finished Photoman prints also appear on the main booth screen for guests to see. It is skipped while a guest is taking photos.

Choose, check, touch up

Pick photos, read the preview, edit

Picking photos

  • Tap a photo to pick it. A number appears: 1 for the first spot, 2 for the second, and so on. Tap it again to un-pick it.
  • The tip line above the queue tells you where you are, for example "You picked 2 of 4 photos. Tap more for the other spots…".
  • If you pick fewer photos than the template has spots, the last photo repeats in the empty spots (the preview marks them (repeats)). You can print like that, or tap more photos.
  • Photos already printed are dimmed and marked Printed. You can still pick them again.

Live Output Preview

The preview on the right is exactly what will be printed. The two tabs switch between 🖼️ Framed + QR (Make print) and 📄 Original (Print original). Under it, small chips show which photo sits in which spot (Spot 1 ← #1). The caption on the picture tells you what you are looking at:

CaptionMeaning
Sample preview — tap photo(s) in queue to fillNothing is picked yet, so you see a stand-in picture.
⚡ Auto-frame: 2 of 4 photos arrived — waiting for the restAuto-frame has received some photos. They are shown in the first spots, the rest are still sample.
✓ Just made: … · Session #12This is the print that was just made. Tap a photo to preview another one.
⚡ Auto-frame preview — waiting for new camera photosAuto-frame is on and nothing new has arrived yet.

✎ Edit photo Optional

Pick a photo, then press ✎ Edit photo. If you picked several, it edits the last one you tapped.

Quick looks

Original, Brighten, Warm, Cool, Vivid, Matte, Black & white.

Sliders

Brightness, Contrast, Warmth (cool ↔ warm), Colour (saturation).

Buttons

Hold to compare shows the untouched photo while you hold it. Reset clears your changes. Apply to print + QR keeps them. Cancel closes without changing anything.

Once you apply an edit, both the print and the photo the guest gets by QR use the edited version. The original file in the Roving inbox stays untouched. Edited photos show an ✎ Edited badge in the queue.

Finish

Print and share with the guest

🖼️ Make print + QR

Puts the picked photos in the template, saves the photo for sharing, creates the Guest Photo QR, and prints it if Print after each session is on. Otherwise press 🖨️ Print on the result card.

📄 Print original

Prints the picked photo as it is, with no frame, filling the paper. The guest's QR still opens the framed version.

The "Ready to Print & Share" card

After a print is made, a green card appears under the preview with the session number (Session #12).

On the cardWhat it does
Guest Photo QRThe guest scans it with their phone to see and save their photo. It says Preparing QR… for a moment, then Scan to save.
Booth Wi-Fi QRShown when the laptop is running the booth Wi-Fi. The guest scans this first to join the Wi-Fi, then scans the photo QR. It shows the Wi-Fi name underneath.
🖨️ PrintPrints another copy of this result.
📺 Show on BoothSends this result to the main booth screen.
DoneCloses the card when you are finished with this guest.

If Windows shows a small "Waiting for printer connection…" window while printing, the printer has not answered yet. Check that it is on and connected, press Cancel, then use 🖨️ Print on the result card to try again.

Run the desk from a phone Remote

The page a phone opens from the QR is also a remote control. Open it on any phone on the booth Wi-Fi to run the desk while you stand with your guests.

🖼️ Photoman Template & Flow

Pick the template, choose Require separate shots or Use 1 shot only, and switch ⚡ Auto-frame on or off. Same choices as the desk, and the desk follows along.

📥 Laptop Photographer Queue

Thumbnails of the latest photos. Tap them in order, then 🖼️ Make Print + QR or 🖨️ Print Original.

🎉 Latest Framed Print & Guest QR

Shows the finished print and its QR so you can hold the phone up to the guest. 🖨️ Print Copy reprints, ⏭️ Next Guest clears it.

📱 Send Photos from This Phone

📸 Take a Photo and 🖼️ Choose Photos, exactly as in option A.

The laptop app must be running and the phone must stay on the same Wi-Fi. If the booth is busy with a guest session, a Make Print sent from the phone waits and runs when the booth is free (for up to a minute). If the page says it cannot reach the laptop, scan the QR again.

Ready-made set-ups

Four ways to run it

1 · Hands-free prints

Template: Solo or a one-photo 4R frame. Auto-frame on, Print after each session on.

Every photo you shoot is framed, printed and given a QR by itself. Check the QR on the result card or the phone remote.

2 · Pick the best shot

Auto-frame off. Shoot freely.

Guests come to the desk, you tap their favourite, optionally ✎ Edit photo, then Make print + QR.

3 · Strip or hero layout

Pick a template with several spots and Separate photos.

Auto-frame off: tap the photos in the order you want. Auto-frame on: every group of N new photos becomes one print, in arrival order.

4 · Booth and Photoman together

Use ↗ Pop Out Window for the desk.

The booth takes guest sessions while Photoman prints in its own window. Photoman's template never changes the booth's layout.

When something does not work

Troubleshooting

What you seeTry this
The phone cannot open the QR pageThe phone and laptop must be on the same Wi-Fi. On Windows, set the Wi-Fi network profile to Private. In Connect & QR the row Booth Wi-Fi / LAN address needed means the laptop has no local address yet: join the booth Wi-Fi and press Refresh. Then scan the QR again.
Photos do not reach the queueLook at the phone: did the upload finish? On the DSLR bridge, check the status says READY and press Test Shutter. If the desk is popped out into its own window, that window receives the photos (the booth's desk then says Pop-out Window Active), so look there.
Bridge stays on WAITING (no DSLR)Unplug the cable and check the OTG end is in the phone. Switch the camera off, wait 3 seconds, on again. Close the camera menus. Canon: dial on P / Av / Tv / M. Sony: USB Connection → PC Remote.
Camera took the shot but nothing arrivedThe camera may be saving RAW only. Set image quality to JPEG (or RAW + JPEG).
I tap photos but Auto-frame uses othersUpdate to Desktop 1.1.89 or newer. From that version Auto-frame waits for you while you pick by hand. To stop it fully, untick Auto-frame new photos.
Auto-frame made no printCheck the paper and printer in Setup → Printer, and that Print after each session is on. Without it, the print is made and shown but you press 🖨️ Print yourself.
The preview shows a sample pictureNothing is picked and nothing is waiting. Tap a photo, or wait for Auto-frame's photos. After a print, the preview shows Just made until you press Done or pick another photo.
The Guest QR does not openIf the booth runs its own Wi-Fi, the guest must first join it with the Booth Wi-Fi QR shown on the result card.
