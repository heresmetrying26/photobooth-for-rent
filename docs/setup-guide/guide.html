01 · Before you start

What you need

Classic booth
  • iPad or Android tablet (a phone works too)
  • Tripod or stand at the guests' eye level
  • Internet: venue Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot
  • Optional: photo printer, if your package includes printing
Pro booth
  • Laptop with Chrome or Edge, and a camera: built-in, USB webcam, or a Sony / Canon camera (page 5)
  • Second monitor or TV + HDMI cable, facing the guests
  • Optional: presenter clicker, so guests can start a shot
  • Optional: photo printer (4R / 4×6 recommended)
Day-before checklist
  • Device charged, charger packed
  • Event name decided (you type it in Setup)
  • Booth opens and the camera works
  • Printer + paper loaded, one test print done
  • Internet tested at (or near) the venue
  • Pro Booth: HDMI cable and guest screen tested

02 · First time on each device

Unlock your devices

  1. 1

    Open the booth

    Go to photobooth4u.netlify.app in Chrome, Edge or Safari.

  2. 2

    Enter your code

    Type your PB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX code and tap Unlock. Lifetime codes open your My Events page first, so tap Open photo booth from there.

  3. 3

    That device is now yours

    It stays unlocked, so next time just open the website. Each new device uses one of your device slots. To replace a device (new laptop, lost tablet), message us and we'll free its slot.

Keep your code private. Anyone with it can use your device slots and see your event links.

03 · Every event

Set up each event

In the booth, open Setup and go through the 5 steps with Next. The bar at the bottom shows what's still needed and has Start photo session.

  1. 1

    Design

    Under Ready designs, tap a template to see it big, then Use this design. Colours & background changes the background, gold details colour or adds your own photo. ★ Save my design keeps yours for next time.

  2. 2

    Text

    Type the name on the photo and tap the calendar icon to add the date. Pick a title style, then a font: the cards show your own name. Pairs set the name and small-line fonts together.

  3. 3

    Layout

    Pick one of 12 layouts (each card shows your design) and the countdown. Optional: guests choose the layout each session.

  4. 4

    Extras

    All optional, in tabs. Frame: upload your Canva frame with the photo spots left white or see-through. It becomes My frame, and two or more make a Frame collage on one 4R print. Logo, Stickers, and Filter (B&W, Sepia, Warm, Cool, soft skin).

  5. 5

    Share

    Lifetime codes: type the event name for a new folder. Rental codes connect by themselves. The All photos QR is here to print or share ahead. On a laptop, choose a backup folder here too (page 5).

Saves by itself

Your setup saves automatically. It comes back after a refresh or restart, and every device on your code gets the same design. Lifetime codes keep a separate design for each event name. ⋯ → Reset setup starts fresh.

Using more than one device?

Type the exact same event name on every device at that event. They share one folder, one QR code and one design. Each device's photos end in its number (…_cam1.jpg, …_cam2.jpg).

04 · Pro booth

Laptop + guest screen

Guests see the camera, countdown and their photos on the big screen. You run setup, printing and the QR code on the laptop.

  1. 1

    Connect the second screen

    Plug in the monitor or TV. Set the display to Extend, not Duplicate. Windows: press Win + P → Extend. Mac: System Settings → Displays → turn off mirroring.

  2. 2

    Start Chrome with silent printing

    Use the shortcut below so prints go straight to the printer without a pop-up. Close every Chrome window first.

  3. 3

    Open the guest screen

    Finish Setup, go to Photo Session and tap Open guest screen. Allow pop-ups and "window management" if Chrome asks. It moves to the second screen. Click it once to go full screen.

  4. 4

    Let guests start

    Guests tap the guest screen, or press Space / Enter or a presenter clicker.

Silent-printing shortcut

Windows: right-click the desktop → New → Shortcut, and paste this as the location:

"C:\Program Files\Google\Chrome\Application\chrome.exe" --kiosk-printing https://photobooth4u.netlify.app/

Mac: open Terminal and run:

open -a "Google Chrome" --args --kiosk-printing https://photobooth4u.netlify.app/
Don't add --kiosk for the Pro Booth. It forces a single full-screen window and blocks the guest screen. Edge works the same way with msedge.exe.

05 · Pro booth camera

Use a Sony or Canon camera

A real camera gives sharper photos and a softer background than a webcam. The booth works with any camera your laptop sees as a webcam.

  1. 1

    Turn on webcam mode

    Sony: newer models (ZV-E10, ZV-1, A7 IV, A6700…) have USB Streaming in the menu; older ones need Sony's free Imaging Edge Webcam. Canon: install the free EOS Webcam Utility and check your model is listed. Any brand: clean HDMI out + an HDMI-to-USB capture card.

  2. 2

    Power it for the whole event

    Use a dummy battery or USB power, and turn off the camera's auto power off. Set focus to continuous face / eye AF.

  3. 3

    Pick it in the booth

    In Photo Session → Camera, choose the camera. The booth remembers it on this laptop and picks it again next time.

  4. 4

    Mirror or not

    Mirror the live view (Camera section) shows guests a mirror image. Saved and printed photos are never mirrored.

Good to know
  • "Camera disconnected. Reconnecting…" means the cable or power slipped. Fix it and the booth carries on by itself.
  • The booth uses the camera's 1080p video; it doesn't press the shutter, so a flash won't fire. Use a continuous light.
  • Tablets (Classic Booth) use their own front camera.

06 · Pro booth backup

Backup folder & weak internet

  1. 1

    Choose a backup folder

    In Setup → 5 Share, tap Choose backup folder and pick one, e.g. Pictures › photobooth4u. Every photo is saved there in a subfolder named after the event. Chrome or Edge on a computer.

  2. 2

    Allow saving after a restart

    After Chrome restarts, tap Allow saving (in Setup or the Photo Session panel), then allow Chrome to edit files.

  3. 3

    Check it's on

    The Photo Session panel shows Backup folder: Saving to … ✓. Without one, each photo goes to Downloads.

Internet drops?

The booth tries again, then keeps the photo on the device. The panel shows Waiting to upload, and the photos upload by themselves when the internet is back. Guests get the All photos QR meanwhile. Keep the booth open until the list is empty.

07 · If your package includes printing

Printing

  1. 1

    Prepare the printer

    Make the photo printer your computer's default printer. In its preferences, choose the paper size you'll use (e.g. 4×6) and borderless if offered.

  2. 2

    Do one test print

    After a session, open the Print box, pick the paper size and tap Print. If the size or margins are off, fix them in the printer's own settings.

  3. 3

    Turn on auto-print

    Pro Booth only: after your first print, tick Auto-print every session. Every session after that prints by itself on the same paper.

Good to know
  • Strips print two side by side on 4R paper. Cut down the middle and you get two strips.
  • No silent-printing shortcut? A print dialog opens. Choose the photo printer, set Margins: None and Scale: 100%.
  • Only the paper sizes included in your package appear in the Print box.

08 · Showtime

During the event

Guests

Tap the welcome screen, pick a layout if offered, follow the countdown and pose. The photo appears with a Your photo QR code that opens it in the online gallery.

Photos

Each session uploads to the event's online gallery, and a copy saves on the device: Downloads, or your backup folder. Allow multiple downloads if asked.

You

Print the All photos QR from Setup → Share ahead and put it near the booth. Guests can scan it any time to see every photo.

Pro tips
  • Put the camera at eye level, with light facing the guests (not behind them).
  • Keep the device plugged in. The booth keeps the screen awake while it's open; on tablets, also turn off auto-lock.
  • Weak venue internet? Use a phone hotspot. Photos that can't upload wait on the device and upload by themselves later.

09 · After the event

Send the photos to your client

  1. 1

    Open My Events

    Go to photobooth4u.netlify.app/lifetime/ on any phone or computer and sign in with your code. Every event is listed, newest first.

  2. 2

    Copy or show the link

    Use Copy link to send the online gallery by Messenger, or Show QR to let your client scan it. Drive folder opens the same photos in Google Drive for downloading them all.

  3. 3

    Remind them to download

    Photos are deleted 7 days after the event's last photo. Each event shows its "Download by" date.

7-day rule

Send the link the same day, and tell your client to save their favourites within the week. After deletion the link stops working.

10 · Quick fixes

Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
"We need access to your camera"Allow camera access in the browser (the icon in the address bar), then tap Try Again. Close other apps using the camera.
"Camera disconnected. Reconnecting…"Check the camera is on and its USB cable is in. The booth carries on by itself.
Sony / Canon not in the Camera menuTurn on USB Streaming or the webcam app, re-plug the cable, then reload the booth.
"Photos waiting to upload"The internet dropped. They upload by themselves, or tap Retry now / Save to this computer.
"Already used on another device"All your device slots are taken. Message us to free one.
The QR code doesn't appearCheck the internet, and make sure the event name is typed in Setup → 5 Share.
A print dialog pops up every timeChrome wasn't started with the silent-printing shortcut. Close all Chrome windows and use the shortcut (page 4).
Guest screen is on the wrong monitorCheck the display is set to Extend, then tap Move / reopen guest screen.
Two events' photos in one folderThe same event name was used twice. Use a new, unique name for each event.
