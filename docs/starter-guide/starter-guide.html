Part 1 · Laptop Guide

Running on a Laptop: Online & Offline

The laptop is the powerhouse of professional event booths: supporting DSLR tethering, dual-screen HDMI mirror displays, and direct USB connection to commercial high-speed printers.

Laptop Desktop App (Offline)

100% No Internet

Available for Windows 10/11 (.exe) and macOS 11+ (.dmg). Built specifically for remote venues, outdoor weddings, basements, and venues with zero Wi-Fi.

  1. 1

    Local Booth Wi-Fi Hotspot

    The laptop turns on a mobile hotspot (or connects to an offline portable router). Guests join that Wi-Fi network.

  2. 2

    Instant Local QR Downloads

    At session end, the guest scans the on-screen QR. Their phone downloads the high-res photo straight from the laptop — zero internet or data required!

  3. 3

    Built-in Background Printing

    Talks directly to Windows and Mac print spoolers. Prints silently in the background with no pop-ups, no helper tools, and no internet.

  4. 4

    DSLR Tethering & Dual Screen

    Connect Canon, Nikon, or Sony via USB. Press Win + P → Extend to display a live countdown mirror on a TV facing the guests.

Chrome Web Booth (Online)

Cloud Galleries

Open photobooth4u.netlify.app in Google Chrome on any computer. Perfect for venues with reliable Wi-Fi where instant cloud galleries are desired.

  1. 1

    Live Online Cloud Gallery

    Photos upload instantly to your secured cloud event album. Guests scan the QR to open their private online link.

  2. 2

    The Print Helper Utility

    Open photobooth4u-print-helper.exe in the background. It intercepts print commands, eliminating Chrome's print dialog for true 1-click silent auto-printing.

  3. 3

    Smart Offline Protection

    If venue Wi-Fi stumbles during the event, the web app automatically queues photos in browser memory and syncs them to the cloud the moment internet returns.

  4. 4

    Remote Monitor & Share Station

    Any secondary device can open your event's live gallery URL to act as an iPad sharing station or slideshow kiosk.

Part 2 · Tablet & Mobile Guide

Using with Tablets: Android APK & iPad

Tablets provide an ultra-sleek, portable kiosk enclosure. Run fully autonomous guest sessions with front-facing cameras or connect external cameras and printers.

Android Tablet App (APK)

Native USB & BT

Download the APK directly from photobooth4u.netlify.app/downloads/. Works on Android tablets running Android 7.0 or newer.

  1. 1

    Activate Once with Lifetime Code

    Connect to Wi-Fi once to activate your code. The app is then permanently authorized and runs 100% offline.

  2. 2

    Direct Wi-Fi Mopria & AirPrint

    Go to Print settings → Wi-Fi printer → Find Wi-Fi printers. The tablet discovers network printers and prints without popup dialogs.

  3. 3

    Native USB OTG Ink-Tank Printing

    Plug an OTG cable directly from the tablet into Epson L8050, L3210, etc. Built-in ESC/P2 drivers print borderless photos with no extra apps!

  4. 4

    Direct Bluetooth Thermal Printing

    Pair thermal printers (Vozy, MPT, XP-80) via Bluetooth. The app prints retro receipt strips instantly with zero print dialogs.

iPad Kiosk (iOS Web App)

AirPrint Ready

Runs cleanly on iPad Air, Pro, or standard 10.2" iPad using Safari's native standalone Progressive Web App (PWA) container.

  1. 1

    Create Fullscreen Web App

    Open Safari, visit photobooth4u.netlify.app, tap the Share icon → Add to Home Screen. Launches with no browser bars!

  2. 2

    Native Apple AirPrint

    In Print settings, choose AirPrint. Wirelessly prints directly to Canon SELPHY CP1300/1500, Epson, Brother, and HP on the same Wi-Fi.

  3. 3

    Enable Guided Access Mode

    In iOS Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access. Triple-click the top power button to lock guests out of exiting the photo booth!

  4. 4

    High-End iPad Camera Sensor

    Take advantage of Apple's Center Stage front cameras or rear 12MP Ultra-Wide lens for crisp, sharp photo strip captures.

The Bridge: Printing from Tablets to Commercial Dye-Sub Printers

Laptop Print Station

The Challenge: Commercial dye-sub printers (DNP, HiTi, Citizen, Mitsubishi, Fuji) require specialized Windows or Mac USB drivers and cannot plug directly into an iPad or standard Android tablet.
The Solution — Laptop Print Station:

1. Connect Printer to Laptop
Plug your DNP or HiTi into a laptop with USB. Open photobooth4u.netlify.app/print-station/ in Google Chrome.
2. Connect Both to Same Wi-Fi
Ensure your iPad/Android tablet and laptop are on the same Wi-Fi network (or portable router).
3. Auto-Print Wirelessly!
On the tablet, select Send prints to → Laptop Print Station. Every photo taken on the tablet prints instantly on the laptop's dye-sub!

Part 3 · Photo Printing

The Complete Printing Process

How photo printing works: choosing between high-speed commercial dye-subs and ultra-low-cost continuous ink tanks, configuring 2×6 strip razor cuts, and setting up hands-free auto-print.

Commercial Dye-Sub Printers

12s · Auto-Cut

Models: DNP (DS620, RX1HS), HiTi (P525L), Citizen (CX-02), Mitsubishi, Fuji ASK-300, Kodak 305.

  • High-Speed Event Duty: 8 to 14 seconds per print. Prints drop out 100% dry and smudge-proof with a protective clear coat.
  • Roll Capacity: 400 to 700 prints per roll — run entire weddings without reloading paper or ink!
  • Internal Strip Slicing: Built-in razor blades automatically slice two 2×6 strips down the middle with zero manual trimming.

Pro-Tip: Add Printer Twice in Windows

Install the driver twice: name one "DNP - 4x6 Postcard" (standard cut) and the other "DNP - 2x6 Strips" (2-inch cut enabled). Assign each in booth settings!

Continuous Ink-Tank Printers

<₱0.50 per print

Models: Epson EcoTank Photo (6-color L8050, L18050), EcoTank (L3210, L3250), Canon MegaTank G620.

  • Unbeatable Profit Margins: Less than ₱0.50 ($0.01) ink cost per photo. Thousands of prints from single refill bottles.
  • 6-Color Photo Quality: Models like the L8050 deliver laboratory-grade color reproduction and skin tones.
  • Android USB OTG Direct: Plug USB straight into your Android tablet for automated driverless photo printing!

Glossy Photo Paper Settings

Always load premium inkjet glossy photo paper (180–260 gsm) and set driver paper type to Epson Premium Glossy for rich, non-smudging prints.

How 2×6 Photo Strip Auto-Cutting Works

1. Layout Generation
The booth automatically places two duplicate 2×6 strips side-by-side onto a single 4×6 (4R) digital canvas.
2. 2-Inch Cut Signal
In Windows/Mac Printing Preferences, enable 2-inch cut. The driver signals the printer's internal cutter.
3. Sliced Down the Middle
During the final lamination pass, the razor cuts precisely at the 2-inch mark, dropping two finished strips!

Configuring 100% Hands-Free Auto-Print

  1. 1

    Open Print Settings

    In the booth, tap Photo Session → Print settings (or Setup → 4 Camera & printer).

  2. 2

    Assign Printer per Paper Size

    Tap your event's paper size (e.g. 4×6 in (4R) or 2×6 in strip) and pick your printer from the list. The app saves this choice.

  3. 3

    Run a Test Print & Verify

    Tap Test print. Confirm that the print size is correct, colors are vibrant, and edges are borderless with no white trim.

  4. 4

    Enable Auto-Print Every Session

    Check Auto-print every session. As soon as guests finish their photos, the physical print starts automatically — no operator clicks needed!

Part 4 · Thermal Receipt Guide

Vintage Receipt Photobooth (Thermal)

The surging retro photo booth trend: instant 1–2 second black-and-white dithered strips printed on continuous thermal paper rolls. Zero ink cartridges, zero ribbons, and pennies per roll.

80 mm Desktop POS (Auto-Cut)

Automatic Cutter

Featured Models: Vozy G80, Epson TM-T20III / TM-T88, Star Micronics TSP143, Munbyn ITPP047, Xprinter XP-N160II.

  • Paper Width: 80 mm roll (~3.15 inches wide). Wide canvas for large, clear 3-photo vertical layouts.
  • Automated Razor Cut: The printer's motorized guillotine cutter slices the receipt right below the QR code!
  • Connectivity: USB to laptop, or Bluetooth / Network to Android tablet app.

58 mm Mobile Bluetooth (Battery)

Battery Powered

Featured Models: Vozy MP210 (battery), Vozy 50, MPT-II, PT-210, Goojprt, PeriPage, Phomemo.

  • Pocket Size: Handheld mini printer with rechargeable lithium battery — perfect for mobile roaming booths (Rove).
  • Paper Width: 58 mm roll (~2.28 inches wide) with serrated manual tear bar.
  • Bluetooth SPP: Pairs wirelessly with Android tablet or phone with zero extra equipment.

Step-by-Step Android Bluetooth Setup (Zero Dialogs)

  1. 1

    Pair Printer in Android Bluetooth Settings

    Turn on the thermal printer. On the tablet, open Settings → Connected devices → Bluetooth → Pair new device. Tap your printer (named like Vozy, G80, MP210, POS-80, or Printer001). Enter PIN 0000 or 1234.

  2. 2

    Select Receipt Layout in the App

    In photobooth4u, go to Setup → Layout and choose Receipt. The booth converts live camera frames into retro halftone black-and-white dithered graphics engineered for thermal paper.

  3. 3

    Configure Thermal Printing

    In Print settings, choose Receipt 80 mm or Receipt 58 mm. Enable Bluetooth thermal receipt printer, tap Connect, and select your paired Vozy/POS printer from the list.

  4. 4

    Test Print & Auto-Print

    Tap Test print — the printer feeds and prints a test header in 1 second! Tick Auto-print every session. Each guest receives a retro receipt strip instantly.

Customizing the Receipt Branding

Header & Monogram
Add the couple's names, event title, custom date, or venue name in bold retro monospace.
Photo Halftones
Booth renders 3 or 4 photos using Floyd-Steinberg dithering for sharp, distinct facial features.
Digital QR Code
A sharp QR code is printed at the footer so guests can scan to download the original full-color digital file!

Part 5 · Event Day Checklist

Event-Day Checklist & Troubleshooting

Follow this pre-flight checklist before opening your booth to guests, and use these field solutions to quickly handle unexpected issues.

✓ 10-Minute Pre-Flight Checklist

  • Hardware Power: Laptop charger plugged into reliable outlet. Tablet connected to mains power (or high-capacity power bank).
  • Camera Power: DSLR plugged into AC dummy battery adapter — disable camera's "Auto Power Off" in menu.
  • Camera Focus: Autofocus (AF) on guests initially, then switch lens switch to Manual Focus (MF) so focus never hunts.
  • Paper & Ribbon: Ensure media is freshly loaded with at least 1 spare box in your event kit. Empty scrap cut bin!
  • Lighting Check: Position ring light or studio flash strobe slightly above eye level. Take test photo to verify exposure.
  • Test Print Run: Run 1 full guest session. Test the QR code scan on your phone and verify physical print cutting.

☆ Top 6 Quick Field Fixes

1. Guest phone says "Wi-Fi Has No Internet"
Normal on offline booths! Tell guests to tap "Keep connection" or "Connect anyway". The phone downloads photos directly from the booth over local Wi-Fi.
2. Chrome Print Preview Dialog Appears
Open photobooth4u-print-helper.exe in the background. It intercepts print commands so prints occur silently with zero dialogs.
3. 2×6 Strips Not Slicing in Two
Open Windows/Mac Printing Preferences for your dye-sub printer. Ensure the 2-inch cut setting is enabled and saved as default.
4. Receipt Print Appears Too Faint
Increase contrast in booth layout settings. On thermal printers, slow down the speed setting in preferences for darker, richer heat transfer.
5. Bluetooth Printer Disconnects
Keep the tablet within 5 meters of the printer. In tablet settings, disable battery saver mode for Bluetooth services. Tap Reconnect in Print settings.
