What you get

Everything in one booth

Set it up in minutes on the device you already have. Your design is saved, so it's ready again on the day.

Ready-made designs

20 themes and 10 templates for weddings, birthdays, debuts, kids' parties, christenings, graduations, Christmas and more.

Your own Canva frames

Upload a frame with white or see-through spots and the photos drop right in. Put two or more on one print as a frame collage.

Logo, stickers & filters

Add your logo, drag on props and stickers (or your own PNGs), pick fonts and colours, and watch it all in the live preview.

Online photo gallery

After every session, guests scan a QR for their own photo. The event gallery fills up live, ready to view, download and share.

Prints on the spot

Bring a photo printer: 2×6 strips, 4R, A4 and more, plus 2.5×7.5 keychain prints. Strips print two side by side.

Tablet or laptop booth

Run it on an iPad or Android tablet, or a laptop with a second screen for guests and automatic printing. No internet at the venue? Use the Windows / Mac app.

How it works

From booking to photos

No booth to rent, no one to send. Just your code and your device.

  1. BookMessage us with your event date. You get your booth code plus a 1-hour practice code.
  2. Set upOpen the booth, enter your code, and pick a design, layout and countdown.
  3. SnapGuests tap, pose and get their photo — printed on the spot if you have a printer.
  4. ShareEveryone scans for the gallery. Download your favourites within 7 days.
