2. What’s included

The code never expires and can be used for unlimited events, with no monthly or per-event fees. Items marked (Online) apply to Lifetime Online and Lifetime Online + Offline. Items marked (Offline) apply to Lifetime Offline and Lifetime Online + Offline.

  • Unlimited events and sessions.
  • Use on up to 5 devices at the same time on one code, for every plan (tablet, iPad, Android tablet, Windows or Mac laptop).
  • (Online) The online booth in the browser: each event gets its own online gallery and QR code for guests, and a My Events page with every event’s gallery and QR, account details, devices and printing settings.
  • (Offline) The Offline App for Windows and Mac laptops and Android tablets: the booth works without internet at the venue, photos save on the device, and guests get their QR over the booth Wi-Fi.
  • Booth setup: layouts, custom frame/overlay, logo, countdown and session settings, saved per event.
  • Printing (when switched on for the code): paper sizes including Canon Selphy and receipt printers, plus custom sizes and print limits. In the Android Offline App, printing is to Bluetooth thermal receipt printers only; photo printers (such as Canon Selphy, DNP or HiTi) need the online booth or the Windows / Mac Offline App.
  • The setup guide (PDF).
  • Supported devices: the devices, operating systems and browsers listed under “Supported devices” on the about-us page (photobooth4u.netlify.app/about-us/#devices) on the day the Renter pays. Support for versions that are more than 5 years old may end with 90 days’ notice.
  • Updates to the features above, as described in section 6.

Not included: optional add-ons and any future paid features (section 5), and hardware (tablet, laptop, camera, printer, paper, ink, internet).

3. Your code, account and devices

  • The code belongs to the Renter’s business and is linked to one email address.
  • Account details: for the online booth, the Renter can change their name and email anytime on the My Events page. For the Offline App, the Renter can ask photobooth4u by message or email. The email stays with the same business; giving the code to a different owner is a transfer under section 7.
  • Forgot the code: use “Forgot your code?” on My Events (online plans), or message photobooth4u from the registered email and it will be sent again.
  • Devices: up to 5 at the same time on every plan. The Renter can remove a device on My Events (online) or message photobooth4u (Offline App) to free its slot, for example a lost or replaced tablet or laptop. A removed online device locks within about 5 minutes once it is online. The Renter can free up to 3 device slots per calendar month; for a genuinely lost, stolen or broken device, photobooth4u can free more on request.
  • Keep it private: the code and activation key work like a password. Don’t post them publicly or share them outside the Renter’s business.
  • Lost or leaked code: photobooth4u can replace it with a new code on request, keeping the Renter’s events and settings, and deactivate the old one.

4. Photos, storage and privacy

  • (Online) Event photos taken in the online booth are stored for 7 days after the event’s last photo, then deleted automatically. Guests and the Renter download photos from the event’s online gallery (“Download all” is available). The Renter should save their own copies within the 7 days.
  • (Online) Photos are stored in photobooth4u’s secure cloud storage. The Renter and guests only see their own event galleries, never the storage itself.
  • (Offline) Photos taken with the Offline App stay on the Renter’s device and are never sent to photobooth4u. The Renter keeps their own backups; if a device is lost, damaged or reset, photobooth4u cannot recover the photos. No online gallery or cloud storage is included in Lifetime Offline.
  • photobooth4u does not use, post or share the Renter’s event photos. Online photos are accessed only when the Renter asks for help with a specific event.
  • (Online) On request, photobooth4u will delete a specific event’s online photos before the 7 days are up, for example when a guest or client asks for their photos to be removed.
  • Guests’ photos are personal information under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). The Renter is responsible for telling guests that photos are taken and shared, and for following their client’s or venue’s privacy requests.

5. Add-ons, future paid features and adding the other side

What your Lifetime price covers. Your one-time price covers the features listed in section 2 for your plan, and the updates in section 6. Nothing in that list will be removed or put behind a new fee.

What it does not cover. Add-ons and any new paid feature that photobooth4u launches in the future are not included in the Lifetime price, even though updates to the included features are free. Examples today are retakes and guest payments, and examples tomorrow could be new modules, integrations or services. These are optional and the Renter is never forced to buy them.

Additional pay, if ever. If the Renter wants an add-on or a new paid feature, an additional fee may apply. The price and what it covers are told to the Renter and agreed in writing (message or email is enough) before it is switched on. Nothing is switched on or charged without the Renter’s written yes.

  • Add-ons and paid features are switched on per code by photobooth4u.
  • Hardware, internet, and third-party fees (for example PayMongo, paper and ink) are never included.

Guest payments (optional add-on, Online). Guests can pay per session with vouchers the Renter sells, or by QR Ph through the Renter’s own PayMongo account.

  • Money from guest payments goes straight to the Renter’s PayMongo account. photobooth4u never receives or holds it and takes no commission.
  • The Renter’s PayMongo key is stored encrypted and protected by the Renter’s own payments PIN. photobooth4u cannot view it.
  • PayMongo’s own fees and terms apply between the Renter and PayMongo.

Adding the other side later. These are the current prices on the about-us page and are one-time, with no time limit:

  • Lifetime Online owners can add the Offline App (Windows, Mac and Android) for ₱2,499.
  • Lifetime Offline owners can add the online booth (online gallery and QR, My Events, cloud storage) for ₱1,999.
  • After adding, the plan works exactly like Lifetime Online + Offline. The price is confirmed in writing before it is switched on, and photobooth4u will tell the Renter first if these prices change; the price confirmed in writing is the price the Renter pays.

How the Offline App works. A device needs internet once to activate (or an offline activation key from photobooth4u), and should go online at least once every 75 days so its licence renews; after a further 30 days without internet it pauses until it goes online again. QR payments, the online gallery, My Events and cloud storage need the internet, so they belong to the online booth, not the Offline App.

6. Lifetime meaning, updates, support and service continuity

“Lifetime” means the code never expires and the Renter is never charged again to keep using the features included in their plan, for as long as photobooth4u operates them. If it ever stops, the Renter gets at least 90 days’ notice and the protections below.

  • Included updates: bug fixes, security fixes, reasonable-effort compatibility updates for the current and previous major versions of the supported browsers and operating systems, and improvements to the features in section 2 are free. Support for new printer or camera models, or other new hardware, is not guaranteed and may be offered as an add-on. The online booth updates automatically; the Offline App updates when the device is online.
  • Not covered by updates: new add-ons or paid features, as described in section 5. Features may be improved or changed, but the core booth (sessions, layouts, gallery and QR where included, printing) stays available to Lifetime codes.
  • Support: by Messenger or email, normally answered within 24 hours. For event days, message ahead so help is ready. Support covers the photobooth4u software and the Renter’s account; it does not cover the Renter’s hardware, printers, internet or third-party services, and it is for reasonable use.
  • Planned maintenance: announced at least 48 hours ahead and scheduled outside the Renter’s booked events where possible. An activated Offline App keeps working during maintenance.
  • Downtime: photobooth4u keeps the service running and fixes problems as fast as possible, but can’t promise zero interruptions (for example internet, Google or hosting outages). If the internet drops during an event, the open online booth keeps taking photos and uploads them when it reconnects, and an activated Offline App is not affected.
  • Events outside anyone’s control: photobooth4u is not liable for interruptions caused by natural disasters, power or internet outages, government orders, or failures of hosting, Google or other providers it relies on.
  • If photobooth4u ends the online booth or the Offline App: at least 90 days’ written notice by email and message. During the notice period the Renter keeps full access and can download all photos and back up their setup. If a hosting, payment or platform provider forces the shutdown sooner, photobooth4u gives as much notice as it can and still provides the final Offline App below.
  • Successor: photobooth4u may hand the service to another operator, who must honour this agreement. The Renter is told in writing before the handover.
  • Protection if it ends: if photobooth4u ends the service entirely, every Lifetime Renter, whatever plan they bought, gets a final version of the Offline App with a licence that keeps working without photobooth4u’s servers, with no check-ins and no fees. The final version is provided as is, with no further updates, for the Renter’s own booth business and the same 5-device limit.
  • If photobooth4u retires only the online booth and keeps offering the Offline App, Lifetime Online renters get the Offline App included at no extra cost, and Lifetime Offline and Lifetime Online + Offline renters keep working as they are.

7. Payment, refunds and fair use

  • The Lifetime price is paid in full, once; the code is issued after payment is confirmed.
  • Try first: before paying, the Renter can use the free Trial Mode (the trial code is shown on the about-us page) to test their devices, camera and printer.
  • Refunds: because the Renter can test everything first, payments are non-refundable once the code is issued. The one exception is if the booth doesn’t work on the Renter’s supported devices: photobooth4u first fixes it, or moves the Renter to the plan that works, within 7 days of payment, and refunds the Lifetime price only if neither is possible.
  • Fair use: the code is for the Renter’s own photo booth business. It may not be resold, sublicensed or shared with other photo booth businesses, or used for illegal or offensive content.
  • Suspension: for a breach of fair use, photobooth4u gives a written warning and a chance to fix it before suspending the code. photobooth4u may suspend a code immediately if it is posted publicly, shared with other photo booth businesses, or used for illegal content, or to protect the security of the service. If the code was leaked rather than shared on purpose, the Renter gets a new code with their events and settings kept.
  • Transfer: if the Renter sells their whole photo booth business, the code can be moved once to the new owner’s email, on request and with proof of the sale. A code cannot be sold or passed on separately from the business.
