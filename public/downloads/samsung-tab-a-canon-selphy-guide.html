Method 1 — Direct USB Cable (Recommended)

Connect your Samsung Tab A11 directly with a cable. Prints immediately with no extra apps.

📦 What You Need

  • Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 (USB-C port)
  • USB-C OTG Adapter (USB-C male to USB-A female)
  • USB Printer Cable (Standard USB-A to Mini-B or USB-C, matching your SELPHY's data port)
  • Canon SELPHY printer (powered ON with paper and ink cassette loaded)
  • Photobooth4U Android App (version 1.0.60+)
1

Connect the Cable

  • Plug the USB-C OTG adapter into your Samsung Tab A11.
  • Connect the USB cable from the adapter into the data port on the side or back of the SELPHY printer.
2

Allow USB Permission on Tablet

A pop-up will appear on your Samsung tablet screen: "Allow photobooth4u to access the USB device?"

  • Check the box "Always open photobooth4u when this device is connected".
  • Tap OK / Allow.
3

Select the Printer in Photobooth4U

  • Open the Photobooth4U app on your tablet.
  • In the Settings / Printer panel, your printer appears as Canon SELPHY CP... (USB) · photo (SELPHY).
  • Tap it to select it. That's it!
4

Ready to Print!

Whenever a photo session finishes, the app automatically converts the image to the Canon SELPHY dye-sub raster format and streams it directly to the printer. The paper will cycle 4 times (Yellow → Magenta → Cyan → Laminate) and eject into the tray!

💡
No Third-Party Apps Needed! You do NOT need NokoPrint, PrintHand, or Canon PRINT apps. The Photobooth4U app drives the SELPHY hardware directly over USB.

Method 2 — Wi-Fi Connection

Print completely wirelessly using Wi-Fi Direct or your local venue network.

📦 What You Need

  • Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 with Wi-Fi turned ON
  • Canon SELPHY CP1300 / CP1500 with Wi-Fi capability
  • No cables required!
📡
Wi-Fi Direct (Direct Connection)
Recommended for events — no router or venue Wi-Fi required.
1

Enable Direct Connection on Printer

  • On CP1500: Press OK → Wi-Fi Settings → Connection Settings → Other → Direct Connection → toggle ON.
  • On CP1300: Press Home → Wi-Fi Settings → Direct Connection → toggle ON.
  • Take note of the SSID (Wi-Fi Name) and Password shown on the printer display.
2

Connect Tablet to Printer's Wi-Fi

  • On your Samsung Tab A11, open Settings → Connections → Wi-Fi.
  • Tap the network named SELPHY-CP....
  • Enter the password shown on the printer display and tap Connect.
  • If Android shows "Internet may not be available. Stay connected?", tap Yes / Stay Connected.
3

Select Printer in Photobooth4U

  • Open Photobooth4U. The app will automatically discover the SELPHY via IPP/Bonjour.
  • Tap your printer in the list to select it. Photos will print wirelessly in background!

Troubleshooting & Pro Tips

Quick solutions to common issues during events.

🔴 USB Printer says "Tap to allow USB access"
Tap on the printer name in the app. Android will show the USB permission prompt. Check "Always open photobooth4u" and tap OK.
🔴 "Paper feed error — check paper cassette" (Error 0x02)
Make sure the paper cassette is inserted all the way until it clicks. Ensure you don't load more than 18 sheets at once into the cassette, and make sure photo paper is glossy-side UP.
🔴 "Ink cassette empty" (Error 0x06)
The dye-sublimation ink ribbon has completed its print cycles (usually 36 or 54 prints per cassette). Eject the ribbon on the side compartment and slide in a new cassette.
🔴 "Media/job mismatch" (Error 0x0A)
You are printing a 4×6" Postcard layout, but a Card or L-size paper tray is loaded (or vice versa). Check that both the paper cassette and ink ribbon match (e.g. Canon KP-108IN for standard postcard 4×6").

Event Pro Tips

📦 Clear Space Behind Printer

  • SELPHY paper passes out of the back 4 times!
  • Leave at least 15 cm (6 inches) of free space behind the printer.

🔒 Lock Screen / Kiosk Mode

  • Keep the Samsung Tab A11 plugged into power during events.
  • Photobooth4U keeps the tablet awake continuously.
