Lifetime Access FAQ

About the plans

What are the three Lifetime plans?

Lifetime Online (₱2,999): the online booth with galleries, QR codes and My Events. Lifetime Offline (₱3,499): the Offline App for Windows, Mac and Android tablets. Lifetime Online + Offline (₱4,999): both on one code.

Which plan should I choose?

Online if your venues have reliable internet. Offline if venues often have weak or no signal. Both if you want either option for any event.

Is it really one payment?

Yes. There are no monthly or per-event fees for the features in your plan, and the code never expires.

Can I add the other side later?

Yes. Lifetime Online owners add the Offline App for ₱2,499, and Lifetime Offline owners add the online booth for ₱1,999. The price is confirmed in writing before it is switched on.

What you get

How many events can I run?

Unlimited.

How many devices can I use?

Up to 5 at the same time on one code, on every plan. You can free up to 3 slots per month; if a device is lost, stolen or broken, message us and we can free more.

Which devices are supported?

The devices, operating systems and browsers listed under “Supported devices” on the about-us page on the day you pay (Windows 10 or later, macOS 11 or later, Android 7.0 or later for the Offline App; a current Chrome or Safari for the online booth). Support for versions older than 5 years may end with 90 days’ notice.

Does it include printing?

Yes, when printing is switched on for your code. It covers Canon Selphy, receipt printers and custom sizes. The one limit: the Android Offline App prints only to Bluetooth thermal receipt printers, so for photo printers use the online booth or the Windows / Mac Offline App.

Are updates free?

Yes for bug fixes, security fixes, compatibility updates and improvements to the features in your plan.

Are future new features free too?

Not always. Add-ons and new paid features (for example retakes and guest payments today) are not part of the Lifetime price. If you want one, an additional fee may apply, and it is agreed in writing before it is switched on. You are never forced to buy one, and nothing already included is taken away or put behind a fee.

What is not included?

Hardware (tablet, laptop, camera, printer, paper, ink), internet, third-party fees such as PayMongo, and add-ons.

Online and Offline

What happens when the venue has no internet?

The online booth keeps taking photos and uploads them when it reconnects. The Offline App works fully without internet, and guests get their QR over the booth Wi-Fi.

Which devices does the Offline App run on?

Windows and Mac laptops and Android tablets.

How does Offline activation work?

A device needs internet once to activate, or you can ask for an offline activation key. It should go online at least once every 75 days to renew; after a further 30 days without internet it pauses until it reconnects.

Photos and privacy

How long are my photos kept?

Online photos are kept 7 days after the event’s last photo, then deleted automatically, so save your copies in that time. Offline photos stay on your device only, so keep your own backups.

Can photobooth4u see or use my photos?

No. Photos are only accessed when you ask for help with a specific event.

Do I need to tell guests?

Yes. Under the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) you must tell guests that photos are taken and shared.

Trust and protection

What does “Lifetime” mean?

The code never expires and you are never charged again to keep using the features in your plan, for as long as photobooth4u operates them.

What if photobooth4u shuts down?

You get at least 90 days’ written notice, with full access and time to download your photos and setup. Every buyer then gets a final Offline App that works forever without our servers, with no check-ins and no fees. It is provided as is, with no further updates.

What if photobooth4u retires only the online booth?

If the Offline App keeps going, Lifetime Online buyers get the Offline App included at no extra cost. Lifetime Offline and Lifetime Online + Offline buyers carry on as they are.

Can I try before paying?

Yes. Use the free Trial Mode to test your devices, camera and printer. The trial code is shown on the about-us page.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are non-refundable once the code is issued. The only exception is if the booth doesn’t work on your supported devices: we fix it or move you to the plan that works within 7 days, and refund only if neither is possible.

What if the internet or hosting goes down?

Planned maintenance is announced at least 48 hours ahead. An activated Offline App keeps working through outages.

Account and support

I forgot my code. What do I do?

Use “Forgot your code?” on My Events (online plans), or message us from your registered email and we’ll resend it.

I lost a device or my code leaked. What do I do?

Remove the device on My Events (online) or message us (Offline App). We can issue a new code and keep your events and settings.

Can I share or resell the code?

No. It is for your own photo booth business only. If a code is posted publicly or shared with other photo booth businesses, we can suspend it right away.

Can I transfer the code if I sell my business?

Yes, once, if you sell your whole photo booth business. Message us with proof of the sale and we move the code to the new owner’s email. A code cannot be sold on its own.

How fast is support?

By Messenger or email, normally within 24 hours. Message ahead on event days. Support covers the photobooth4u software and your account, not your hardware, printers or internet.

What if the booth fails during my event?

We fix problems as fast as we can, and an activated Offline App keeps working without internet. Our responsibility is limited to the price you paid, so test before each event and keep a backup plan for important ones.

How do I pay?

Maribank or GoTyme. The code is emailed after payment is confirmed, normally the same day.
