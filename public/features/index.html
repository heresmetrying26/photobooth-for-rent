Printing & hardware compatibility

2 Printers Setup, Thermal & Canon SELPHY

Speed up busy event lines with two or more printers, print 1-second retro strips on Bluetooth or USB thermal printers, or run wireless/wired Canon SELPHY prints with zero pop-up dialogs.

Multi-printer setup

Set Up 2 (or More) Printers

  • + Add Printer 2 / 3+: Add a second or third printer right inside Print settings under Printer 1.
  • Share the work (round-robin): Rotates pages and copies across Printer 1 → Printer 2 → Printer 3 with automatic failover if one printer stops.
  • Parallel Extra Size: Or set Printer 2 to automatically print a second paper size (e.g., 4R on Printer 1 + a thermal receipt strip or Instax on Printer 2) with zero print-limit penalty.
  • Per-printer tuning: Each printer keeps its own quality, margins/edges, and lightness/color correction—or copy Printer 1's settings in one tap.
80 mm wide & 58 mm

Bluetooth & USB Wired Thermal

  • Wide 80 mm & portable 58 mm: Compatible with Vozy G80 (80 mm auto-cut), Vozy MP210 / 50, Epson TM-T20III / T88 / m30, Xprinter (XP-80C, XP-N160II, XP-58), Munbyn, Rongta, Star, ArcticBreeze RP30, Goojprt PT-210 & pocket printers.
  • Direct Bluetooth & USB wired: Pairs via Classic Bluetooth (RFCOMM SPP) on Android or prints over USB / Wi-Fi ESC/POS on Windows, Mac, and Android.
  • Clean retro dithering: Floyd-Steinberg halftone dithering, Best / Normal / Draft quality, 5-level darkness test strips, and anti-banding smooth-print calibration.
Wireless & USB OTG

Canon SELPHY & USB Printers

Setup › Extras › Output mode

GIF Styles & Live Motion

Available on Online, Windows/Mac Desktop, and Android Offline apps. Choose GIF only or Photo + GIF (Both) to give guests a still strip and an animated GIF on one QR code.

1 · Picture frame

Where the GIF sits

  • Chosen layout (default): Places the animated frames inside your active event layout (4-cut strip, 2×2 grid, polaroid, etc.) with all custom branding and text.
  • Solo layout: Places the motion inside a large 1-photo frame with your title and design, even when printed photos use a strip.
  • Plain camera: Full wide camera feed with no borders, event title, or overlays.
2 · Animation style

4 Motion Styles

  • ⚡ Auto (Smart Match): Automatically picks Story Replay for vertical/receipt strips and Multi-Box Boomerang for grids and multi-cut sheets.
  • ✨ Multi-Box Boomerang: Captures motion during the countdown before each shot; every box animates together in sync.
  • 🎬 Story Replay: Plays one box at a time in order (Box 1 → 2 → 3 → 4), then pauses on the finished strip.
  • 🎞 Full Session: Continuous recording across the whole session, dynamically fast-forwarded into a smooth 4–5 second recap.
3 · Loop mode & Looks

Playback & Filters

  • 🔄 Boomerang (default): Plays forward to the final pose, then smoothly reverses in a seamless ping-pong bounce loop.
  • ▶️ Forward only: Plays straight through from start to finish and restarts, with automatic fast-forward pacing so nothing feels cut short.
  • Vintage & color looks: Selected booth filters (Film, Faded, 70s, Instant, Noir, Soft skin) apply to GIF frames too.

Imposition & Speed

Sheet Layout, Binding & Two-Printer Pooling

  • Stack-cut or In-order collation: Stack-cut arranges cards across sheets so cutting the stacked pages drops every booklet into the exact page order automatically.
  • Grid presets: Auto (~45×34 mm cards; 8 on 4×6, 32 on A4), 6 per sheet, or 8 per sheet. Works on real sheet sizes (4×6, A4, Letter—not receipt rolls or narrow strips).
  • Binding gutter & page numbers: Optional 6 mm left binding margin with dashed fold guide, staple placement dots, and rotated frame numbers.
  • Two-printer pooling: Optionally assign a second printer to split sheets or copies in parallel and cut guest wait time in half.

XL Print Support
