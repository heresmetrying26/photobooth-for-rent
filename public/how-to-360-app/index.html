Step 1 · Overview

One separate app, two ways to run your 360 booth

360 Arm Cam (com.photobooth4u.spinarm) is a dedicated standalone app with its own dark 360 Home Screen icon. It installs side-by-side with photobooth4u without replacing your main photo booth app, and opens straight to the live camera preview.

Mode A · Phone / iPad Only

Standalone 360 Mode (No Laptop Needed)

Your iPhone, iPad, or Android phone records, applies Slow-Mo / Speed Ramp / Boomerang, burns in your transparent PNG overlay & music, and hosts a local Wi-Fi Sharing Station right on the device.

  • Unlock with your normal code — automatically uses 0 booth device slots
  • Requires 360 video desk switched ON for your code
  • Opens straight to the full-screen camera on every launch
Mode B · Free Companion

Connected to Laptop 360 Desk

Mount your phone on the spinner arm as a wireless 60/120fps camera. Every spin auto-uploads over 5 GHz Wi-Fi to your Windows/Mac laptop for FFmpeg presets, Live Wall, and Spin & Print.

  • Tap Pair to Laptop 360 Desk (No Code Needed)
  • Zero activation code needed on the phone
  • Pair by scanning the Laptop QR or typing the laptop URL

Why a separate app?

No booth webview overhead, instant native full-screen layout (no clipping on iPhone 15 Pro Max / 16 / 17), immediate camera startup, and a distinct dark charcoal icon so event staff never tap the wrong app in a hurry.

Step 2 · Installation

Install 360 Arm Cam on iPhone, iPad, or Android

Pick your device below. Both platforms install beside photobooth4u and keep all of your main booth settings untouched.

  1. 1

    Download Sideloadly & photobooth4u-360-arm-cam-ios.ipa

    On a Windows PC or Mac, install free Sideloadly (Windows: also install Apple’s non-Microsoft-Store iTunes linked on sideloadly.io; Mac: drag Sideloadly into Applications). Then click Download iPhone / iPad (.ipa) at the top of this page. Do not unzip the .ipa file.

    On Mac: If macOS says “Sideloadly” Not Opened, click Done (never Move to Trash), go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

  2. 2

    Connect your iPhone or iPad via USB

    Plug your iPhone/iPad into the computer, unlock the screen, and tap Trust This Computer if prompted.

  3. 3

    Drag the .ipa into Sideloadly & click Start

    Open Sideloadly, confirm your device appears under iDevice, drag photobooth4u-360-arm-cam-ios.ipa onto the IPA icon, enter your Apple ID email (a free Apple ID works), and click Start. Enter your Apple ID password & 2FA code if asked.

  4. 4

    Trust Developer Certificate & turn on Developer Mode (iOS 16+)

    Once the dark 360 Arm Cam icon appears on your Home Screen:

    • Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID under Developer App, and tap Trust.
    • On iOS 16 or newer, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode, toggle it ON, restart the phone, and confirm Turn On.
  5. 5

    Allow permissions on first launch

    When you open 360 Arm Cam, allow Camera, Microphone (optional), Photos (to save clips & pick PNG overlays), and Local Network (required for Wi-Fi Sharing Station & Laptop 360 Desk).

Free Apple ID 7-day refresh tip

Apps installed with a free Apple ID stay signed for 7 days. Before an event, connect your iPhone to Sideloadly and click Start again (~30 seconds) without deleting the app — your activation, PNG overlays, music, and sensitivity settings all stay saved.

  1. 1

    Download photobooth4u-360-arm-cam-android.apk

    Open this page (or /downloads/#spinarm) directly on your Android phone or tablet and tap Download Android (.apk). No computer needed.

  2. 2

    Allow “Install unknown apps” & install

    Tap the finished .apk file in your browser Downloads or Files app and choose Install. If Android prompts, turn on Allow from this source for Chrome/Files, then tap Install again.

  3. 3

    Open the dark 360 Arm Cam icon & allow permissions

    Find the dark charcoal 360 Arm Cam icon in your app drawer (separate from photobooth4u). On first launch, allow Camera, Microphone, and Photos/Media so overlays, music, and clip saving work smoothly.

Step 3 · Automatic 0-Slot Activation

Activate Standalone 360 (Uses 0 Device Slots Automatically)

Just enter your normal photobooth4u access code — no -360 suffix needed. Because you are signing in through the standalone 360 Arm Cam app, it automatically activates with 0 device slots used.

  1. 1

    Connect to internet once to activate

    Make sure the phone is online (Wi-Fi or mobile data) for the one-time activation check. After activation, Standalone 360 & the built-in Wi-Fi Sharing Station work offline at your venue.

  2. 2

    Type your normal photobooth4u access code

    In the Activate Standalone 360 card, type your existing code — for example PB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. No special suffix is needed (if you already typed -360 at the end, that still works too).

  3. 3

    Tap Activate & Open Camera

    As soon as it verifies, the app jumps straight into the full-screen live 360 camera preview. Every time you open 360 Arm Cam after that, it skips the activation screen and opens the live camera immediately.

  4. 4

    Need to check status or switch codes later?

    Tap ✕ in the top-right corner of the camera screen to return to the status card. You’ll see ✓ Standalone 360 Active with the 0 slots used badge, plus buttons to reopen the camera or deactivate/switch codes.

Zero booth slots consumed — works even if your booth slots are full

Activating inside the standalone 360 Arm Cam app never counts against your iPad, Android tablet, or laptop device limit. Just make sure 360 video desk is switched ON for your access code (message us if it says 360 Video Booth isn’t enabled on this code yet). If you only want to connect the phone to your laptop’s 360 Desk, skip activation and tap Pair to Laptop 360 Desk (No Code Needed).

Adding a Transparent PNG Frame Overlay & Music

  1. 1

    Prepare a transparent PNG matching your aspect ratio

    Export from Canva or Photoshop as PNG with Transparent Background:

    • 9:16 Portrait (Reels / TikTok — Default): 1080 × 1920 px
    • 1:1 Square: 1080 × 1080 px
    • 16:9 Landscape: 1920 × 1080 px
  2. 2

    Open 🎨 Look & Overlay (or ⚙️ Settings)

    Notice that Gyro Auto-Start pauses automatically while Settings is open so you can adjust everything safely on the arm.

  3. 3

    Pick your PNG overlay

    Tap Files PNG (recommended — preserves 100% alpha transparency directly from iCloud Drive / Files / Downloads) or Photos PNG (from your photo library). You’ll see a live thumbnail preview right inside Settings. You can also type an optional Overlay Title (e.g. Anna & Mark) and Subtitle / Date.

  4. 4

    Choose Motion Effect, Color Look & Music

    • Motion: Slow-Mo, Super Slow, Speed Ramp (fast → slow → fast), Boomerang, or Original.
    • Color Look: Clean natural or stylized looks applied on-device during hardware rendering.
    • Music: Tap 🎵 Choose Music to select an MP3 or M4A audio file from Files/Storage.
  5. 5

    Calibrate Gyro Spin Auto-Start (OFF by default until calibrated)

    On first open, Auto-Spin is OFF and the trigger threshold stays Not set so carrying or testing the phone in your hand never starts an accidental recording. A welcome card over the live camera explains the 5-second calibration with two choices: 🎯 Start Calibration (once clamped on the 360 arm) or Start in Manual Mode (tap ● Record manually and calibrate anytime later).

    When you run 🎯 Calibrate (from the bottom bar or ⚙️ Settings): Step 1 (Hold Still) measures resting vibration, and Step 2 (Spin Arm) measures your arm’s real speed, saves the trigger threshold, and switches Auto-Spin ON automatically!

Step 5 · Guest Delivery (Scan QR + Typing)

Share 360 videos via Quick Share & the Built-in Sharing Station

Guests can grab their 360 video in seconds — whether they prefer scanning a QR code or typing their WhatsApp number, Email, or the download link directly.

[360 Arm Cam finishes spin & renders MP4] → Instant Quick Share on Phone + Auto-Pops on Sharing Station Tablet/Phone
Option 1 · Right on the Phone

Quick Share Sheet

Right after a spin finishes rendering (~3–8 seconds), the Quick Share overlay shows:

  • Looping preview of the finished 360 video
  • QR Code for instant guest scan on the same Wi-Fi
  • Tappable / Copyable Link right below the QR
  • WhatsApp & Email inputs + AirDrop / native Share

Running a separate Sharing Station? Turn ON Send straight to Sharing Station (skip phone QR) in ⚙️ Settings so the arm phone resets immediately for the next guest!

Option 2 · Dedicated Guest Tablet / Phone

Built-in Wi-Fi Sharing Station

Turn any second iPad, Android tablet, phone, or laptop into a dedicated guest Sharing Station with zero extra app install:

  • Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi (or hotspot)
  • Tap 📱 Station on the 360 Arm Cam HUD
  • Scan the Station QR (or type the URL, e.g. http://192.168.x.x:8088/station) in Safari or Chrome on the second device
  • Leave that screen open — every new 360 video pops up automatically!

What guests can do on the Sharing Station screen

1. Scan QR

Point their phone camera at the big QR code to view and save the MP4 right away.

2. Type / Copy Link, WhatsApp & Email

Copy or type the visible link below the QR, type a WhatsApp number (auto-formats 09… → 63…), or type an Email address. The 35s auto-close timer pauses automatically while typing.

3. Browse Gallery

Tap Gallery in the header anytime to pick any earlier 360 spin from the event and re-open its QR / WhatsApp / Email options.

Optional · Connected to Laptop Mode

Pair with Laptop 360 Desk (Free Companion)

Want desktop FFmpeg speed-ramp presets, a big Live TV Wall, or Spin & Print on your Windows/Mac laptop? You can pair the same 360 Arm Cam app with your laptop in 10 seconds.

  1. 1

    Put laptop & phone on the same 5 GHz Wi-Fi

    Connect your Windows/Mac laptop and your 360 Arm phone to the same router or enable Make this laptop the booth Wi-Fi inside photobooth4u Desktop (no internet needed for local transfer).

  2. 2

    Open 360 Desk on your laptop

    In photobooth4u Desktop, open 360 Video Booth / 360 Desk to show the pairing QR code and local /send/… URL.

  3. 3

    Switch 360 Arm Cam to Laptop Desk & pair (QR or Manual URL)

    On the Entry Screen tap Pair to Laptop 360 Desk (No Code Needed) — or if already in Standalone mode, open ⚙️ Settings and switch the top mode pill to Laptop Desk. Then tap 📷 Pair QR:

    • Scan QR: Point at your laptop’s 360 Desk QR until the top pill turns green.
    • Type / Paste URL: Tap Paste / Enter Laptop URL (or Enter Laptop URL Manually in Settings) and type the address shown on the laptop — you don’t even need to type http://.
  4. 4

    Record spins → auto-upload → render & share on Laptop

    Record from the phone (manually or via Gyro Auto-Start) or trigger remotely from the laptop. The raw clip uploads straight into your laptop’s 360 Desk queue, applies your laptop PNG overlay & effects, and pushes directly to the laptop’s Sharing Station!

Troubleshooting

Quick fixes

What you seeHow to fix it
Code says 360 Video Booth isn’t enabled on this code yet Ask us to switch 360 video desk ON for your access code in Admin, then activate your normal code again inside 360 Arm Cam.
Worried about using a booth device slot? Activating inside the standalone 360 Arm Cam app automatically uses 0 device slots — even when all of your iPad / laptop / Android booth slots are already full. (No -360 suffix required.)
PNG overlay has a solid white or black background Re-export with Transparent Background checked (PNG, not JPEG), save it to the Files app (or Downloads), and pick it with Files PNG instead of a compressed messaging photo.
Sharing Station page won’t open on the second tablet Confirm both devices are on the same Wi-Fi (turn off mobile data or VPN on the second device). On iPhone, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and ensure 360 Arm Cam is ON.
Gyro Auto-Start triggers too early or doesn’t start Open ⚙️ Settings → 🎯 Run Configure Sensitivity Wizard while the phone is clamped on the actual spinner arm, or drag the threshold slider higher (less sensitive) / lower (more sensitive).
iPhone says App is no longer available after 7 days Free Apple IDs expire every 7 days. Reconnect to Sideloadly and click Start again without deleting the app — takes ~30 seconds and keeps all your settings.
Laptop 360 Desk URL won’t connect Switch Windows Wi-Fi profile from Public to Private (or allow photobooth4u through Windows Firewall), verify both devices are on the same network, and try typing the IP:port via Enter Laptop URL Manually.
