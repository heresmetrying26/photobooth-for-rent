Run a 360 booth
with photobooth4u

The 360 arm's camera records the spin. photobooth4u gets the video, edits it (slow motion, your frame and music) and gives the guest a QR code to watch and save it. Editing is in the laptop app; the website shares the original video.

Part 1 · Before the event

What you need

Arm

The 360 arm

Any 360 platform with a camera or phone on the arm. It can carry an iPad, iPhone, Android phone, or a camera. The arm is not connected to the booth software: you start it with its own remote or switch.

Laptop

A Windows or Mac laptop

With the photobooth4u laptop app. It edits the videos and shares them with guests over the booth Wi-Fi, with no internet needed.

Wi-Fi

One Wi-Fi network

A small router at the booth (no internet needed) or the laptop's hotspot. The recording phone and guests' phones join it.

Part 2 · The camera on the arm

Record the spin

  1. 1

    Choose the quality

    Record at 1080p or higher. For smooth slow motion record at 60 frames per second (most phones have this setting). At 30 frames per second slow motion works but looks less smooth.

  2. 2

    Hold the picture steady

    Lock focus and exposure on the guests (tap and hold on a phone) so the picture doesn't pump as the arm turns. Turn off auto-sleep and keep the camera charged.

  3. 3

    Hold the phone upright

    Record in portrait for social media. The app crops to portrait or landscape for you, whichever way the phone was held.

  4. 4

    Aim for 10 to 15 seconds

    One full turn is plenty. Shorter clips edit faster. A slow-motion edit is twice as long as the clip you record.

  5. 5

    Light the guests

    A turning arm needs even light all round. Bright, even light gives a cleaner video than a dark room.

Part 3 · In the laptop app

Get the video to the laptop

Open the 360 desk from the dock at the bottom of the photo session screen. Videos arrive in the 360 inbox folder (in your Pictures → photobooth4u folder, and you can change it). Any of these routes works:

RouteHow
From the recording phoneThe desk shows a QR code. Scan it with the phone that recorded (on the booth Wi-Fi), tap Choose a video and pick the clip. It appears on the laptop in a few seconds. The page's address has a code that changes each time the app starts, so guests on the Wi-Fi can't use it.
Drop or choose filesDrag videos onto the desk, or tap Choose videos.
Save into the folderAnything saved into the 360 inbox shows up by itself: AirDrop to the Mac, a USB cable or card reader, a cloud folder, or a camera's Wi-Fi or FTP transfer.

Part 4 · The 360 desk

Edit and share

Tap a video to play it. Edit video makes the finished MP4 while you keep using the booth. Share + QR saves it in the event and shows the QR code for the guest.

StyleWhat it does
Classic slow-moHalf speed, with your frame, logo and title and your music. Turn on Smoother slow motion for a softer look (it takes longer).
Speed rampFast, then slow in the middle, then fast again.
BoomerangThe first 5 seconds forward, then backward. Made at 720p.
Original speedNormal speed, with the frame and title, and the original sound.
  1. 1

    Frame, logo and title

    Uses the booth's own design from Setup (your frame, stickers, logo, event name and date). Switch it off with Frame, logo and title.

  2. 2

    Music

    Tap Choose music once on this laptop and pick a song file you have the right to use at the event. It fades in and out and is cut to the video's length. Or choose Original sound or No sound.

  3. 3

    Trim

    In the player type a start and end time in seconds to cut the beginning or end before editing.

  4. 4

    Format

    Portrait (phones) is the default. Landscape and 720p (faster) are in the same panel.

  5. 5

    Do it automatically

    Tick Edit new videos by themselves and every video that arrives is edited as it comes in. Then you only tap it to show the QR.

  6. 6

    Guests watch and save

    The guest scans the QR code on the booth Wi-Fi and the video opens in their phone's browser with a Save video button. The edited video is saved in the event folder, and the original stays in the 360 inbox.

Part 5 · No laptop app

Using the website

The 360 desk is also in the website booth. There you can drop or choose videos and share them with a QR code, but the video is shared as recorded: no slow motion, frame or music. The website takes videos up to about 28 MB; for bigger ones, or to edit, use the laptop app. Guests watch the video in the event gallery's Videos tab.

Help

If something goes wrong

ProblemTry this
The 360 desk button isn't thereIt is an extra on your code. Message us if it is missing.
No editing controls, only the original is sharedThis copy of the laptop app has no video editor (it is installed with the app). Reinstall the latest app from the Downloads page. On a Mac with an Intel chip, message us.
The phone can't open the send pageIs the phone on the booth Wi-Fi and the laptop too? Restart the app to get a new QR code.
Slow motion looks jerkyThe camera recorded at 30 frames per second. Record at 60 next time, or tick Smoother slow motion.
Editing is slowChoose 720p, switch off Smoother slow motion, and trim the video to what you need. A 10 to 15 second clip should be ready in under a minute on a normal laptop.
The video is rotated or croppedThe app crops to fill a portrait (or landscape) picture. Keep the guests in the middle of the frame when you record.
