Part 1 · Before the event

What you need

Arm

The 360 arm

Any 360 platform with a camera or phone on the arm. It can carry an iPad, iPhone, Android phone, or a camera. The arm is not connected to the booth software: you start it with its own remote or switch.

Laptop

A Windows or Mac laptop

With the photobooth4u laptop app. It edits the videos and shares them with guests over the booth Wi-Fi, with no internet needed.

Wi-Fi

One Wi-Fi network

A small router at the booth (no internet needed) or the laptop's hotspot. The recording phone and guests' phones join it.

Part 2 · The camera on the arm

Record the spin

  1. 1

    Choose the quality & frame rate

    Record at 1080p or higher. For instant, buttery-smooth slow motion, record at 60 fps (for half-speed slow-mo) or 120 fps (for super slow-mo 0.25×). When the phone records at high frame rates, the booth renders real slow-motion frames instantly without extra processing wait time. At 30 fps slow motion still works, but may look less smooth unless smoother interpolation is enabled.

  2. 2

    Hold the picture steady

    Lock focus and exposure on the guests (tap and hold on a phone) so the picture doesn't pump as the arm turns. Turn off auto-sleep and keep the camera charged.

  3. 3

    Hold the phone upright

    Record in portrait for social media. The app crops to portrait or landscape for you, whichever way the phone was held.

  4. 4

    Aim for 10 to 15 seconds

    One full turn is plenty. Shorter clips edit faster. A slow-motion edit is twice as long as the clip you record.

  5. 5

    Light the guests

    A turning arm needs even light all round. Bright, even light gives a cleaner video than a dark room.

Part 2b · Controls on the desk and the phone

Set it on the laptop, control it from the phone

Three ways to record onto the 360 Desk:
• Option A (Recommended for iPhone/iPad): Native 360 Arm Camera — Open the photobooth4u iOS app, tap 🎥 360 Arm Camera (Free Companion), and scan the Desk QR. Uses the phone's gyroscope to auto-start recording the moment the arm spins, auto-stops when the arm stops, records buttery 60/120 fps, and uploads automatically. (Read detailed Arm Camera guide →)
• Option B (Desktop v1.1.94+ for DSLR / Mirrorless / GoPro / HDMI Capture Card): 📷 Record 360 — Inside the 360 Desk, click 📷 Record 360 to connect a Canon EOS, Nikon DSLR/Z, GoPro over USB or Sony / any camera via HDMI Capture Card. Configure shutter/aperture/ISO/WB in-app and trigger automatically using a Windows Tablet Gyro (Surface Pro on arm) + Configure sensitivity, a paired iPhone wireless sensor, or a remote clicker (F9), streaming clips straight into the 360 Inbox for auto-editing and QR sharing. (Read DSLR 360 step-by-step →)
• Option C (Zero install, any phone): Browser Send Page — Scan the 360 Desk QR in Safari/Chrome. Shows countdown timers, look choices, and video upload directly in the mobile browser.

  1. 1

    Set the spin on the laptop (once)

    Open the 360 desk and find Phone send page & spin timer. Enter how long one spin takes, how many spins, when the arm starts after you press record, and the tail. Tick Cut each new video to the spin so the laptop trims the clip for you. Under Phones may choose tick what a phone can pick: look, music, speed, a name on the video, a GIF.

  2. 2

    Scan the QR and attach the phone

    Scan the QR on the 360 desk with the phone (same booth Wi-Fi). The page says Connected to the laptop and shows how long to record. Attach the phone to the arm. Save the page to the Home Screen so next time it is two taps.

  3. 3

    Countdown, record, stop

    Press Start spin timer on the laptop (or Start the countdown on the laptop on the phone page). The laptop shows a big 3, 2, 1 and GO: start recording on the phone then, and start the arm when it says SPIN. At the STOP beep, stop the recording. A Bluetooth camera remote or an Apple Watch can start and stop the phone's camera if you have one.

  4. 4

    Upload, edit, share

    The video uploads by itself (it retries if the Wi-Fi drops). The page shows Waiting, Editing, Ready and then the guest QR. You can Re-edit, Share, Print or Delete a video from the phone. With Auto mode on, the laptop edits each new video by itself.

Which camera?

For zero cables on the arm, an iPhone 11+ (60/120 fps ultra-wide) with 360 Arm Camera is the fastest setup. For DSLR/mirrorless image quality, mount a Windows tablet (Surface Pro) + Canon/Nikon/GoPro inside the ring light via USB, or run a capture card / paired iPhone gyro sensor inside 360 Desk → 📷 Record 360 (DSLR 360 Guide →).

Part 3 · In the laptop app

Get the video to the laptop

Open the 360 desk from the dock at the bottom of the photo session screen. Videos arrive in the 360 inbox folder (in your Pictures → photobooth4u folder, and you can change it). Any of these routes works:

RouteHow
From the recording phoneThe desk shows a QR code. Scan it with the phone that recorded (on the booth Wi-Fi), tap Choose a video and pick the clip. It appears on the laptop in a few seconds. The page's address has a code that changes each time the app starts, so guests on the Wi-Fi can't use it.
Drop or choose filesDrag videos onto the desk, or tap Choose videos.
Save into the folderAnything saved into the 360 inbox shows up by itself: AirDrop to the Mac, a USB cable or card reader, a cloud folder, or a camera's Wi-Fi or FTP transfer.

  1. 1

    Open the 360 Studio

    In the photobooth4u Laptop App, open Photo Session, tap Extras in the bottom dock, and click 360 desk.

  2. 2

    In the top navigation bar (📷 Record 360 · 🎬 Videos & Queue · ✨ Presets · 🎨 Look & Frame · ✍️ Text & Branding · 🎵 Audio & FX), click ✨ Presets.

  3. 3

    Watch the Live Preview on the right

    Even before shooting a clip, the Live 360 Studio Preview panel on the right plays a built-in Sample Demo (or any video selected from your queue) with the preset's frame, particle effects, text, and color grade live. Filter across 6 tabs: Wedding & Debut (5), Birthday & Family (6), Corporate & School (8), Holidays (6), Nightlife (5), and ⭐ My presets.

Every new clip edits itself and generates its QR automatically. For manual clips, click ⚡ Auto-edit this video, ⚡ Auto-edit all videos, or tick One-tap.

Part 4b · The 360 desk

Customize, automate & share

Tap any video to preview it, choose one of the 8 built-in motion speed styles, or customize frames, text, music, and sharing options.

Motion StyleWhat it does
Classic slow-moHalf speed (0.5×), with your frame, logo and title and your music. Turn on Smoother slow motion for a softer look on 30 fps clips (it takes longer).
Speed rampFast, then slow in the middle, then fast again.
BoomerangThe first 5 seconds forward, then backward. Made at 720p.
Original speedNormal speed (1×), with the frame and title, and the original sound.
Super slow-moEverything at a quarter of the speed (0.25×). Best with a 60 or 120 fps recording; the smoother option helps on 30 fps clips.
Fast, then slowThe clip is cut in two equal parts: the first at 2×, the second at 0.5×.
Fast, slow, fastThree equal parts at 2×, 0.5× and 2×.
Slow-mo rollerFour equal parts at 1×, 0.25×, 2× and 0.5×, then the whole thing played backwards. It is made at 720p and is long, so record 8 seconds or less; a longer clip plays without the backwards part.
  1. 1

    The Connect panel & first-time check

    At the top of the 360 desk, 🔌 Connect lists what must be ready for videos to arrive: the video editor, the inbox folder (with free space), the network address phones connect to, the optional Wi-Fi QR for guests, whether a recorder has been seen, and when the last video was received. Click Test to check everything at once. The first time you open the 360 desk it asks Edit new videos automatically?—one click turns on Auto mode. If a recording starts and no video arrives within two minutes, Connect shows a warning with likely fixes.

  2. 2

    Save the send page & Smart Inbox Watcher

    Open the QR send page on your phone once, then add it to the Home Screen (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen; Android: browser menu → Add to Home screen). From then on it is two taps: open 360 Send, tap Record now. Use the laptop's hotspot to keep the address steady. Dropping files from slow SD cards or camera Wi-Fi? Smart Inbox Watcher verifies file readiness and settling time before queueing so no render is ever cut off.

  3. 3

    Hands-free start: spin timer & Bluetooth clickers

    Click Start spin timer on the 360 desk (or the button on the phone's send page) for a full-screen 3, 2, 1, GO, then SPIN and STOP with beeps. You can also trigger spins and sessions from up to 30 feet away using pocket selfie clickers (AB Shutter 3), presentation remotes (PageDown/Next), USB foot pedals, or wireless gamepads (8BitDo).

  4. 4

    Quick Customise & live tile previews

    Each preset tile draws a miniature preview using a still from your open video (or color swatches when none is open). Right under the preset grid, use 🎛️ Quick customise to swap Accent colour, switch Motion chips, move text (Top / Middle / Bottom, A− / A+), or adjust Effect intensity (Off / Subtle / Normal / Bold / Shuffle) without leaving the Presets tab.

  5. 5

    Frame, logo, custom Canva PNG & animated effects

    Under Look & Frame, use your booth's Setup design (frame, logo, stickers, event name, and date), choose one of the built-in designer frames, or click Upload Custom PNG to layer your own transparent Canva overlay. Add animated particle effects (and tune Trajectory, Location, Speed, Opacity, Size, and Wobble live).

  6. 6

    Format & light size for crowded events

    Portrait (9:16) is the default for TikTok, Instagram Stories, and Reels. Square (1:1) fits Instagram feeds, and Landscape (16:9) fills wide TVs and YouTube. Choose 720p for faster renders, or 540p Light when many guests are downloading on the booth Wi-Fi at once (files are a quarter to a sixth of Full HD size).

  7. 7

    Cinematic Color Engine (3D LUTs)

    Apply professional color grading looks in Look & Frame: Glamour B&W (high-contrast Kardashian-style skin tones), Blockbuster Teal & Orange, Golden Hour Warm Glow, Vibrant Pop, and Vintage Film. Grades preview in real time and render directly into the finished MP4.

  8. 8

    Custom text overlay & drag-to-position

    In Text & Branding, customize Line 1 & Line 2 (names, date, hashtag, emojis), pick from 8 built-in fonts, choose colors and drop shadow, and pick Always on or Fade in / out. Drag text directly on the Live 360 Studio Preview and pull the corner handle to resize.

  9. 9

    Trim

    In the player under the Live Preview, type a start and end time in seconds to cut the beginning or end before editing.

  10. 10

    Music & Beat-Drop Sync speed ramping

    In Audio & FX, tap Choose music track once on the laptop and pick a song file (it fades in/out and trims to fit), or choose Original video sound or Mute. Set a custom Beat drop (s) timestamp (or leave on Auto) in Look & Frame so the slow-motion speed ramp, Dynamic zoom punch-in, and optical Beat flash snap together on the beat.

  11. 11

    A song & intro/outro stingers per look

    In Audio & FX, click ＋ Add tracks to load multiple songs and ＋ Add intro/outro clips to load short sponsor or logo animations. Then in ✨ Presets, choose any look and set its own Music for this look, Opens with, and Ends with stingers (scaled and audio-normalized automatically).

  12. 12

    A different look for each guest (Rotating looks)

    In ✨ Presets → Guest choice & rotating looks, tick Give each new video a different look. Every automatically edited clip gets the next look from your active chips (shuffled with no repeats, or in list order). The chosen look name appears on the video tile, share card, and Sharing Station.

  13. 13

    Let the guest choose (on booth screen or phone)

    Tick Ask the guest to pick a look when their video arrives. A full-screen Choose your look picker appears on the booth screen (waiting until any photo session finishes)—and on the phone's 360 Send page after upload, where guests can also type their name as the video heading. Whoever picks first wins.

  14. 14

    Auto-process & auto-share in the background

    In Audio & FX (or via ⚡ Auto mode), tick Automatically process and edit incoming 360 videos and and get the QR code ready automatically so clips edit and share even while the 360 desk is closed. A badge on the 360 desk button counts waiting clips.

  15. 15

    GIFs, Square clips & Event numbers

    Once a video is edited, click 🎞️ GIF (~5 s sped-up loop) or ⬜ Square to share social-ready versions alongside the MP4 (or tick Also make a GIF for each shared video in Auto mode). Above the queue, event stats show shared count, guest views, and downloads.

  16. 16

    Two QR codes: join the Wi-Fi, then download

    In Setup › 6 Share, type your booth Wi-Fi name and password once. Share + QR then shows two codes: Step 1: Network joins the guest's phone to the booth Wi-Fi with no typing, and Step 2: Download opens the video offline. Use a dedicated booth Wi-Fi, not the venue's.

  17. 17

    Dual online & offline sharing

    When online, guests scanning the QR can use their phone's native Share sheet to post directly to TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp, or AirDrop. When offline on the booth Wi-Fi, guests get high-speed local downloads (Save video), one-tap link copy, and step-by-step guidance for iPhone Safari and Android Chrome.

  18. 18

    Spin & Print: photo strips & keychains from 360 spins

    Tap 🖨️ Spin & Print on the 360 desk to extract frames from any 360 video and print branded 2×6 photo strips, 2.5×7.5″ acrylic keychains, or 4R prints. Choose Multi-angle sequence (different rotation angles on a 3-shot strip) or Solo (1 hero pose). Every print carries a QR linking back to the guest's 360 video! Tick and 🖨️ auto-print strip / keychain to print automatically after each render.

Part 5 · Secondary displays

Sharing Station & Live Wall

Keep lines moving at busy events by offloading video downloads and crowd entertainment to secondary screens over the booth Wi-Fi without internet.

Station

Sharing Station (iPad / Tablet)

Set up a tablet on a stand a few paces away from the 360 platform. Connect it to the booth Wi-Fi and open http://<laptop-ip>:47777/station. Guests browse finished videos in a smooth gallery, tap their clip to display their personal download QR code, and download their video. The QR dialog automatically resets after 35 seconds of inactivity so the next guest can use it.

Wall

Live Wall & Crowd Sync (TV / Projector)

Connect a smart TV, laptop, or projector to the booth Wi-Fi and open http://<laptop-ip>:47777/wall. Features real-time Live Audience Sync: during recording countdowns, the screen displays high-impact pulsating crowd prompts (“3... 2... 1... JUMP! 🎬”) so the whole audience joins in. As soon as a new 360 spin finishes processing, the wall immediately interrupts idle playback with a celebration spotlight banner (“✨ New 360 Spin Ready!”) and instant replay, complete with an on-screen QR code for spectators to scan.

Part 6 · No laptop app

Using the website

The 360 desk is also in the website booth. There you can drop or choose videos and share them with a QR code, but the video is shared as recorded: no slow motion, frame or music. The website takes videos up to about 28 MB; for bigger ones, or to edit, use the laptop app. Guests watch the video in the event gallery's Videos tab.

Help

If something goes wrong

ProblemTry this
On a Mac, phones or the tablet can't reach the laptopThe first time the app runs, macOS asks "Do you want the application photobooth4u to accept incoming network connections?" Click Allow. If you clicked Deny, open System Settings → Network → Firewall → Options, find photobooth4u and set it to Allow incoming connections. Then check that the Mac and the phones are on the same booth Wi-Fi.
Guests' downloads are slow at a busy eventSet the video Quality to 540p Light in the 360 desk, and keep the booth Wi-Fi to the booth (not the venue's shared Wi-Fi).
An iPhone video shows black or won't play in the 360 deskNewer iPhones save HEVC (.mov) video that some laptops can't play. The 360 desk makes a small preview copy by itself (a few seconds). The finished video is still made from the original.
The 360 desk button isn't thereIt is an extra on your code. Message us if it is missing.
No editing controls, only the original is sharedThis copy of the laptop app has no video editor (it is installed with the app). Reinstall the latest app from the Downloads page. On a Mac with an Intel chip, message us.
The phone can't reach the laptopSame Wi-Fi? Scan the QR on the laptop's 360 desk again. The page says Connected to the laptop when it works. The address stays the same when the laptop app is reopened; it only changes if the app is reinstalled or the laptop joins a different Wi-Fi.
Step 2 won't open after joining the Wi-FiSome phones leave a Wi-Fi that has no internet and go back to mobile data. Tell the guest to stay on the booth Wi-Fi (on Android, tap Stay connected if asked), then scan Step 2 again.
The Step 1 code does nothingiPhones and Android 10 or newer join from the Camera app. Older Android phones need Google Lens or a QR app. The name and password are under the code to type in.
Only one QR code showsNo booth Wi-Fi is set. Add it in Setup › Share & start. The video also has to be shared on the device itself, not queued for later.
The phone can't open the send pageIs the phone on the booth Wi-Fi and the laptop too? Restart the app to get a new QR code.
Slow motion looks jerkyThe camera recorded at 30 frames per second. Record at 60 or 120 fps next time, or tick Smoother slow motion.
Editing is slowRecord at 60 fps (real slow-mo needs no extra frame generation), choose 720p, switch off Smoother slow motion, and trim the video. Hardware GPU encoding is used automatically when available.
The video is rotated or croppedThe app crops to fill a portrait, square, or landscape picture. Keep the guests in the middle of the frame when you record.
Sharing Station or Live Wall won't openEnsure the tablet or TV browser is connected to the same booth Wi-Fi router as the laptop. Check the laptop IP address shown on the 360 desk (e.g. http://192.168.1.20:47777/station).
Guest cannot save video on iPhoneIn Safari, tap Save video to download. Then tap the blue download arrow or the Share icon in the Safari toolbar and choose Save Video to save it directly to the Apple Photos camera roll.
