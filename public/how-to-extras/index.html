Part 1 · Setup → Extras

Vintage looks

  1. 1

    Pick a look

    In Setup → Extras, open the filter row and tap Film, Faded, 70s, Instant or Noir. The live view changes at once. The 70s and Instant looks add a warm light leak in the corner, and all of them add grain and slightly darker corners to the saved photo.

  2. 2

    Date stamp (optional)

    Tick Date stamp to print an orange camera-style date, like '26 10 29, in the bottom-right corner of each photo.

  3. 3

    Guests can choose too

    The filter chips under the live view and on the “Get ready” screen include the vintage looks, so guests can pick their own.

  4. 4

    Guest screen (traditional booth)

    In Setup → Extras → Filter, tick Let guests choose a look on the guest screen. It is off until you turn it on, so the guest screen stays clean. Then tick up to 6 looks to offer. Guests see one small ✨ Look button; tapping it opens a row of round previews of themselves in each look, and a tap picks one. The look lasts for that guest: the next guest starts again with the look you chose, and so does the booth after 90 seconds with nobody there. The button is shown while the booth waits for a guest and on “Get ready”, never during the countdown.

Good to know

Looks apply to photos, GIFs, flipbook frames and photos from the photographer desk. The look is saved with your setup, and Soft skin can be used on top of it.

Setup → Extras → Output mode

GIFs in your frame

A GIF is no longer just the camera view. By default it sits in the one-photo Solo layout with your frame, title and design, even when the photos use a strip.

  1. 1

    Choose GIF only or Both

    Under Output mode pick GIF only or Both. The GIF frame choice appears underneath.

  2. 2

    Pick the GIF frame

    Solo layout (the default) puts the GIF in one big picture with your title. Chosen layout uses the layout of the photos, with the same picture in every spot. Plain camera view is the old wide video without a frame.

Good to know

The Solo layout is tall, so the camera view is cropped to fit. Ask guests to stand in the middle. The GIF takes a few seconds longer to make while it is placed in the frame.

Part 2 · Dock → Photographer desk

Photographer desk

For a photographer who walks around the event with a real camera. An assistant at the booth drops each photo into your frame, prints it and gives the guest a QR code.

  1. 1

    Open the desk

    Tap Photographer desk in the dock at the bottom of the Photo Session screen.

  2. 2

    Add the photos

    Drop the photographer's photos on the desk or tap Choose photos (JPEG, PNG or WebP, from the memory card or phone). Laptop app: tap Choose folder once, and photos the camera software saves into that folder appear by themselves. Photos already in the folder when you pick it are not added.

  3. 3

    Pick the photo

    Tap a photo. If your layout uses several photos (a strip, say), tap up to that many in order. One photo fills them all. For a roving photographer, tick One photo per print above the photos: every print is then one big photo in the Solo layout, even when the event uses a strip. The choice is remembered on this device.

  4. 4

    Make print + QR

    The photo goes into your frame or layout with your chosen look, and the normal result screen opens: the Print button (or auto-print) and the guest's QR code. Press Done and the desk is back for the next photo. Used photos are greyed out as “Printed”.

Part 3 · Dock → Quick shot

Quick shot

The booth as a handheld camera on a tablet or phone. Each tap takes a photo and saves it quietly in the background; you show the QR code only when a guest asks.

  1. 1

    Open Quick shot

    Tap Quick shot in the dock. You see the camera full screen. The shutter uses the tablet or phone camera (not a USB camera). Flip camera switches between the front and back cameras. With a USB camera selected the screen still opens, without a shutter, to show the photos sent from phones (see below).

  2. 2

    Tap the big button

    One photo per tap, no countdown. It goes into your frame with your chosen look and is saved in order. A Quick shot is always one big photo in the Solo layout, even when the event's photos are strips, and its file name ends in _solo.

  3. 3

    Watch the dots

    Each shot has a small thumbnail with a dot: grey saving, green saved, orange saved on the device and waiting for the internet, red could not be saved.

  4. 4

    Show the QR when asked

    Tap the thumbnail of that guest's photo to see its QR code. If the internet is down the photo is kept on the device, and the QR opens the event gallery where it appears once it uploads. Two buttons on the same screen open the normal result screen with its Print button: Print framed (the photo in your frame) and Print original (the photo as it came from the camera, fitted to the paper, with no frame).

Photos sent from a phone

The photographer takes photos with their own phone and sends them to the booth. Each photo is put in your frame and saved in the background, and gets a QR code, the same as a Quick shot. They all appear in one row on the Quick shot screen.

  1. 1

    Show the phone QR

    On the Quick shot screen tap 📱 Phone upload. A QR code appears. The photographer scans it with their phone and taps Take a photo or Choose photos. Several photos can be sent at once.

  2. 2

    Laptop app and Android app: same Wi-Fi

    The phone joins the Wi-Fi (or hotspot) the laptop or tablet is on. The photos go straight to the booth and keep working with no internet. The booth keeps the originals in a Roving inbox folder inside the photos folder.

  3. 3

    Website: any internet

    The phone can be on any network. Its photos are sent to the event's Drive folder and the booth page fetches them about every 10 seconds, so there is a short delay. The link works for up to 12 hours; show the QR again after that. The booth only starts checking Drive after you have shown this QR once. The photographer's own copies are kept in the event folder as rove_… files and are not shown in the guests' gallery.

  4. 4

    Show the guest's QR, print

    Photos arrive on their own, newest first, with a coloured dot (see above). Tap one for its QR code, Print framed or Print original. A small number on the dock button shows photos that arrived while the screen was closed.

Good to know

Every photo from a phone uses the one-photo Solo layout, even when the event uses strips. Phone photos are shrunk on the website path (to about 2400 px) so they send quickly; the laptop path keeps the full size. On the offline Android app the phone joins the tablet's Wi-Fi or hotspot and sends photos to the tablet.

Part 4 · Setup → Extras → Output mode

Flipbook

The guest records a few seconds of video. The booth prints its frames as small cards, in order. Cut them apart, stack them in order, staple one edge, and flick through for a tiny moving picture. They also get an animated GIF QR code.

  1. 1

    Choose Flipbook

    In Setup → Extras → Output mode tap Flipbook. Set the clip length (4 to 7 seconds, 6 by default) and the frames in the book (16, 24, 30 or 40; 30 by default). A longer clip or more frames gives a thicker book.

  2. 2

    Cover and back cards

    Both are ticked by default. The cover shows the event name, date and logo from your design. The back says “Thank you!”. Untick either if you don't want it.

  3. 3

    The guest records

    Guests tap Start, see a 3-2-1 countdown and “Go!”, then move for the whole clip. There is no retake. The result screen shows all the cards in order.

  4. 4

    Print

    Choose a printer and a normal paper size in Print settings (4×6, A4, Letter…). Receipt rolls and narrow strips aren't offered for flipbooks. The cards are about 45 × 34 mm: a 4×6 sheet holds 8, an A4 sheet 32. For example 30 frames plus both cards is 32 cards: one A4 sheet, or four 4×6 sheets. Each sheet counts toward your print limits.

Cut, stack and bind checklist

1

Cut

Cut along the thin grey lines with a paper trimmer, in rows, so the cards are all the same size.

2

Stack in order

The sheet is printed in order: left to right, top to bottom, sheet after sheet. Keep the cards in that order as you cut, with the cover on top and the back card at the bottom. The cards have no numbers, so cut one sheet at a time.

3

Bind

Tap the edges even, then staple along the left edge or use a binding clip. Flick the right edge with your thumb.

Setup → Print · Dock → Print requests

Guests ask for a print from their phone

A guest scans their QR code, sees their photo on their phone, and taps Ask for a print at the booth. The request appears on the booth for you to print. It is off until you turn it on.

  1. 1

    Switch it on

    In Setup → Share → Print settings tick Let guests ask for a print from their phone (it shows when printing is on for your code). The button then appears on the guest's photo page. On the website it needs the event folder to be set up first.

  2. 2

    See the requests

    A Print requests button appears in the dock with a number for new ones, and a message says when a guest asks. Tap it to see each photo with its time.

  3. 3

    Print or dismiss

    Tap Print to open the normal result screen with the Print button (the photo as it was saved, fitted to the paper; a photo strip prints two to a sheet). Tap Dismiss to remove it without printing. Prints count toward your print limits, copies included.

Good to know

A photo can only be asked for once, and a phone can ask a few times in ten minutes, so nobody can flood the booth. It works on the booth Wi-Fi (laptop app and offline Android app) and on the online gallery (website). GIFs and videos have no print button. Turn it off again and the button disappears.

Dock → Video guestbook

Video guestbook

A guest records a short video message on the booth, watches it, saves it and gets a QR code to keep it. Open it from the dock and hand the device over.

  1. 1

    Open and record

    Tap Video guestbook in the dock. The guest taps Record a message, sees 3, 2, 1, then talks for up to 30 seconds and taps Stop (it also stops by itself). The first time, the device asks to use the microphone: allow it.

  2. 2

    Watch, save or retake

    The message plays back. Save my message keeps it, Retake records it again.

  3. 3

    Scan the QR

    After saving, the guest sees a QR code to watch and keep the message, and Next guest clears the screen for the next one. The video is saved in the event folder (file name ends in _guestbook) and shows under Videos in the gallery.

Good to know

Messages are kept small (about 10 MB for 30 seconds) so they upload quickly. Without a microphone the message has no sound and the screen says so. On the website a message that can't upload yet is kept on the device and sent later, like a photo. Switch it off for a client with Hide video guestbook in Admin.

Part 5 · In Admin

Switching one off for a client

All extras are on by default. To hide one for a client, open the booking in Admin and tap Hide vintage looks, Hide photographer desk, Hide flipbook or Hide video guestbook (the same buttons say Give to turn it back on). When creating a code, untick it under Extras. An open booth picks the change up within about 5 minutes; the laptop and Android apps pick it up the next time they check their licence.

Help

If something goes wrong

ProblemTry this
A button isn't in the dockThe dock shows only during Photo Session, not while a session is running. The extra may also be switched off for your code: message us.
Photographer desk shows no photos from the folderOnly the laptop app watches a folder, and only for photos saved after you chose it. Check the camera software saves JPEG into that exact folder.
Quick shot says it has no pictureWait a second for the camera to start, and check the camera is allowed. It does not use a USB camera.
Photos sent from the phone don't appearLaptop app or Android app: the phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the laptop or tablet, and that device must be on a network (the Phone upload panel says so if it isn't). Website: show the Phone upload QR once on the booth, keep this page open, and wait about 10 seconds. If the phone says the link has expired, show the QR again.
Guests don't see the Look buttonIt is off until you tick Let guests choose a look in Setup → Extras → Filter, and it only exists on the guest screen of a traditional booth. It is hidden during layout choosing, the countdown and the result.
A Quick shot dot stays grey or orangeIt is saving or waiting for the internet. Orange photos are safe on the device and upload by themselves later.
Flipbook cards are cut offThe sheet needs a real page size. Print borderless if the printer offers it, and keep the printer's scaling at 100%.
No Flipbook button in Output modeIt is an extra on your code. Message us if it is missing.
