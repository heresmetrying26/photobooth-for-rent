Before you start

What you need

iPadOS 17 and newer natively accept USB-C webcams, so your DJI Osmo Pocket 3 plugs straight into your iPad and appears directly inside photobooth4u.

iPad & App Required

  • USB-C iPad running iPadOS 17.0 or newer (iPad 10th gen / A16, iPad Air 4+, iPad mini 6+, or USB-C iPad Pro).
  • photobooth4u iPad Offline App (Strongly Recommended) — talks directly to Apple’s native USB-C camera system for reliable 1080p live view, auto hot-plug switching, offline QR downloads, and AirPrint.
  • Important: iPad Safari frequently blocks or shows a black screen on USB-C cameras like the Osmo Pocket 3. Older Lightning iPads do not support USB webcams.

Camera, Power & Light Recommended

  • DJI Osmo Pocket 3 (update to the latest firmware via the DJI Mimo app before your event).
  • Powered USB-C Hub with Power Delivery (PD) + a 30W–65W wall charger so both the iPad and Pocket 3 stay powered all event.
  • USB-C data cable (the cable included with the Pocket 3 works great — avoid cheap charge-only cables).
  • Continuous front lighting (5500K daylight ring light or softbox) — webcam mode uses steady light, not a camera strobe/flash.

Install or update to iPad Offline App v1.1.29+

v1.1.29 adds automatic plug-and-play switching for the DJI Osmo Pocket 3, default 1080p framing on portrait iPads, selfie mirroring, and auto-reconnect if the USB cable is bumped.

Part 1 · Cables & Power

How to connect the Pocket 3 to your iPad

The Pocket 3 draws power over USB-C while streaming video. For full-length events (2+ hours), always route through a powered USB-C PD hub so neither device drains mid-party.

Event Setup (All-Day Power) Recommended

Plug the charger into the hub’s PD power port, the hub into your iPad, and the Pocket 3 into a USB data port on the hub.

Quick Test Setup Short Sessions

Works immediately for testing or short shoots — just start with both batteries charged to 100%.

Part 2 · On the Camera

Set up the DJI Osmo Pocket 3

Do these quick steps on the Pocket 3 every time you power on your booth.

  1. 1

    Mount the Pocket 3 at guest eye level

    Attach the included 1/4" Thread Handle (or Battery Handle) and mount the Pocket 3 on your tripod or booth stand facing the guests. Keep the camera body in open air — avoid sealing it inside a closed box so heat can escape.

  2. 2

    Power ON first, then plug in the USB-C cable

    Rotate the Pocket 3 screen to turn it on, wait 2 seconds for the gimbal to stabilize, then plug the USB-C cable into your iPad (or powered USB-C hub).

  3. 3

    Tap Webcam on the Pocket 3 screen

    A USB connection prompt will pop up on the Pocket 3 touchscreen. Tap Webcam (do not tap File Transfer or Charging).

    Missed the prompt or tapped the wrong button? Simply unplug the USB-C cable, wait 2 seconds, plug it back in, and tap Webcam.

  4. 4

    Keep the screen in Horizontal (Landscape) orientation

    Leave the Pocket 3 in standard horizontal orientation so it sends a full, clean 16:9 picture without black side bars. photobooth4u automatically crops to fit portrait 4R, 2×6 strips, or square layouts cleanly.

  5. 5

    Decide on ActiveTrack (Face Tracking)

    Pocket 3’s gimbal face tracking still works in Webcam mode! Choose what fits your event:

    • Group booths (3–6 guests): Turn ActiveTrack OFF and double-tap the joystick to center-lock the gimbal on your backdrop so it doesn’t wander toward one guest.
    • Solo / Couple booths: Leaving ActiveTrack ON gently keeps guests centered even if tall or short guests step up.

Why 1080p is better than 4K for multi-hour events

The Pocket 3’s 1-inch sensor gets noticeably warm during continuous 4K USB streaming and can trigger thermal auto-shutoff in warm indoor or outdoor venues. Running at 1080p (Full HD) keeps the camera much cooler while still producing sharp, clean photobooth prints all night.

Part 3 · Inside photobooth4u

Select the Pocket 3 in photobooth4u

Once the Pocket 3 is in Webcam mode, open photobooth4u on your iPad and verify these settings in Setup → Step 4 (Camera).

  1. 1

    Open the photobooth4u iPad app and allow camera access

    Launch the photobooth4u iPad Offline App. If iPadOS asks for Camera permission, tap Allow.

  2. 2

    Go to Setup → Step 4 · Camera and confirm Osmo Pocket 3

    The iPad app automatically switches to Osmo Pocket 3 when plugged in and set to Webcam mode. If the iPad’s built-in camera is still showing, tap the Camera dropdown and choose Osmo Pocket 3.

    Do not choose the “USB DSLR / Canon / Nikon / Sony” option — the Pocket 3 runs as a USB webcam, not a tethered DSLR.

  3. 3

    Check Live viewfinder resolution (Auto or 1080p)

    On the latest iPad app, Auto opens external USB-C cameras at crisp 1080p (Full HD) automatically — sharp for 4R & strip prints without overheating the Pocket 3. (On older app versions where Auto defaults to 720p, pick 1080p (Full HD) from the dropdown.)

  4. 4

    Tap Crop to layout & check Photo clean-up

    Tap Crop to layout directly under the live preview box so you see the exact framing that will print on your strip or 4R sheet. Under Webcam Image & Focus Controls, you can set Photo clean-up to Auto (or Strong in dim venues) for crisp print sharpening.

  5. 5

    Take one full test session

    Tap Start Booth, run through a full countdown and shot set, and check the finished print/preview for framing, brightness, and sharpness before guests arrive.

What works (and two things to keep in mind)

• Fully supported on iPad: Photo strips, 4R layouts, live filters, GIFs, Boomerangs, Video Guestbook, AirPrint, and offline guest QR downloads.

• Keep Background Swap / Green Screen OFF: On iPad, Background Swap switches to the browser camera engine, which can fall back to the iPad’s built-in camera instead of your Pocket 3. Use a physical backdrop with the Pocket 3.

• 360 Video Booth note: USB Webcam mode outputs at 25/30 fps, so live USB capture won’t give ultra-smooth 60/120 fps slow motion. If you also run a 360 arm with the Pocket 3, record 60/120 fps clips directly onto its microSD card and drop them into the laptop app’s 360 Inbox.

Part 4 · Event-Day Checklist & Quick Fixes

Event-Day Checklist & Troubleshooting

1

Lock Down Your iPad

Set Auto-Lock → Never, turn on Do Not Disturb + Orientation Lock, and enable Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility) so guests can’t exit the booth.

2

Use a Powered PD Hub

Keep wall power plugged into your USB-C hub’s PD port so neither the iPad nor the Pocket 3 drains during a long reception.

3

Steady 5500K Lighting

Place a ring light or softbox facing the guests (not behind them). Steady front light makes the Pocket 3’s 1-inch sensor look studio-sharp.

4

Keep It Cool & Centered

Use 1080p, keep airflow around the Pocket 3 body, and double-tap the joystick to center the gimbal before starting.

Problem Quick Fix
Osmo Pocket 3 doesn’t appear in the Camera menu (or shows a black screen) Use the photobooth4u iPad Offline App (not Safari), confirm a USB-C iPad on iPadOS 17+, and keep Background Swap OFF. Try powering the Pocket 3 ON first, replugging the original DJI USB-C data cable, and tapping Webcam immediately on the Pocket 3 screen.
Live view freezes or disconnects mid-event Use a powered USB-C PD hub (the iPad port alone may drop power under load). Check if the Pocket 3 feels very hot — switch Live viewfinder resolution to 1080p and give it airflow. Unplug and replug, tap Webcam, and tap Try Again.
Black bars appear on the left & right of the photo Rotate the Pocket 3 screen back to Horizontal (Landscape) orientation and tap Crop to layout in photobooth4u.
Camera keeps turning away from the group Turn ActiveTrack OFF on the Pocket 3 screen, then double-tap the joystick to lock the gimbal centered on your backdrop.
Photos look slightly soft or dark indoors Change Live viewfinder resolution from Auto to 1080p (Full HD), add a continuous ring light or softbox in front of guests, and set Photo clean-up to Auto or Strong under Webcam Image & Focus Controls.
Selected “USB DSLR” and got a “No camera found” error Do not use the USB DSLR option — that is only for Canon, Nikon, and Sony DSLRs. Choose Osmo Pocket 3 directly in the Camera dropdown instead.
