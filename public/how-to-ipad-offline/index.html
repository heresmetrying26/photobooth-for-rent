Before you start

What you need

Because the iPad app is not yet on the public App Store, it is installed once from a Windows PC or Mac using Sideloadly (100% free, takes ~5 minutes).

For Installing the App (Once)

  • iPad or iPhone running iPadOS / iOS 15.0 or newer.
  • Windows PC or Mac + USB cable to connect the device.
  • Sideloadly installed on your computer (free tool used to sideload the .ipa onto your iPad):
    • Mac: Download the macOS .dmg from sideloadly.io and drag Sideloadly to Applications. If macOS shows "Sideloadly" Not Opened, click Done → System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
    • Windows: Run the Sideloadly installer, and also install Apple's non-Microsoft-Store iTunes linked on sideloadly.io if Sideloadly doesn't see your iPad.
  • Your Apple ID (a free regular Apple ID works).
  • An active photobooth4u rental code with the Offline App add-on enabled.

For Running a DSLR Booth

  • Canon EOS, Nikon, or Sony Alpha camera + its USB data cable.
  • Powered USB-C Hub (with wall charger / Power Delivery) so the iPad stays charged while running the camera. (Lightning iPads need the official Apple Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter with power.)
  • Pocket Wi-Fi router or phone hotspot (no internet load needed) if guests will scan QR codes to download photos.

Part 1 · ~5 Minutes

Install Sideloadly & Sideload the iPad Offline App

Follow these steps on your Windows PC or Mac to install Sideloadly and load photobooth4u onto your iPad.

  1. 1

    Install Sideloadly & download the .ipa file

    On your computer, download and install Sideloadly (Windows: also install Apple's non-Microsoft-Store iTunes from sideloadly.io; Mac: open the .dmg and drag Sideloadly into Applications). Then click Download iPad App (.ipa) at the top of this page to save photobooth4u-kiosk-ipad.ipa. Do not unzip the .ipa file.

    On Mac? If macOS blocks Sideloadly on first launch with "Sideloadly" Not Opened, click Done (do not click Move to Trash), then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, and confirm with your Mac password or Touch ID.

  2. 2

    Connect your iPad to your computer

    Plug the iPad into your Windows PC or Mac using a USB cable. Unlock the iPad and tap Trust This Computer if a pop-up appears.

  3. 3

    Install with Sideloadly

    Open Sideloadly on your computer. Confirm your iPad appears under iDevice, click the big IPA icon (or drag photobooth4u-kiosk-ipad.ipa into it), type your Apple ID email, and click Start. Enter your Apple ID password and 2FA code if prompted.

    Tip: If Advanced Options is open like in the screenshot below, leave the checkboxes as-is (keep Use automatic bundle ID checked) and click the blue Advanced Options bar to collapse it so the Start button shows up.

  4. 4

    Trust your Apple ID on the iPad

    Once Sideloadly says Done, the photobooth4u app appears on your iPad Home Screen (it may show the white grid app icon). Before opening it, go to iPad Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID under Developer App, then tap Trust.

  5. 5

    Turn on Developer Mode (iPadOS 16+)

    Open iPad Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode and switch it ON. Your iPad will restart; after rebooting, tap Turn On when asked.

  6. 6

    Activate your rental code

    Connect the iPad to Wi-Fi once, open photobooth4u, enter your PB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX code, and tap Activate. After activation, the booth works completely offline — you'll see the ready Tap to Start booth screen.

Easy Future Updates (No Sideloadly Needed!)

Once you are on v1.0.4+, most booth updates (new layouts, themes, camera fixes, QR sharing & printing improvements) install straight inside the app: connect to Wi-Fi, open Setup → 6 Share → App updates, and tap Check for updates → Update now (~2 seconds, no computer needed).

Important: Free Apple ID 7-Day Refresh

Apps sideloaded with a free Apple ID stay active for 7 days. Before a weekend event, open Sideloadly on your computer (over Wi-Fi or USB) and click Start again (~30 seconds) without deleting the app to reset the 7-day timer — all your frames, designs, and saved photos stay intact. Part 2

Connect your DSLR or Mirrorless Camera

Plug your camera straight into the iPad through a powered USB-C hub for live view, real shutter control, and external flash/strobe sync.

[DSLR / Mirrorless Camera] <-- USB Data Cable --> [Powered USB-C Hub + Charger] <--> [iPad]
Camera BrandRequired Camera Settings Before Plugging In
Canon EOS
(DSLRs, M & R series)		Mode dial on M, Av, Tv, or P (not Auto/Video). Image quality set to JPEG (L/M/S or RAW+JPEG). In menu: Live View shoot: Enable, Wi-Fi: Off, Auto Power Off: Off or long.
Nikon
(D-series & Z-series)		Mode dial on M, A, S, or P. Set USB mode to MTP/PTP (Setup menu). Image quality: JPEG Fine (or Normal). Turn Wi-Fi / Airplane mode properly off for USB.
Sony Alpha
(A7, A6xxx, ZV series)		Turn ON PC Remote (MENU → Network → PC Remote Function → PC Remote: On and/or MENU → Setup → USB → USB Connection → PC Remote). Set Still Img. Save Dest. to PC Only (or PC+Camera) and Release w/o Card: Enable. File Format: JPEG (or RAW+JPEG).
Olympus / Other PTPSet USB mode to Tether / Control / PTP and JPEG format. (Note: Cameras that do not stream live view over USB PTP can use an HDMI capture card or the iPad's built-in camera.)
  1. 1

    Plug in and power on the camera

    Turn the camera on first, then connect it to the powered USB-C hub plugged into your iPad. If iPadOS asks to allow photobooth4u to access the USB camera, tap Allow.

  2. 2

    Pick the DSLR in Setup

    In the booth, go to Setup → 3 Camera and choose DSLR / Mirrorless camera (USB). Live view from your camera will appear on the iPad screen.

  3. 3

    Take a test shot with your flash

    Run a test session to confirm autofocus, flash/strobe firing, and photo transfer speed. Use a dummy battery or AC adapter on the DSLR for long events.

Part 3

Offline Guest QR Sharing, Printing & Kiosk Lock

📡 Offline Guest QR Downloads

No internet needed at the venue:

  • Connect the iPad to a pocket Wi-Fi router or a phone hotspot (data/SIM can be off).
  • In Setup → 6 Share, type that Wi-Fi name and password so guests get a Join Wi-Fi QR.
  • Guests scan the Wi-Fi QR first, then scan their Photo QR to save pictures straight from the iPad!

🖨️ AirPrint, LAN Receipt & Photo Backup

  • AirPrint (Silent Auto-Print): Print straight to any wireless AirPrint printer (Canon SELPHY CP1300/CP1500, Epson, DNP WPS Pro). Pick a Silent AirPrint printer once in Print settings to skip pop-up dialogs every session.
  • LAN Thermal Receipt Printing: Add a 58mm/80mm LAN receipt printer IP (port 9100) in Print settings for direct thermal booth strips.
  • Photo Backup: Every photo strip, animated GIF, and video saves in Files → On My iPad → photobooth4u and copies straight to the iPad Photos app.

🔒 Lock the iPad for Guests (Guided Access)

Turn on Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access and set a passcode. Once your booth is ready on the Welcome screen, triple-click the iPad's Top/Side button to start Guided Access so guests cannot exit the app or open Settings.
