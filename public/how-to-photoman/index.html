photobooth4u · Roving Photoman

Shoot anywhere. Frame, edit and print from the laptop.

Two ways to get photos in: a phone that scans a QR, or a DSLR tethered to an Android or iPhone. One place to finish them: the Photographer Desk on the laptop base station—with 2-template Portrait Polaroid + Landscape auto-switching, Favorite templates, an independent Template Maker, and Pick-to-Send + Brightness on the phone bridge.

Option A

Send photos from a phone by scanning the QR

No app to install. The phone opens a small page served by the laptop and sends its photos straight into the queue.

  1. On the laptop, open Connect & QR

    Look at Booth & Camera Links. You want a green tick on Roving DSLR / Phone QR ready. If you see Booth Wi-Fi / LAN address needed for Phone QR, connect the laptop to the booth Wi-Fi first, then press Refresh.

  2. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi

    It must be the same network as the laptop.

  3. Scan the QR with the phone's normal camera app

    A page titled Photoman Control & Queue opens in the phone's browser. (Copy link under the QR gives you the same address to send by message.)

  4. Send photos

    Tap 📸 Take a Photo to shoot with the phone, or 🖼️ Choose Photos to send several from the gallery at once. A progress bar shows each upload.

  5. Watch them arrive

    Each photo appears in the laptop's Queue within a couple of seconds. A 📱 badge marks photos that came in over Wi-Fi from a phone or the Bridge.

On the website version (no laptop app) the QR is different: the phone uploads into the event's online folder and photos show up on the desk about every 10 seconds. The phone remote in section 9 is a laptop-app feature.

Photographer Desk

  1. Set the camera

    Image quality JPEG (or RAW + JPEG), camera mode dial not on movie, camera Wi-Fi and Bluetooth off, a memory card inside. Canon R50 / R-series: set Choose USB connection app to Photo Import/Remote Control. Sony: set USB Connection to PC Remote. See the Camera Setup table or Roving DSLR Guide (PDF).

  2. Install Roving DSLR Bridge on your phone

    Download Android v1.1.4+ (.apk) or iPhone/iPad (.ipa) from /downloads (full install steps in the Roving DSLR Bridge Install Guide). It is free and needs no booth code on the phone.

  3. Pair it with the laptop

    Phone on the booth Wi-Fi. On the laptop open Photographer desk → Connect & QR. In the phone app tap 📷 Scan QR Code on Laptop and point it at the laptop screen until it shows ✅ Connected! Base station ready.

  4. Plug in the camera

    Connect the USB data cable from the phone to the camera and switch the camera on. If Android asks to open the app when the device is connected, choose Always allow. The status card goes from WAITING to READY and shows the camera model and battery. Tap 📸 Test Shutter once to verify.

  5. Choose Send Mode & Brightness (Android v1.1.4+)

    In the 🎛️ Send Mode & Brightness card:

    • ⚡ Auto-Send All: Every shutter click uploads straight to the laptop right away (default).
    • ☑️ Pick to Send: Shots stay saved safely on the phone first. Tap ☑️ Review to browse uncropped thumbnails with ▯ Port / ▭ Land badges, adjust brightness live, and tap 📤 Send to Laptop or 🗑️ Skip—or tap ☑ Multi-Pick to check and send only your chosen shots.
    • ☀️ Brightness (-30% to +40%): Lighten dim venue photos on the phone before uploading (per-photo live slider or a default boost). At 0%, the untouched original camera JPEG is sent with zero re-encoding.

  6. Optional: Pocket Mode & shoot

    Tap 🔒 Pocket Mode before putting the phone in your pocket—the screen dims and ignores accidental taps while keeping USB capture and offline auto-retry alive (tap 3 times to unlock). If Wi-Fi drops, shots queue safely on phone storage and send automatically when Wi-Fi returns.

Already using the camera's own software on the laptop? (EOS Utility, Imaging Edge, FTP.) In Connect & QR press Choose folder and pick the folder that software saves into. New files in that folder join the queue. You can also drop photos onto the desk or press Choose photos.

Two photographers? Both scan the same QR. Their photos merge into the one queue.

On the laptop

Templates, Favorites, Orientation Auto-Switch & Designer

Open 🖼️ Template & Auto or 🎨 Template Maker. Everything you design here stays 100% separate from the main Photobooth 1 layout.

⭐ Favorite Templates & 1-Tap Quick-Switch

Stop scrolling through a long dropdown between solo Polaroids and group Landscape shots:

  • Tap ☆ Favorite beside any template (or inside Template Maker) to pin it as ★ Favorited.
  • Pinned templates appear in the dedicated ⭐ Favorite Templates section, in a ⭐ QUICK SWITCH 1-tap bar right at the top of the Queue, at the top of template dropdowns, and synced on the Phone Remote.

📐 Auto-switch by orientation (Portrait / Landscape)

The classic 2-template roving photoman workflow—hands-free:

  • Tick 📐 Auto-switch template by orientation and pick your ▯ Portrait template (e.g. 4R 2-up Polaroid) and ▭ Landscape template (e.g. 4R Landscape 1-Spot).
  • Manual picking: Tap a portrait shot and it switches straight to your Portrait template; tap a landscape shot and it switches straight to your Landscape template (even without hitting Clear first).
  • ⚡ Auto-frame ON: Portrait and landscape photos fill separate buckets automatically so solo portraits pair into 2-up Polaroids while group shots print in 4R Landscape—never mixed together.

🎨 Independent Photoman Template Maker

Press ✨ New Template (or open the 🎨 Template Maker tab) to freely design your own Polaroid and Landscape frames independently of the main booth:

  • Starter presets: 📸 4R 2-up Polaroid (4R split into two 3×4" portrait Polaroids), 🖼️ 4R Landscape 1-Spot, 💐 2-up Floral Polaroid, 💐 Landscape Floral, 🎞️ 4R 2-Shot Film, grids, strips, or ⬆️ Import PNG Frame (auto-detects transparent photo spots + 🔍 Detect spots).
  • Full design controls: 24 background styles, sprinkles, metallic accents, decorative borders & florals with ✂️ 2-up half-sheet frame decor, built-in sticker packs + custom PNG character/logo uploads, rotated text (0° / +90° / -90° for side studio credits), and text outlines.
  • 🪞 Mirror Half: Select any sticker or text on the left Polaroid and tap Mirror Half to copy it symmetrically onto the right half in one click.
  • 🔄 Matching Pair: Design your 2-up Polaroid (or 4R Landscape) first, then click Matching Pair—it automatically creates the matching companion layout with the same theme, colours, fonts, and stickers, pins both to ⭐ Favorites, and assigns them to Portrait/Landscape auto-switch!

Template dropdown groups & multi-spot fill

The Photoman template list has five groups (the number shows how many spots it needs):

  • ⭐ Favorites: your pinned go-to templates at the very top.
  • Same as the booth: mirrors whatever layout the main booth uses.
  • 🎨 My Photoman templates: designs built in Photoman Template Maker.
  • Built-in layouts & My frames: booth layouts and uploaded PNG frames.

When a template has 2+ spots (like 4R 2-up Polaroid), choose Separate photos (tap 2 different portrait shots for Spot 1 and Spot 2) or One photo fills every spot (1 portrait prints twice so you cut the 4R in half and give 2 copies).

⚡ Auto-frame new photos

Switch it on and the desk does the work: each new photo (or each pair of portraits / single landscape when orientation auto-switch is on) is placed in its matching template, gets its Guest QR, and is printed if Print after each session is on.

The rules

  • Only photos that arrive after you switch it on. Photos already in the list are left alone.
  • With Orientation auto-switch ON, portraits wait in their own bucket (e.g. 2 portraits → 4R 2-up Polaroid) while landscapes print immediately on 4R Landscape 1-Spot.
  • It pauses while the Edit photo window is open, while a print is being made, and (inside the booth window) while the booth is busy.

Want to choose which photo goes where?

Just start tapping photos in the queue. Auto-frame waits for you and does not take your photos in arrival order. It carries on 30 seconds after your last tap. To stop it completely, untick Auto-frame new photos.

📺 Also show result on Booth Screen: when the booth is idle, finished Photoman prints also appear on the main booth screen for guests to see. It is skipped while a guest is taking photos.

Choose, check, touch up

Pick photos, read the preview, edit

Picking photos & telling Portrait vs. Landscape apart

  • Uncropped thumbnails & orientation badges: Every queue photo keeps its true shape on a dark frame with a purple ▯ Portrait or teal ▭ Landscape badge, so you never guess whether a shot is vertical or horizontal.
  • Filter pills: Use All, ▯ Portrait, or ▭ Landscape above the queue to focus on one orientation at a time.
  • 1-tap selection & spot swap: Tap a photo to assign Spot 1, tap a second for Spot 2 (tap again to un-pick). When 2+ spots are filled, tap ⇄ Swap spots under the preview to flip left ↔ right instantly.
  • Auto-switch on tap: With Auto-switch template by orientation enabled, tapping a Landscape photo while Portrait photos are selected switches straight to your Landscape template (and vice versa)—no need to hit Clear first.
  • Quick-switch bar: Your pinned ⭐ Favorite Templates sit right at the top of the Queue for 1-tap switching anytime.
  • If you pick fewer photos than the template has spots, the last photo repeats in the empty spots (marked (repeats)). Photos already printed are dimmed and marked Printed, and can still be reprinted anytime.

Live Output Preview

The preview on the right is exactly what will be printed. The two tabs switch between 🖼️ Framed + QR (Make print) and 📄 Original (Print original). Under it, small chips show which photo sits in which spot (Spot 1 ← #1). The caption on the picture tells you what you are looking at:

✓ Just made: … · Session #12This is the print that was just made. Tap a photo to preview another one.
⚡ Auto-frame preview — waiting for new camera photosAuto-frame is on and nothing new has arrived yet.

✎ Edit photo Optional

Pick a photo, then press ✎ Edit photo. If you picked several, it edits the last one you tapped.

Quick looks

Original, Brighten, Warm, Cool, Vivid, Matte, Black & white.

Sliders

Brightness, Contrast, Warmth (cool ↔ warm), Colour (saturation).

Buttons

Hold to compare shows the untouched photo while you hold it. Reset clears your changes. Apply to print + QR keeps them. Cancel closes without changing anything.

Once you apply an edit, both the print and the photo the guest gets by QR use the edited version. The original file in the Roving inbox stays untouched. Edited photos show an ✎ Edited badge in the queue.

Finish

Print and share with the guest

🖼️ Make print + QR

Puts the picked photos in the template, saves the photo for sharing, creates the Guest Photo QR, and prints it if Print after each session is on. Otherwise press 🖨️ Print on the result card.

📄 Print original

Prints the picked photo as it is, with no frame, filling the paper. The guest's QR still opens the framed version.

The "Ready to Print & Share" card

After a print is made, a green card appears under the preview with the session number (Session #12).

On the cardWhat it does
Guest Photo QRThe guest scans it with their phone to see and save their photo. It says Preparing QR… for a moment, then Scan to save.
Booth Wi-Fi QRShown when the laptop is running the booth Wi-Fi. The guest scans this first to join the Wi-Fi, then scans the photo QR. It shows the Wi-Fi name underneath.

If Windows shows a small "Waiting for printer connection…" window while printing, the printer has not answered yet. Check that it is on and connected, press Cancel, then use 🖨️ Print on the result card to try again.

Laptop app only

Run the desk from a phone Remote

The page a phone opens from the QR is also a remote control. Open it on any phone on the booth Wi-Fi to run the desk while you stand with your guests.

🖼️ Photoman Template, ⭐ Favorites & Orientation

Switch templates in 1 tap from the synced ⭐ Favorites bar, tap ☆ to star/unstar a template, toggle 📐 Auto-switch by orientation, choose spot fill, and switch ⚡ Auto-frame on or off—the laptop follows immediately.

📥 Uncropped Laptop Queue + Filters

Uncropped thumbnails with ▯ Port / ▭ Land badges and All / Portrait / Landscape filter pills. Tap photos in order, then 🖼️ Make Print + QR or 🖨️ Print Original.

🎉 Latest Framed Print & Guest QR

Shows the finished print and its QR so you can hold the phone up to the guest. 🖨️ Print Copy reprints, ⏭️ Next Guest clears it.

📱 Send Photos from This Phone

📸 Take a Photo and 🖼️ Choose Photos, exactly as in option A.

The laptop app must be running and the phone must stay on the same Wi-Fi. If the booth is busy with a guest session, a Make Print sent from the phone waits and runs when the booth is free (for up to a minute). If the page says it cannot reach the laptop, scan the QR again.

Ready-made set-ups

Four ways to run it

1 · 2-Template Photoman (Polaroid + Landscape)

Design a 4R 2-up Polaroid in Template Maker, click 🔄 Matching Pair to generate its 4R Landscape 1-Spot twin, and switch on 📐 Auto-switch by orientation.

Shoot solo/duo portraits vertically and group shots horizontally—each photo automatically routes to its matching template (and you can also use Pick to Send + Brightness on the Android Bridge phone).

2 · Hands-free prints

Template(s) ready, ⚡ Auto-frame on, Print after each session on.

Every photo (or pair of portraits) is framed, printed and given a QR automatically. Hold up the Phone Remote or desk result card so the guest can scan.

3 · Pick & touch up the best shot

Turn on ☑️ Pick to Send on the phone bridge (or leave Auto-frame off on the laptop).

Skip blinked shots, brighten dark venue photos with live preview, optionally tweak colours with ✎ Edit photo, then tap Make print + QR.

4 · Booth and Photoman together

Use ↗ Pop Out Window for the desk.

The main booth runs guest sessions while Photoman prints in its own window. Photoman's templates and designs never change the booth's layout.

When something does not work

Troubleshooting

What you seeTry this
The phone cannot open the QR pageThe phone and laptop must be on the same Wi-Fi. On Windows, set the Wi-Fi network profile to Private. In Connect & QR the row Booth Wi-Fi / LAN address needed means the laptop has no local address yet: join the booth Wi-Fi and press Refresh. Then scan the QR again.
Photos stay on phone instead of arriving on laptopIn Roving DSLR Bridge v1.1.4+, check if ☑️ Pick to Send is active—shots wait for you to tap ☑️ Review → 📤 Send to Laptop (or switch back to ⚡ Auto-Send All for instant streaming).
Photos do not reach the queue (in Auto-Send mode)Check the Bridge status says READY and press 📸 Test Shutter. If the desk is popped out into its own window, that window receives the photos (the booth's desk then says Pop-out Window Active), so look there.
Bridge stays on WAITING (no DSLR)Unplug the cable and check the OTG end is in the phone. Switch the camera off, wait 3 seconds, on again. Close the camera menus. Canon R50/R-series: Choose USB connection app → Photo Import/Remote Control. Sony: USB Connection → PC Remote.
Camera took the shot but nothing arrivedThe camera may be saving RAW only. Set image quality to JPEG (or RAW + JPEG).
Uploaded PNG frame didn't detect photo spotsIn Template Maker, click 🔍 Detect spots next to PNG Overlay (or tick Punch out pure white areas if your PNG has solid white cut-outs instead of transparency).
Auto-frame made no printCheck the paper and printer in Setup → Printer, and that Print after each session is on. Without it, the print is made and shown but you press 🖨️ Print yourself.
The preview shows a sample pictureNothing is picked and nothing is waiting. Tap a photo, or wait for Auto-frame's photos. After a print, the preview shows Just made until you press Done or pick another photo.
The Guest QR does not openIf the booth runs its own Wi-Fi, the guest must first join it with the Booth Wi-Fi QR shown on the result card.
