How to print
on a Citizen printer

For the Citizen CX-02, CX-02W, CY-02 and CZ-01 dye-sublimation photo printers. They connect by USB to a Windows or Mac laptop, and the booth prints to them like any other printer. Set up once, then test before the event.

Part 1 · Check first

Where the booth works with these printers

All four connect by USB 2.0 and have Windows (7, 10, 11) and Mac drivers on Citizen's website. None lists Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or AirPrint, so the printer is always plugged into a laptop.

Part 2 · Once

Install the printer on the laptop

  1. 1

    Install the driver

    Download the Windows or Mac driver for your model from its page on Citizen's website, install it, then restart the laptop.

  2. 2

    Load paper and ribbon

    Use Citizen's own media for your model, and match the paper size to the ribbon. Load the roll and ribbon as shown in the printer's quick-start guide.

  3. 3

    Connect it

    Turn the printer on and plug its USB cable straight into the laptop (no hub). Use the cable that came with it, or a short good-quality one.

  4. 4

    Set the paper and finish

    Windows: Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners → your Citizen → Printing preferences. Mac: System Settings → Printers & Scanners, then the print dialog's options. Choose the paper size for the loaded roll (for example 4×6), and the finish (glossy or matte). Leave it borderless if the driver offers it.

  5. 5

    Test it

    Print one photo from the Photos app (Windows) or Preview (Mac) at the same paper size to check the printer works.

Part 3 · In the booth

Choose the printer and paper

  1. 1

    Laptop app or Print Helper

    Laptop app: nothing to open. Online booth in Chrome: open the Print Helper and keep its window open during the event. Always open the booth from photobooth4u.netlify.app.

  2. 2

    Pick a printer per paper size

    In Photo Session → Print settings, tap the paper size (for example 4×6 in) and choose your Citizen in the printer list. The booth remembers it.

  3. 3

    Need another size?

    Sizes such as 4×4, 6×9 or 8×12 aren't in the standard list. Lifetime codes can add an own size in the print panel; other codes: message us and we'll add it to your code.

  4. 4

    Auto-print (optional)

    After one good test, tick Auto-print every session so each guest's print starts by itself.

Part 4 · If you print 2×6 strips

Photo strips

Automatic strip cutting is not confirmed

Citizen's pages for these four models don't mention a 2-inch strip cut. The booth puts two 2×6 strips side by side on one 4×6 sheet. Unless your driver has a cut option for it, cut the sheet in half by hand with a paper trimmer. Check the driver's printing preferences for a “2 inch cut” style option, and tell us if you find one.

Part 5 · Before the event

Test and fixes
