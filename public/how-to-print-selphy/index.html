Part 1 · Android Tablet & Phone App

Direct Printing from Android to Canon SELPHY

Choose Option A (USB OTG Cable) for events — it never drops out, needs no router, and lets your Android tablet keep Booth Wi-Fi ON for offline guest QR photo downloads.

Option A · USB OTG Cable Best for Events · Offline

  1. Load the matching paper cassette (up to 18 sheets) and ink cassette into the Canon SELPHY and power it ON.
  2. Plug a USB OTG adapter (or USB-C to USB-C / Mini-USB data cable) between your Android tablet and the SELPHY’s side USB port.
  3. When Android pops up “Allow photobooth4u to access the USB device?”, tick Always open and tap OK.
  4. In photobooth4u, open Setup → 4 Printer and choose paper size Selphy postcard (100×148).
  5. Under Printer 1, tap Canon SELPHY CP1500 (USB) · Photo (SELPHY) (or CP1300/CP1200/CP910) so it shows a checkmark ✓.
  6. Tap Test print to verify borderless colour output, then turn on Send a print automatically after each session.

Option B · Wi-Fi Direct (No Cable) Same Wi-Fi Router

  1. On the SELPHY screen, open Wi-Fi Settings → Connection Settings → Via Wi-Fi Router and join your Wi-Fi network. (Avoid venue “guest” Wi-Fi that blocks device-to-device discovery.)
  2. Connect your Android tablet to the exact same Wi-Fi. (Switch Booth Wi-Fi OFF while using router Wi-Fi.)
  3. In photobooth4u, go to Setup → 4 Printer → select Selphy postcard (100×148).
  4. Tap Find Wi-Fi & USB printers. After ~10 seconds, your Canon SELPHY appears — tap it to select.
  5. Not showing? Check the SELPHY’s IP address on its LCD screen, type it into the IP box, and tap Add / Search.
  6. Tap Test print. The app sends a zero-margin borderless photo straight to the SELPHY with no pop-up dialog.

Android Tip (Xiaomi / Redmi / Oppo / Realme / Vivo Tablets & Phones)

Some Android brands disable USB OTG by default to save battery. If your SELPHY does not show up when plugged in via USB, open Android Settings → Additional settings → OTG connection and switch it ON, then unplug and replug the cable.

Part 2 · Windows & Mac Laptop

Direct Printing from Laptop to Canon SELPHY

Install the photobooth4u Desktop App from photobooth4u.netlify.app/downloads (Windows 10/11 or Mac Apple Silicon / Intel). Works 100% offline once activated — choose either USB Cable or Driverless Wi-Fi.

Option A · USB Cable (Windows & Mac) Best for Events · Offline

  1. Load the matching paper & ink cassette into the Canon SELPHY, switch it ON, and connect it to your laptop with a USB data cable.
  2. Open the photobooth4u Desktop App → go to Setup → 4 Printer → select Selphy postcard (100×148) (or 4R 4×6).
  3. Open the Printer 1 dropdown:
    • Driverless USB: pick Canon SELPHY CP1500 (USB direct) / CP1300 (USB direct) — works right away with no Canon driver!
    • Already installed in Windows/Mac? Pick your system queue (e.g. Canon SELPHY CP1500). photobooth4u automatically uses it cleanly without USB conflicts.
  4. Not listed yet? Tap Refresh printer list (or open Find a Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or Instax printer → Find Wi-Fi / network printers, which scans USB too).
  5. Tap Test print (or run 1 test session), then turn ON Send a print automatically after each session.

Option B · Wi-Fi Direct (Zero Driver!) Wireless · 1-Click

  1. Connect your Canon SELPHY (CP1500 / CP1300 / CP1200 / CP910) to the same Wi-Fi as your Windows or Mac laptop.
  2. Open the photobooth4u Desktop App → go to Setup → 4 Printer → pick Selphy postcard (100×148).
  3. Expand Find a Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or Instax printer → click Find Wi-Fi / network printers (~10 seconds).
  4. Next to your Canon SELPHY, click Use this printer. No Windows or Mac driver installation is required — the app sends borderless photos directly over IPP!
  5. Using Chrome/Edge Web Booth instead of the Desktop App? Install the SELPHY in Windows/Mac Printers & Scanners, open photobooth4u Print Helper, turn on Print directly with Print Helper (skip dialog), and select your SELPHY.

Using Windows Laptop Hotspot for Offline Guest QR Downloads?

If your Windows laptop is running the built-in Laptop Hotspot (so guests can scan the QR code with zero venue internet), connect your Canon SELPHY by USB cable! That keeps Wi-Fi dedicated to guest phones while every print flows reliably over USB.

Part 4 · Built-In Protections & Event Checklist

What photobooth4u Handles Automatically + Pre-Event Checklist

No Yellow-Only Stalls

Sending multiple copies in one batch can freeze a CP1500/CP1300 mid-print. photobooth4u automatically splits multi-copy prints into one photo per job, back-to-back.

Full-Bleed Borderless

Automatically requests zero margins over Wi-Fi IPP and full-bleed 1248×1872 px raster over USB — avoiding unwanted white borders around postcards.

Live Printer Status

Over direct USB, photobooth4u monitors the SELPHY’s live status and alerts you immediately if paper is out, ink ribbon is empty, or cassettes don’t match.

1

Check Your USB Cable

CP1500: uses a USB-C port on the side.
CP1300 / CP1200 / CP910: uses a Mini-USB port. Always use a data-capable USB cable (not a charge-only cable).

2

15 cm Rear Clearance

Leave at least 15 cm (6 inches) of clear space behind the SELPHY so the paper never hits a wall or backdrop during its 4 passes.

3

Max 18 Sheets per Tray

Don’t overfill the paper cassette (max 18 sheets). Glossy printing side faces UP, and avoid touching the glossy surface with bare fingers.

4

Match Paper & Ink

Postcard paper requires a Postcard ink cassette (KP-108IN / RP-108). Each ink cartridge prints 36 or 54 sheets — keep spare cassettes ready.

Part 5 · Quick Fixes

Troubleshooting Checklist

ProblemQuick Fix
Android says “Tap to allow USB access” Tap the printer row in the list → tick Always open → tap OK.
SELPHY not listed over USB (Android) Unplug and replug the cable. Verify it’s a data cable + OTG adapter (not charge-only). On Xiaomi/Redmi/Oppo/Realme/Vivo, switch ON Settings → Additional settings → OTG connection.
Laptop says “USB port is busy” or “Could not open over USB” Your Windows/Mac already has a system printer queue holding the USB port. Select the system queue name (e.g. Canon SELPHY CP1500) in Printer 1 instead — or switch to Wi-Fi Direct.
Wi-Fi SELPHY not found during scan Confirm both device and SELPHY are on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest/isolated venue network). Turn Booth Wi-Fi OFF on Android. Or type the SELPHY’s IP address from its LCD screen into the IP box and tap Search.
White border around printed photo Set paper size to Selphy postcard (100×148) and make sure Borderless is ON. On the SELPHY’s own LCD menu, ensure Borders is set to Borderless. Fine-tune if needed with Edges (margins).
Prints look slightly dark or warm Open Lightness & Colour Correction inside Printer 1 and bump Lightness by +10 to +20. This brightens printed output only — guest QR/digital photos stay untouched.
“Media/job mismatch” or “Paper feed error” Push the paper cassette in firmly until it clicks, verify max 18 sheets, and confirm the paper cassette size matches the loaded ink cassette and the selected paper size in photobooth4u.
Multi-copy print stalls or prints only yellow Keep SELPHY or CP1500 / CP1300 in the printer name so photobooth4u sends each copy as a separate single-page job automatically.
