How to print
on a Sinfonia printer

For the Sinfonia (formerly Shinko) Color Stream CS2, S2145 / S3 and the 8-inch S1245 / CE1 dye-sublimation printers. They connect by USB to a Windows or Mac laptop. Set up once, then test before the event.

Part 1 · Check first

Where the booth works

Sinfonia (formerly Shinko) Color Stream printers connect by USB 2.0 to a Windows or Mac laptop. The booth prints to them like any printer that has a driver.

How you run the boothWorks?
Windows / Mac laptop app (offline)Yes. Prints straight to the printer, no pop-up, no internet needed.
Windows / Mac laptop, online booth in ChromeYes. Open the Print Helper so the booth prints without a pop-up.
iPad or Android tablet booth (online)Through a laptop. Plug the printer into a laptop running photobooth4u.netlify.app/print-station/; on the tablet choose Printer → Send prints to → Laptop print station.
Android tablet app (APK)No. The Android app prints only to Bluetooth thermal receipt printers.

Part 2 · Your model

Models and drivers

ModelWhat the listings say
Color Stream CS2 (CHC-S6145)Dye-sub, USB 2.0, 300 dpi. Prints 4×6, 5×7 and 6×8, and cuts a 4×6 into two 2×6 strips by itself. Driver listed for Windows 2000 / XP / Vista / 7 / 10 (32 and 64-bit) and Mac OS X 10.5 and later.
S2145 / S34×6 workhorse. The S2145 is listed for 2×6, 4×6 and 5×7 prints; the S3 is its successor (about 8 seconds for a 4×6, 6 in maximum width).
S1245 and CE1The 8-inch models: the S1245 is an 8×10 printer, and the CE1 is sold with 8×10 and 8×12 print packs.

Drivers come from Sinfonia's distributor site (KG Digital, a login is needed) and on the CD in the box. Sinfonia lists Mac driver support from macOS 10.6 and later; the CS2 listing names Windows up to 10. Install the one for your model and check it works with your laptop's Windows or macOS version before the event.

Check the model name before you buy

Sinfonia's photo printers are the Color Stream CS2 and the S-series. A printer called SP-300 or SP-310 is a different kind of printer (the name belongs to label printers), so confirm the exact model with the seller.

Part 3 · Once

Install the printer

  1. 1

    Install the driver

    Use the driver from the CD in the box or from your Sinfonia distributor, install it, then restart the laptop.

  2. 2

    Load media

    Use Sinfonia's print kit for your model: the paper and ribbon must be the same size.

  3. 3

    Connect it

    Turn it on and plug the USB cable straight into the laptop (no hub). Wait for the printer to appear in Windows or macOS.

  4. 4

    Set the paper

    Windows: Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners → your printer → Printing preferences. Mac: the print dialog's options. Choose the paper size for the loaded kit (for example 4×6).

  5. 5

    Test it

    Print one photo at the same size from the Photos app (Windows) or Preview (Mac).

Part 4 · In the booth

Choose the printer and paper

  1. 1

    Laptop app or Print Helper

    Laptop app: nothing to open. Online booth in Chrome: open the Print Helper and keep its window open. Always open the booth from photobooth4u.netlify.app.

  2. 2

    Pick a printer per paper size

    In Photo Session → Print settings, tap the paper size (for example 4×6 in) and choose your Sinfonia in the list.

  3. 3

    Auto-print (optional)

    After one good test, tick Auto-print every session.

Part 5 · If you print 2×6 strips

Photo strips

The booth puts two 2×6 strips side by side on one 4×6 sheet. On the Color Stream CS2 the printer itself cuts the sheet into two 2×6 strips.

  1. 1

    Add the printer a second time

    Add the same printer again in Windows or macOS and name it Sinfonia 2x6 cut. In that copy's printing preferences choose the 2×6 (two strips) paper option. Keep the first copy on plain 4×6.

  2. 2

    Assign them in the booth

    In Print settings, give the 4×6 size the first printer and the 2×6 strips the 2x6 cut printer.

  3. 3

    Other models

    For printers other than the CS2 and S2145, look for a 2×6 option in the driver. If it isn't there, cut the sheet in half by hand.

Part 6 · Before the event

Test and fixes

ProblemTry this
Printer not in the booth's listCheck it shows in Windows / Mac Printers & scanners. Reopen the Print Helper, or restart the laptop app.
Wrong size or a white borderSet the paper size in the driver's preferences to match the kit; choose borderless if offered.
The program crashes after you change colour in the driverOne Mac tester reported the driver's colour and brightness corrections crashing the program they were printing from. Leave the driver at its defaults and set the look in the booth.
Pop-up print dialog every timeOnline booth: open the Print Helper. Or use the laptop app.
