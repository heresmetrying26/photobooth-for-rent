How it works

DSLR on iPad + USB Printer on Laptop (Zero Internet Needed)

Your camera and printer run on two clean, separate paths so they never slow each other down: the DSLR plugs straight into your iPad over USB, and finished photos go over your local booth Wi-Fi straight to the photobooth4u Laptop App.

Before leaving for the venue

One-time updates & checklist (while online)

  1. 1

    Install or update the Laptop App (1.1.111 or newer)

    On your Windows or Mac laptop, open Setup › Share › App updates › Check for updates (or download the latest installer at photobooth4u.netlify.app/downloads/). Install your printer’s official driver too (for example, the Mitsubishi CP-D80DW driver) and load matching paper & ribbon.

  2. 2

    Update your iPad app over-the-air

    While connected to Wi-Fi, open the photobooth4u iPad app → go to Setup → tap Check for updates (no app reinstall required).

  3. 3

    Confirm power & adapter cables

    Keep both the laptop and iPad plugged into power at the event, and turn off Windows/macOS Sleep when plugged in. If connecting a Canon/Nikon/Sony DSLR to a Lightning iPad, use a genuine powered Apple Lightning-to-USB 3 Camera Adapter (or USB-C cable/hub on USB-C iPads).

Step 1 · Where to click on your Windows / Mac laptop

Start Print Station inside the Laptop App

  1. 1

    Plug in the USB printer & turn on your Wi-Fi

    Connect your Mitsubishi CP-D80DW (or DNP / HiTi) directly to a USB port on the laptop and switch the printer on. If Windows Firewall asks for access when opening the app, tick both Private and Public networks and click Allow access.

  2. 2

    Open Offline Print Station inside the Laptop App

    On the Setup bottom bar, tap ⋯ (next to Profiles) → Offline Print Station (iPad / Tablet) — or inside Photo Session, tap Tools → Print Station.
    Tip: Opening Print Station automatically pauses laptop camera preview so all laptop speed goes to your printer.

  3. 3

    Start your Wi-Fi or Booth Hotspot

    Connect both your laptop and iPad to the same travel Wi-Fi router — OR on Windows with no router, simply click Turn On Booth Hotspot at the top right of the Print Station window.

  4. 4

    Check Driver Preferences & run a Test Print

    Under Installed Printers on This Laptop, click Driver Preferences next to your printer to confirm 4×6 or 2×6 cut, then click Test 4×6 (or Test 2×6 Cut). Leave this window open on the laptop showing your green Laptop Wi-Fi Address and yellow 6-Digit PIN.

Using the ⋯ button in Setup

The bottom-bar ⋯ menu in Setup also gives 1-tap access to your active event tools (Offline Print Station, Photographer desk, 360 desk, Print requests, Arrange & Print, Video guestbook, All photos, and Booth Wi-Fi) without starting a photo session first.

Step 2 · Where to tap on your iPad (+ DSLR)

Pair your iPad App to the Laptop Print Station

  1. 1

    Join the laptop’s Wi-Fi on your iPad

    Open iPad Settings → Wi-Fi and connect to the laptop’s hotspot or booth router. If iPadOS displays “No Internet Connection”, tap Keep Trying Wi-Fi / Use Without Internet.

  2. 2

    Connect your DSLR camera (if using a Canon 550D / DSLR)

    Turn the camera dial to M or Av, set Auto Power Off → Disable in the camera menu, plug it into your iPad via USB adapter, then open photobooth4u → Setup → Camera and pick your camera.

  3. 3

    Turn ON Laptop Print Station in Setup → Printer

    Choose your paper size (4×6 or 2×6 strips), then switch ON Laptop Print Station (Wi-Fi · Offline USB printer).

  4. 4

    Find Laptop & enter the 6-Digit PIN

    Tap Find Laptop on Wi-Fi (or type the green IP address shown on your laptop), type the 6-Digit PIN shown on the laptop Print Station screen, and tap Connect.

  5. 5

    Pick Printer 1 & enable Auto-Print

    In 1 · Printer 1, pick your Mitsubishi / photo printer from the dropdown. Run one session to verify alignment & cut, then switch on Send a print automatically after each session.

Quick fixes

Troubleshooting at the event

What you seeFix it in seconds
Print Station is not shown under ⋯ or ToolsConnect to internet once, go to Setup › Share › Code & devices › Check now to sync your account features, or message us so we can enable Print Station on your code.
iPad can’t find the laptop on Wi-FiConfirm both devices are on the exact same Wi-Fi/hotspot, check that Windows Firewall allows the photobooth4u app on both Private & Public networks, and try typing the Laptop IP manually.
Wrong PIN message on iPadCheck the current 6-Digit PIN on the laptop’s Print Station panel (or click New PIN on the laptop and re-enter it on the iPad).
Prints 4×6 instead of cutting 2×6 stripsAdd a second printer queue in Windows (e.g. Mitsubishi 2x6 cut) with 2-inch cut enabled in Driver Preferences, then test with Test 2×6 Cut. See our Mitsubishi 2×6 strip guide.
