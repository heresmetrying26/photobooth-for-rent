How to print
on a thermal printer

For 80 mm and 58 mm receipt printers (Epson TM-T20 / T82, Xprinter, Rongta, Munbyn…). They print the black & white Receipt layout on a roll and cut it. Set up once, then test before the event.

Part 1 · Check first

Before you start

80 / 58

Roll width

Buy the paper to match the printer: 80 mm or 58 mm thermal paper. The booth makes the picture the full width of the roll.

B&W

Receipt layout

Thermal paper prints black & white only. Use the Receipt layout: its photos are dotted so they print sharp. Other layouts need a photo printer.

Plan

Paper size allowed

Receipt 80 mm and Receipt 58 mm are included with your code. If you don't see them in Print settings, message us.

Part 2 · Android tablet app

Pair and connect over Bluetooth

The Android app talks to the printer directly, so no driver and no Wi-Fi are needed.

  1. 1

    Pair the printer

    Turn the printer on. On the tablet open Settings → Connected devices → Bluetooth → Pair new device and tap the printer (often named like XP-80C, POS-80, TM-T82X or Printer001). If it asks for a PIN use 0000 or 1234.

  2. 2

    Open photobooth4u

    In the app go to Photo Session → Print settings. Choose Receipt 80 mm (or 58 mm).

  3. 3

    Turn on the Bluetooth printer

    Enable Bluetooth thermal receipt printer, tap Connect, and allow the Bluetooth permission if Android asks. If you have more than one paired device, pick your printer from the list.

  4. 4

    Test print

    Tap Test print: the printer should feed, print a test header and cut. Then every session prints straight to it.

Part 3 · Windows & Mac

Install the printer on the laptop

  1. 1

    Install the driver

    Install the maker's driver for your model, plug in the printer and print a Windows / Mac test page.

  2. 2

    Choose roll paper

    Windows: Settings → Printers & scanners → your printer → Printing preferences: paper Roll Paper 80 × 297 mm (or longer), and cut at the end of the document. Mac: add it in Printers & Scanners and set the paper to the roll.

  3. 3

    Use the laptop app or the Print Helper

    The laptop app prints straight to it. In Chrome, open the Print Helper (version 1.1.4 or newer on Windows) so the receipt prints at the roll's full width without a pop-up.

Part 4 · In the booth

Choose the Receipt layout

  1. 1

    Pick the layout

    In Setup → Layout choose Receipt (black & white). It looks like a shop receipt with your event name, 3 photos and a barcode.

  2. 2

    Pick the paper and printer

    In Print settings tap Receipt 80 mm or 58 mm and choose the printer for it. Receipt paper only prints the Receipt layouts.

  3. 3

    Auto-print (optional)

    After one good test, tick Auto-print every session so each guest's receipt prints by itself.

Part 5 · Before the event

Test and fixes

ProblemTry this
Printer not in the Bluetooth listPair it in Android Settings first, switch the printer off and on, and keep it within 2 metres.
Prints very small or stretchedSet the printer to roll paper with no fixed page size. On Windows use Print Helper 1.1.4+.
Picture cut off at the edgeThe paper width in the booth (80 / 58 mm) must match the roll in the printer.
Prints are paleThermal paper loaded the right way round? Use fresh paper and raise the printer's print density if it has the option.
Doesn't cutTurn on cut at end of document in the printer's preferences.
Stops after a few receiptsBluetooth printers sleep. Keep it charged or on its adapter, then tap Connect again.
