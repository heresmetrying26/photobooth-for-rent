Before you start

What you need for wired DSLR roving

Your phone acts as a pocket USB-to-Wi-Fi bridge. Choose the cable that matches your phone and camera.

[Canon / Nikon / Sony DSLR] <-- Short USB Data Cable --> [iPhone or Android Phone] ~~~ 5GHz Local Wi-Fi ~~~> [Laptop Photographer Desk]
Apple iPhone / iPad (iOS 15+)

iPhone Hardware

  • iPhone 15 / 16 / 17 or USB-C iPad (Recommended): Connect directly with a short USB-C to USB-C data cable (or USB-C to Mini/Micro-USB for older DSLRs). Avoid charge-only cables.
  • iPhone 14 & older (Lightning): Generic cheap OTG adapters fail with “Accessory requires too much power”. Use the official Apple Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter with a small power bank plugged into its Lightning port.
  • Install tool (once): Windows PC or Mac with free Sideloadly and any Apple ID.
Android Phone (Android 7+)

Android Hardware

  • Any Android phone with USB OTG support.
  • USB OTG cable/adapter matching your camera port (ensure the Host/OTG side plugs into the Android phone).
  • No PC needed to install—download and open the .apk directly on the phone.

Zero extra license fee on the phone

Roving DSLR Bridge installs as its own separate app (com.photobooth4u.rovingbridge) beside the photobooth app. It does not require a booth code on the phone—only the laptop base station needs Photographer Desk enabled.

Option 1 · Apple iPhone & iPad

Install Roving DSLR Bridge on iPhone (.ipa via Sideloadly)

Because the standalone iOS bridge is distributed as a direct .ipa outside the App Store, install it once from a Windows PC or Mac in ~5 minutes using Sideloadly.

  1. 1

    Download Sideloadly and roving-dslr-bridge-ios.ipa

    On your computer, install Sideloadly (Windows: also install Apple’s non-Microsoft-Store iTunes linked on sideloadly.io; Mac: drag Sideloadly into Applications). Then click Download iPhone / iPad (.ipa) at the top of this page. Do not unzip the .ipa file.

    On Mac: If macOS shows “Sideloadly” Not Opened, click Done (never Move to Trash), open System Settings → Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

  2. 2

    Connect your iPhone to your computer

    Plug your iPhone (or iPad) into the Windows PC or Mac via USB, unlock the screen, and tap Trust This Computer if prompted.

  3. 3

    Sideload the .ipa with Sideloadly

    Open Sideloadly, confirm your iPhone shows under iDevice, drag roving-dslr-bridge-ios.ipa into the IPA icon on the left, enter your Apple ID email (a regular free Apple ID works), and click Start. Enter your Apple ID password and 2FA code if asked.

  4. 4

    Trust Developer Certificate & enable Developer Mode (iOS 16+)

    When Sideloadly finishes and Roving DSLR Bridge appears on your Home Screen:

    • Open iPhone Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID under Developer App, and tap Trust.
    • On iOS 16 or newer, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode, switch it ON, restart the iPhone, and confirm Turn On.
  5. 5

    Allow iOS permissions on first launch

    Open Roving DSLR Bridge and allow all three system prompts so QR scanning, local Wi-Fi uploads, and USB cameras work:

    • Camera Access (to scan the laptop Photographer Desk QR code)
    • Local Network (to send photos directly to your laptop over offline booth Wi-Fi)
    • Camera / Accessory Access (when you plug your DSLR into the USB-C port)

Free Apple ID 7-day refresh reminder

Apps signed with a free Apple ID stay active for 7 days. Before an event, connect the iPhone to your computer (or over Wi-Fi), open Sideloadly, and click Start again (~30 seconds) without deleting the app to refresh the 7-day timer while keeping your settings.

Option 2 · Android Phone

Install Roving DSLR Bridge on Android (.apk)

Android installs directly on the phone with no computer required.

  1. 1

    Download roving-dslr-bridge-android.apk

    Open this page (or /downloads/#roving-bridge) on your Android phone and tap Download Android (.apk).

  2. 2

    Allow unknown apps & install

    Tap the finished APK in Chrome downloads or your Files app and choose Install. If Android prompts for your browser/file manager, allow Install unknown apps. It appears in your app drawer as 📷 Roving DSLR Bridge.

  3. 3

    Grant USB OTG permission once

    When you plug in your DSLR via USB OTG, check Always allow and tap OK on the Android USB popup so the bridge connects automatically next time.

Camera Setup & Pairing

Configure your DSLR, pair QR, and shoot

Set these camera menus once before plugging the USB cable into your phone.

Camera ModelRequired Settings Before Plugging USB
Canon EOS R50 / R-series
(R50, R10, R100, R8, RP, M50)		 1. Open Menu → Wrench (Set-up) → Choose USB connection app → select Photo Import/Remote Control.
Do NOT choose “Canon app(s) for iPhone” or “Video calls/streaming”.
2. Turn Wi-Fi / Bluetooth OFF.
3. Mode dial on M, Av, Tv, or P (not Video), JPEG (or RAW+JPEG), formatted SD card inserted.
4. Set Auto Power Off to Off or long so USB stays awake while walking.
Canon EOS DSLRs
(60D, 70D, 80D, 90D, Rebel / 1500D / 2000D, 5D / 6D)		 Turn Wi-Fi / NFC OFF. Mode dial on M, Av, Tv, or P (not Video). Image quality: JPEG (or RAW+JPEG). Insert an SD card with free space and close any open camera menu.
Nikon D5300 / D5600 / D- & Z-series Turn Airplane mode ON (or disable SnapBridge Wi-Fi). Insert a formatted SD card. Set Image quality to JPEG Fine/Normal (or RAW+JPEG). Set USB mode to MTP/PTP if shown in Setup menu. Close camera menus before plugging in.
Sony Alpha
(A6000–A6700, A7 II/III/IV, ZV-E10)		 Set Setup → USB → USB Connection: PC Remote, Still Img. Save Dest.: PC + Camera, and Release w/o Card: Enable. File format: JPEG (or RAW+JPEG). Turn off Smartphone Connection / Imaging Edge.
  1. 1

    Put laptop and phone on the same Wi-Fi

    On the laptop base station, open Setup → Share & start and switch on Make this laptop the booth Wi-Fi (or connect both devices to the same 5GHz router / hotspot—no internet required).

  2. 2

    Scan the Photographer Desk QR

    Open Tools → Photographer desk → Connect & QR on the laptop. In Roving DSLR Bridge on your phone, tap 📷 Scan QR Code on Laptop and point at the laptop screen until it flashes green ✅ Connected! Laptop ready.

  3. 3

    Plug in the DSLR & verify ● READY

    Turn the DSLR on and plug the USB data cable into your phone. Wait 1–2 seconds for ● READY (with camera name & battery). Tap 📸 Test Shutter once to confirm the full pipeline to the laptop queue.

  4. 4

    Choose Send Mode & Brightness (Android v1.1.4+)

    In the 🎛️ Send Mode & Brightness card on the Android app:

    • ⚡ Auto-Send All: Every shutter click streams straight to the laptop right away (default).
    • ☑️ Pick to Send: Shots stay saved on the phone first. Tap ☑️ Review to inspect uncropped thumbnails with ▯ Port / ▭ Land badges, adjust brightness live, and tap 📤 Send to Laptop (or 🗑️ Skip) one by one—or tap ☑ Multi-Pick to send only your chosen batch.
    • ☀️ Brightness before sending (-30% to +40%): Lighten dim venue shots right on the phone with instant live preview before uploading. Leaving it at 0% uploads the original DSLR JPEG byte-for-byte untouched.
  5. 5

    Engage Pocket Mode & shoot with the camera shutter

    Tap 🔒 Pocket Mode before slipping the phone into your pocket or holster—the screen dims to OLED black and blocks accidental taps while keeping capture live (buzzing as photos send, or saving safely into your Pick-to-Send review queue).

Important for iPhone Pocket Mode

Keep the app open on screen inside 🔒 Pocket Mode—do not press the iPhone’s physical side sleep/lock button! iOS suspends USB camera access if the iPhone is locked or switched to another app. (To prevent accidental side-button locks, turn on iPhone Guided Access before walking the floor.)

Troubleshooting

Quick fixes

What you seeHow to fix it
Canon R50 stays on WAITING on iPhoneMake sure R50 Choose USB connection app is set to Photo Import/Remote Control (not Canon app(s) for iPhone), turn camera Wi-Fi OFF, switch the camera off & on, and verify your USB-C cable supports data transfer (not charging-only).
iPhone asks for permissions or camera won’t openCheck iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security: allow Camera, Local Network, and Accessories for Roving DSLR Bridge. Tap the 🩺 diagnostics button inside the app to inspect live USB/PTP logs.
Lightning iPhone shows “Accessory requires too much power”DSLRs draw more current than unpowered Lightning ports supply. Use an official Apple Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter with a USB power bank plugged into its charging port (or use a USB-C iPhone 15/16/17).
Laptop URL test fails even on the same Wi-FiOn Windows laptop, switch the Wi-Fi network profile from Public to Private (or allow photobooth4u through Windows Firewall). On iPhone, ensure Local Network permission is enabled for Roving DSLR Bridge.
Photos shot out of Wi-Fi rangeKeep shooting! Unsent full-res JPEGs stay queued safely on phone storage and upload automatically in order as soon as Wi-Fi signal returns.
