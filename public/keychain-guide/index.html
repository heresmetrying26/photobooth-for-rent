Keychain Printing & Frame Collage Guide

Step-by-step instructions for creating, configuring, previewing, and gang-printing 1:3 keychain inserts at 100% true scale on A4, Letter, and 4R paper.

1. Paper Sizes vs. Keychain Scaling

Acrylic keychain blanks feature rigid rectangular cavities that leave no room for scaling errors. If your printer shrinks the strip by 5%, it rattles inside the case; if it stretches the strip, it will not fit.

Paper Size Dimensions Max Keychain Size (100% Scale) Capacity Scaling Status
A4 Paper 210 × 297 mm (8.27 × 11.69 in) 2.5 × 7.5 in (63.5 × 190.5 mm) 3 upright (or 4 sideways) Exact 100% Scale
US Letter 216 × 279 mm (8.5 × 11 in) 2.5 × 7.5 in (63.5 × 190.5 mm) 3 upright side-by-side Exact 100% Scale
8R / 8×10 203 × 254 mm (8 × 10 in) 2.5 × 7.5 in (63.5 × 190.5 mm) 3 upright side-by-side Exact 100% Scale
4R (4×6 in) 102 × 152 mm (4 × 6 in) 2.0 × 6.0 in (Mini Strip) 2 upright side-by-side Exact 100% Scale
4R (4×6 in) 102 × 152 mm (4 × 6 in) 2.5 × 7.5 in (Requires shrinking) 1 – 2 strips Shrunk to ~80% (2.0×6.0 in)
Why 4R Paper Cannot Print 2.5 × 7.5 in at 100%: Standard 4R paper is 6 inches tall, while the keychain is 7.5 inches long. To produce true 2.5 × 7.5 in keychains, you must load A4, Letter, or 8R paper. If you only have a 4R printer, purchase 2.0 × 6.0 in or 1.8 × 5.5 in keychain blanks.

2. Template Design & Upload (Step 3)

  1. Dimensions: Design at 750 × 2250 px for 2.5 × 7.5 in (or 600 × 1800 px for 2.0 × 6.0 in) at 300 DPI. Both represent an exact 1:3 aspect ratio.
  2. Photo Cutouts: Leave transparent (alpha channel) or solid white boxes where guest photos should appear. The booth auto-detects these windows.
  3. Safe Margins: Keep text, names, and event dates 35 px inside the outer edges. Export as a transparent PNG.
  4. Upload: Go to Step 3 (Layout) → Frames with photo spots → Click + Add frame.
Step 3 Layout Cards: Select Frame collage to place multiple keychain strips together on one sheet.

3. Configuring Frame Collage (`framesheet`)

In Step 3, once Frame collage is selected, configure the multi-strip sheet:

  • Frames on this sheet: Tap your uploaded frame to add it to the sheet (shows ✓).
  • Sheet size: Select A4, Letter, or 8×10 for 2.5 × 7.5 in keychains (or 4×6 for 2x6 mini strips).
  • Arrange them on the sheet: Choose 3 strips (or 2 side by side on 4R).
  • Add copies: Click + Add a copy so multiple duplicate strips are laid out on the page. All strips will populate with the guest's photos!
Sheet Arrangement Panel: Tap the frame, pick the sheet size, select the 3-strip pattern, and duplicate copies.

4. Checking the Live Preview Canvas

Click Open preview (or the eye icon) to verify the imposition:

Preview Canvas: 3 keychain strips laid out side-by-side with guest photos and dashed trimming guidelines.

5. Print Settings & Driver Scaling (Critical)

Open the Print settings modal (via the floating printer button or Step 4 Printer setup):

Print Settings: Select A4 paper, toggle ON Keychain strips at real size (2.5×7.5 in), and confirm the red hint text.
Printer Driver Scaling Rules:
• Scale / Page Sizing: Must be set to 100% / Actual Size. Never use "Fit to Printable Area" or "Scale to Fit".
• Borderless: Keep OFF to avoid the driver's automatic 2% to 4% bleed expansion.
• Paper Stock: Use 200 to 260 gsm glossy photo paper.
Keychain Assembly: Print at 100% scale, cut along inner dashed guidelines, insert into clear casing, and snap lid shut.
