Guest payments

Guests pay before each session — by scanning a QR with GCash, Maya or any bank app, or with vouchers you sell at the booth. Turn it on per event in the booth: Setup → 5 Session → Guest payment. Step-by-step guide (PDF)

Not connected
  1. Log in at dashboard.paymongo.com → Developers → API keys. (QR Ph must be switched on for your account; PayMongo support does this.)
  2. Copy your Secret key. It starts with sk_live_ (or sk_test_ to try it first).
  3. Paste it below and choose a payments PIN. You’ll need the PIN to replace or disconnect the key.

Your key stays private. It’s encrypted on our server and never shown again — not on this page, not to photobooth4u staff, not on the booth. Money goes straight to your PayMongo account. You can disconnect anytime, or make a new key in PayMongo to cut access instantly.
