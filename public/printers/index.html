☆ Have a printer not listed in the table?

If your printer has a Windows or macOS driver, it works with the booth! The laptop app and Print Helper print silently to any printer that shows in your system's Printers & scanners list. Follow the 4-step universal setup guide below to get up and running in minutes.

Category 1 · Event gold standard

Commercial dye-sublimation photo printers

Dye-sublimation is the undisputed choice for professional photo booth businesses. High roll capacity (400–700 prints), 8 to 14-second print times, water and fingerprint-resistant gloss or matte lamination, and internal dual 2×6 strip slicing.

Industry favorite

DNP (DS620 & RX1HS)

The DS-RX1HS is the budget workhorse with 700 prints per roll. The DS620 is the flagship speed king (~8.4s for 4×6), with panoramic options. Both feature automatic 2×6 dual cutting.

Reliable choice

HiTi (P525L & P750L)

The P525L is compact, rugged, and features an LCD status display with wireless dongle expansion. Driver settings allow instant switching between 4×6 postcards and 2×6 twin strips.

Compact & Wide

Citizen (CX-02 & CZ-01)

The CX-02 offers premium print quality and ribbon save technology. The CZ-01 weighs just 5.8 kg, making it the ultimate lightweight portable printer for booth builders on the go.

High Capacity

Mitsubishi & Fuji ASK

Mitsubishi CP-D70DW and D90DW offer fast ink-rewind technology. Fujifilm ASK-300 delivers vibrant Fujicolor reproduction with 12-second cycle times and built-in strip cutting.

How automatic 2×6 strip cutting works

In your printer's Windows or macOS Printing Preferences, enable the 2-inch cut option. The booth will layout two 2×6 strips side-by-side on a single 4×6 sheet; the printer's internal razor blade slices them down the middle during the final pass, dropping two finished strips into your tray!

Category 2 · Ultra-low cost per print

Continuous ink-tank photo printers

Continuous Ink Supply System (CISS) printers like Epson EcoTank and Canon MegaTank have revolutionized photo booth profitability. With ink costs under ₱0.50 ($0.01) per photo, they are ideal for high-volume corporate events and budget-conscious booth owners.

6-Color Photo

Epson EcoTank L8050

The newest high-speed 6-color photo tank. Prints borderless 4R in ~12 to 25 seconds with deep blacks and smooth skin tones. Android tablet app connects directly over USB OTG and Wi-Fi!

Everyday Workhorse

Epson L3210 & L3250

Everyday 4-color ink tanks that print glossy 4R photos and standard A4 layouts. The Android tablet app has direct native USB OTG support for the L3210, L3216, L3250 and L3276 without pop-ups.

Photo MegaTank

Canon PIXMA G620 / G650

Dedicated 6-color photographic dye ink tank. Delivers exhibition-grade color reproduction, borderless 4×6 prints, and wireless AirPrint / Mopria connectivity for iPad and Android booths.

Everyday inkjets & laser printers work too

An ordinary inkjet or laser printer (Epson, Brother, HP, Canon) prints great on A4, A5, A6, and Letter sheets — as well as 4×6 photo paper if your model has a rear photo tray.

  1. 1

    Set the printer up as normal

    Install it on your Windows or Mac laptop the way you would for any document, so it shows in Printers & scanners.

  2. 2

    Choose it in the booth

    In Photo Session → Print settings (or Setup → 4 Camera & printer), tap your paper size (A4, A5, A6, Letter or 4R) and pick the printer. Online booth in Chrome: open the Print Helper first.

  3. 3

    From an iPad or Android tablet

    Put the tablet and printer on the same Wi-Fi. Most recent Epson, Brother, HP and Canon printers support AirPrint (iPad) or Mopria (Android), so the tablet prints directly. The Android app also finds them itself without pop-up dialogs.

  4. 4

    Load photo paper for glossy results

    Load dedicated inkjet photo paper and select "Glossy Photo Paper" in the printer's driver preferences. Plain paper works too for quick guest souvenirs or certificate printouts.

Wireless and tablet setups

How iPad & Android tablets print

Tablets give you the cleanest, most modern booth kiosk. Here is how your tablet connects to different printer categories:

1

Laptop Print Station

Plug ANY USB dye-sub printer (DNP, HiTi, Citizen, Fuji, Kodak) into a laptop running the Laptop Print Station. Your iPad or Android booth sends prints over Wi-Fi silently!

2

Direct AirPrint (iPad)

Canon Selphy CP1300/1500, Epson EcoTank, HP, and Brother printers with AirPrint print directly from the iPad over the same Wi-Fi network with zero print dialogs.

3

Android USB OTG & Wi-Fi

The Android tablet app automatically discovers Wi-Fi Mopria printers and USB printers via OTG cable (including driverless USB and Epson ink-tank printers) with silent direct output.

4

Bluetooth Thermal

Pair a 58 mm or 80 mm thermal receipt printer in Android Bluetooth Settings. The Android app prints retro strips instantly without showing a print dialog.

Universal 4-step guide

Setting up a printer that isn't listed

  1. 1

    Install the maker's official driver

    Download the Windows or macOS driver directly from the printer manufacturer's support site. Plug the printer in via USB or join it to your Wi-Fi until it appears in Printers & scanners.

  2. 2

    Configure printing preferences and paper size

    Open the printer's Printing Preferences (Windows) or Print Options (Mac). Set your loaded paper size (e.g. 4×6 in), choose Borderless, set print quality to Photo/High, and enable 2-inch cut if printing dual strips.

  3. 3

    Choose the printer in photobooth4u

    In Photo Session → Print settings (or Setup → 4 Camera & printer), tap your paper size (e.g. 4×6 or 2×6 strip) and pick your printer from the list. Online Chrome booth: open the Print Helper first.

  4. 4

    Run a test print & turn on auto-print

    Take a test photo in the booth and hit Print. Verify colors, borders, and cuts. Once confirmed, tick Auto-print every session for seamless hands-free operation throughout your event!

Troubleshooting

Common printing fixes

Printer not in the booth list

Ensure the printer is powered on and shows as "Ready" in Windows / Mac Printers & Scanners. In Chrome, make sure the Print Helper is running. In the laptop app, click Refresh Printers or restart the app.

White border or clipped edges

Open the printer's Printing Preferences in Windows/Mac and ensure Borderless is checked. Match the paper size in the booth settings exactly to the media loaded in the printer.

2×6 strips not cutting in two

Your driver must have its 2-inch cut option enabled. Add the printer a second time in Windows (e.g. named "DNP - 2x6 Strips") with the cut option set as default, and select that copy for the 2×6 size in the booth.

Media / ribbon mismatch error

On dye-sublimation printers, the paper roll width and ribbon color roll must be an exact matched set. Always replace both paper and ribbon together from the same box.
