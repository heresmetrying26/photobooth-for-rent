What about the Android tablet app?

The Android app prints only to Bluetooth thermal receipt printers. Photo printers need a Windows or Mac laptop running the laptop app or the Print Helper.

Epson, Brother, HP, Canon and more

Everyday printers work too

An ordinary inkjet or laser printer works with the booth. It prints on A4, A5, A6 and Letter paper (and 4×6 photo paper if the printer takes it), so it suits bigger prints and layouts rather than photo strips.

  1. 1

    Set the printer up as normal

    Install it on the Windows or Mac laptop the way you would for any document, so it shows in Printers & scanners.

  2. 2

    Choose it in the booth

    In Photo Session → Print settings, tap the paper size (A4, A5, A6 or Letter) and pick the printer. Online booth in Chrome: open the Print Helper first. The laptop app needs nothing extra.

  3. 3

    From an iPad or Android tablet

    Put the tablet and printer on the same Wi-Fi. Most recent Epson, Brother, HP and Canon printers support AirPrint (iPad) or Mopria (Android), so the tablet prints to them directly. Older or USB-only models: plug them into a laptop running the print station.

  4. 4

    Photo quality

    Load photo paper and choose the matching paper type in the printer's settings. Plain paper works too, with softer colours.

The same steps for any photo printer

Setting up a printer that isn't listed

  1. 1

    Install the maker's driver

    On Windows or Mac, so the printer shows in Printers & scanners.

  2. 2

    Test print at the paper size you will use

    For example 4×6, from the Photos app (Windows) or Preview (Mac).

  3. 3

    Choose it in the booth

    In Photo Session → Print settings, pick the paper size and the printer. Online booth in Chrome: open the Print Helper first.

  4. 4

    For 2×6 strips

    Add the printer a second time with its 2×6 / 2-inch cut option, and give that copy to the 2×6 size. No cut option in the driver? Cut the 4×6 sheet in half by hand.
